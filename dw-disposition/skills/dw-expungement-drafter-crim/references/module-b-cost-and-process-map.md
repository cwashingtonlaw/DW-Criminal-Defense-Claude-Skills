# Module B — Cost & Process Map (La. C.Cr.P. arts. 979–984)

Build this map per arrest incident before drafting. Fees are non-refundable; the client should see the full cost and timeline before authorizing filing.

## B-1. Filing fees (art. 983)

**Statutory cap: the total cost to obtain an expungement order shall not exceed $550 per arrest incident.** Standard component fees:

| Recipient | Standard fee | First-offense marijuana misdemeanor |
|---|---|---|
| La. Bureau of Criminal Identification and Information (LSP) | $250 | $50 |
| Sheriff | $50 | $50 |
| District Attorney | $50 | $50 |
| Clerk of Court (processing, not to exceed) | $200 | $150 |
| **Total cap** | **$550** | **$300** |

Amounts verified against art. 983 text as of this build; **re-verify current amounts before quoting the client** — the fee schedule has been amended repeatedly. Some clerks collect additional certified-copy or mailing charges on top; confirm locally (see `calcasieu-14thjdc-local-notes.md`).

**DWI add-on:** an expungement of an operating-while-intoxicated conviction requires proof from the Department of Public Safety and Corrections, Office of Motor Vehicles, and carries an additional fee (commonly stated as $50 to OMV) under art. 984 `[VERIFY CITATION — confirm current art. 984 OMV proof requirement and fee amount]`.

## B-2. Fee exemptions (art. 983)

No fees are owed to the clerk, LSP Bureau, sheriff, DA, or any other agency when the applicant presents the applicable certification:

1. **Acquittal** — acquitted after trial of all charges arising from the arrest, including lesser-included offenses.
2. **DA refusal / dismissal** — the DA declined to prosecute, or the prosecution was dismissed, within the time limitations of Chapter 1 of Title XVII (arts. 571–572).
3. **Time-barred** — arrested but never prosecuted within the time limitations.
4. **Factual innocence** — determination under La. R.S. 15:572.8.
5. **Pardon** — governor's pardon (the automatic first-offender pardon does NOT qualify for the fee exemption).
6. **Human trafficking victim** — with the required certification (also relaxes time delays).
7. **Juvenile records** — certain juvenile expungements are no-cost (La. Ch.C. art. 924 companion provisions) `[VERIFY CITATION — confirm juvenile no-cost provision]`.

The exemption for grounds 1–3 requires a **certification obtained from the district attorney** verifying the qualifying ground; build the DA-certification request into the timeline. The exclusive-use uniform-form set includes a form for the no-fee certification route (commonly identified as the art. 988 form) `[VERIFY CITATION — confirm which uniform-form article carries the fee-exemption certification]`.

**In forma pauperis:** an applicant not covered by an exemption may proceed IFP under La. C.C.P. art. 5181 et seq. (art. 983). File the IFP affidavit (income below the poverty threshold or public-benefits proof, notarized, with supporting documentation) **with or before** the expungement motion so costs are never assessed against the client.

## B-3. Service (art. 979)

After filing, the **clerk of court** serves the motion on:

1. The **district attorney** of the parish of conviction,
2. The **Louisiana Bureau of Criminal Identification and Information** (Louisiana State Police), and
3. The **arresting law enforcement agency**.

Service may be made by U.S. mail or electronic means; when by mail, a certificate of service showing the mailing date is filed into the record. The mailing date starts the objection clock — confirm the clerk actually effected service and calendar from that date, not the filing date.

## B-4. Objection window and contradictory hearing (art. 980)

- Any served agency may object by filing an **affidavit of response** stating reasons **within 60 days of service**.
- The court may grant one extension of **up to 30 additional days**.
- **If an objection is filed:** the matter is set for **contradictory hearing**; the objecting agency bears the burden of proving by a preponderance why the expungement should be denied.
- **If no objection is filed:** the defendant may waive the contradictory hearing and the court **shall grant** the motion if the mover is legally entitled — many courts grant these off-docket on the uniform order.
- Art. 978(E) crime-of-violence expungements **always** require the contradictory hearing regardless of objection (see Module A-4).

## B-6. Required attachments at filing

- **Background check** from Louisiana State Police (or parish sheriff) — local practice requires it be **dated within 60 days of filing**; verify whether the 14th JDC clerk requires the LSP version specifically `[VERIFY CITATION — statutory vs. clerk-practice basis for the 60-day background check]`
- Certified **Bill of Information / indictment** for each charge
- Certified **disposition minutes** (and set-aside order where applicable)
- **DA certification** (no intervening convictions / no pending charges) for art. 977(A)(2) and 978(A)(2) routes
- Fee-exemption certification or IFP affidavit, where applicable
- **OMV proof** for DWI expungements (B-1)

## B-7. Timeline expectations (counsel the client)

| Stage | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| Gather certified records + fresh background check + DA certification | 2–6 weeks |
| Filing → service by clerk | days–2 weeks |
| Objection window | 60 days (+ up to 30-day extension) |
| No objection → order signed | shortly after window closes (varies by division) |
| Objection → contradictory hearing → ruling | adds 1–3 months |
| Order → LSP certificate of compliance / records actually sealed downstream | ± 2 months after the signed order reaches LSP |

Realistic total: **4–8 months** from engagement to a fully processed expungement; longer with a hearing. Private background-check companies may lag further — Module D covers counseling on that gap.
