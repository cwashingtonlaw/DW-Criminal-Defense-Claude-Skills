# Module C — Motion Packet Drafting

Produce every packet component as ready-to-edit Word-document content (.docx via the docx skill), saved per SKILL.md Step 6. The uniform statutory forms (Module B-5) are **mandatory** — these drafts mirror the statutory form content so the attorney can transfer entries onto the clerk's current fillable form or file the conformed draft where the clerk accepts it. Always pull the clerk's current form version and reconcile before filing.

## C-1. Packet assembly order (per arrest incident)

1. **Motion to Set Aside Conviction and Dismiss Prosecution** (only if an art. 893(E)/894(B) deferral was completed but never set aside) + proposed order — this must be granted before or contemporaneously with the expungement
2. **Motion for Expungement** (art. 989 uniform-form content) + **Supplemental Sheet** (art. 993) if the arrest involved more than the form's charge capacity
3. **Proposed Order of Expungement** (art. 992 content) — plus proposed order setting contradictory hearing (art. 991 content) when an art. 978(E) hearing is required
4. **Fee-exemption certification** (DA-certified, where applicable) or **IFP affidavit**
5. **Attachments** (C-4 checklist)
6. **Certificate of service** per `dw-shared-protocols-crim/references/certificate-of-service.md` (note: art. 979 makes the clerk the serving party — the certificate documents the filing-party service steps the clerk's office requires locally)

## C-2. Caption and formatting

- Caption per `dw-shared-protocols-crim/references/caption-criminal-14thJDC.md` (or the caption file matching the parish of arrest; `caption-criminal-fill-in.md` for other parishes). The motion is filed in the **criminal district court docket of the parish of arrest** under the original docket number where prosecution was instituted; for arrests never prosecuted, follow the clerk's captioning convention for new expungement filings `[VERIFY per local clerk — see calcasieu-14thjdc-local-notes.md]`.
- Title block: `MOTION FOR EXPUNGEMENT OF ARREST AND CONVICTION RECORD` / `MOTION FOR EXPUNGEMENT OF ARREST RECORD` / `MOTION FOR INTERIM EXPUNGEMENT` as applicable.
- Formatting per `filing-conventions-14thJDC.md` (TNR 12pt, 1" margins, double-spaced body).
- Filed-pleading drafts carry **no work-product marking** (shared protocol).

## C-3. Motion for Expungement — draft content skeleton

The art. 989 uniform form is structured data, not argument. The draft must carry, source-cited from the record:

```
[CAPTION — parish of arrest]

               MOTION FOR EXPUNGEMENT OF [ARREST / ARREST AND CONVICTION] RECORD

NOW INTO COURT comes {{CLIENT_NAME}} ("Mover"), through undersigned counsel, who moves
this Honorable Court to expunge the record of arrest [and conviction] described below
pursuant to Louisiana Code of Criminal Procedure article [976 / 977 / 978 / 985.1]:

I. MOVER IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
   Name; date of birth; last four of SSN; sex/race; driver's license no.;
   address (per uniform-form fields)

II. ARREST INFORMATION (one motion per arrest)
   Arrest date; arresting agency; agency item/report number; parish;
   ATN (arrest tracking number, from the background check — required); docket no.

III. CHARGES ARISING FROM THE ARREST (table — every charge must be listed)
   | Charge (statute) | As billed / as amended | Disposition | Date |

IV. GROUND FOR EXPUNGEMENT (check/plead the statutory ground verbatim)
   ☐ Art. 976(A)(1) — not prosecuted; time limitations have run
   ☐ Art. 976(A)(2) — DA declined to prosecute (incl. diversion completion)
   ☐ Art. 976(A)(3) — dismissal / motion to quash sustained / acquittal
   ☐ Art. 977(A)(1) — misdemeanor conviction set aside per Art. 894(B)
   ☐ Art. 977(A)(2) — misdemeanor; five years elapsed; no felony conviction
       during that period; no pending felony charge
   ☐ Art. 978(A)(1) — felony conviction set aside per Art. 893(E)
   ☐ Art. 978(A)(2) — felony; ten years elapsed; no conviction during that
       period; no pending charge
   ☐ Art. 978(A)(3) — first-offender pardon eligibility
   ☐ Art. 978(E) — enumerated crime of violence; contradictory hearing requested
   ☐ Art. 985.1 — interim expungement (felony arrest; misdemeanor conviction only)

V. FEE STATUS
   ☐ Statutory fees tendered ($550 cap / $300 marijuana schedule)
   ☐ Exempt — DA certification attached (acquittal / refusal / time-barred)
   ☐ In forma pauperis — affidavit attached
   ☐ DWI — OMV proof attached

VI. PRAYER — that the Court order expungement of the above record pursuant to the
    uniform Order (La. C.Cr.P. art. 992 [art. 995 for interim]) directing the clerk,
    the arresting agency, and the Louisiana Bureau of Criminal Identification and
    Information to expunge the record per Art. 973.

[Signature block per dw-shared-protocols-crim/references/signature-block.md]
[Certificate of service]
```

Every entry in Sections I–III must cite its source document in the internal draft (citations stripped from the filing copy). The ATN comes off the LSP background check — a missing ATN is the most common clerk rejection; flag it loudly if absent.

## C-4. Attachments checklist (build into the packet as a cover checklist page)

- [ ] LSP (or sheriff) background check, dated within 60 days of filing
- [ ] Certified Bill of Information / indictment, each charge (or clerk certificate that none was filed)
- [ ] Certified minute entry / disposition for each charge
- [ ] Set-aside order (art. 893(E)/894(B) routes) — or the set-aside motion travels first (C-1)
- [ ] DA certification: no convictions during cleansing period / no pending charges (arts. 977(A)(2), 978(A)(2) routes)
- [ ] DA no-fee certification (fee-exempt routes) or IFP affidavit
- [ ] OMV proof (DWI expungements)
- [ ] Filing fees: separate checks per recipient if the clerk requires (LSP $250 / Sheriff $50 / DA $50 / Clerk ≤$200) — confirm payee format locally
- [ ] Self-addressed stamped envelopes / copies for conformed returns, per clerk practice

## C-5. Proposed Order content

Mirror the art. 992 (or 995) uniform order: identifies mover and arrest exactly as in the motion; orders the clerk of court, the arresting agency, and the Louisiana Bureau of Criminal Identification and Information to expunge the record; recites the art. 973 confidentiality effect; leaves date/judge signature lines per 14th JDC convention (`proposed-order.md` shared protocol governs layout).

## C-6. Interim expungement variant (art. 985.1)

Use the arts. 994–995 form content. Key drafting differences: the motion targets **only the felony arrest entry**; it recites the misdemeanor conviction that resulted; the order expunges the felony arrest from the criminal history while expressly leaving the misdemeanor conviction of record. Do not draft language suggesting the conviction is affected.

## C-7. Multi-arrest clients

One packet per arrest incident, each with its own fees/exemptions, background check copy, and venue. Present the attorney a **filing sequence recommendation** in the deep-screen memo: fee-exempt and art. 976 motions first (fast, free), conviction expungements second, out-of-parish filings batched by parish. Never combine arrests to save fees — clerks reject combined motions.
