# PSI Report Audit Protocol

## Louisiana PSI Requirements

**Authority:** La. C.Cr.P. Art. 875 -- Court may order a PSI before imposing sentence. For felonies, PSI is standard practice.

**PSI Contents (Art. 875):**
- Circumstances of the offense
- Criminal history
- Social history (family, education, employment, health)
- Victim impact information
- Evaluative summary and sentencing recommendation (by probation officer)

## Audit Protocol

**Source Citation Requirement for PSI Audits:** Every error, omission, or discrepancy identified in the PSI must cite both (1) the PSI page/section where the error appears and (2) the contradicting source document with page/paragraph. For example: "PSI Report, p. 8, Section III states three prior felonies; NCIC Rap Sheet, p. 2 shows only two felony entries." This dual-citation approach gives the attorney — and ultimately the court — immediate ability to verify each correction.

### Section 1: Offense Description Audit

| Audit Point | What to Check | Why It Matters |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Factual accuracy** | Does the offense description match the admitted facts (plea) or verdict? | PSI often includes prosecution's version uncritically; may overstate conduct |
| **Relevant conduct** | Does PSI include uncharged or dismissed conduct as though proven? | Uncharged conduct should not drive sentencing without proper findings |
| **Characterization bias** | Are neutral facts described in inflammatory language? | Word choice influences judicial perception ("brutal attack" vs. "altercation") |
| **Victim statements** | Are victim statements presented as uncontested facts? | Victim statements are not evidence; contested facts should be noted |
| **Omitted defense facts** | Does PSI omit facts favorable to the defense? | Provocation, self-defense elements, minimal participation -- all relevant |

### Section 2: Criminal History Audit

| Audit Point | What to Check | Why It Matters |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Accuracy of convictions** | Verify every listed conviction against official records | Incorrect convictions inflate criminal history and sentencing exposure |
| **Dismissed / acquitted charges** | Are dismissed charges listed as convictions? | Dismissed charges should not be counted against the defendant |
| **Juvenile records** | Are juvenile adjudications improperly included? | Juvenile records may be sealed or inadmissible |
| **Expunged records** | Are expunged convictions listed? | Expunged convictions should not appear |
| **Pardoned offenses** | Are pardoned offenses treated as valid predicates? | Pardoned offenses cannot serve as habitual offender predicates |
| **Cleansing period** | Have any convictions aged out of the habitual offender window? | 10-year cleansing period for most offenses |
| **Duplicative entries** | Are the same events listed multiple times under different case numbers? | Duplicates inflate perceived criminal history |

### Section 3: Social History Audit

| Audit Point | What to Check | Why It Matters |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Completeness** | Does PSI capture full life history or only negative facts? | Incomplete social history denies the court mitigating context |
| **Employment** | Is employment history accurate and complete? | Stable employment is a strong mitigating factor |
| **Education** | Are educational achievements reflected? | GED completion, vocational training, college attendance all mitigate |
| **Family** | Does PSI reflect family support and dependents? | Children dependent on client, supportive family -- all relevant |
| **Military** | Is military service accurately described? | Honorable service, combat exposure, service-connected disability all mitigate |
| **Mental health** | Are diagnoses accurately stated? Not overstated or understated? | Accurate mental health picture is essential for treatment-based sentencing |
| **Substance abuse** | Is treatment history reflected? | Completed treatment programs demonstrate rehabilitation |
| **Trauma history** | Are ACEs and trauma documented? | Childhood adversity contextualizes behavior without excusing it |

### Section 4: Recommendation Audit

| Audit Point | What to Check | Why It Matters |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Recommendation basis** | Is the probation officer's recommendation supported by the PSI's own facts? | Recommendations sometimes contradict the PSI's mitigating findings |
| **Sentencing range accuracy** | Did the probation officer correctly calculate the statutory range? | Errors in range calculation directly affect the recommendation |
| **Mitigation acknowledgment** | Does the recommendation acknowledge mitigating factors? | Failure to address mitigation is an omission the defense should highlight |
| **Bias indicators** | Does the recommendation track prosecution's position without independent analysis? | PSI should be neutral; prosecutor-aligned recommendations warrant challenge |

## Module B Overview — PSI Report Auditor (moved from SKILL.md)

### Purpose

Audit the Pre-Sentence Investigation report for factual errors, omissions, misleading characterizations, and any content that inflates the client's sentencing exposure or undermines mitigation. The PSI is often the single most influential document at sentencing -- errors in the PSI can result in years of additional imprisonment.

### PSI Audit Output

When errors are identified, package the corrections as findings within the sentencing memorandum or as supporting tables for oral objections at sentencing.
