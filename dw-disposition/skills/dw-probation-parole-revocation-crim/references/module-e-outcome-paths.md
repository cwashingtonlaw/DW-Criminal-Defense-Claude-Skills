# Module E — Outcome Paths and Post-Revocation Options

## E-1. Outcome map (present in every memo)

| Outcome | Probation | Parole |
|---|---|---|
| No violation found | Supervision continues unchanged | Hold lifted, parole continues |
| Violation, no revocation | Reprimand; intensified supervision; added/modified conditions; extension of term (Art. 900 menu) | Committee continues parole with modified conditions / administrative sanction |
| Technical revocation | Capped custodial sanction, then return to supervision (version-dependent cap) | Capped term (90/120/180-day tiers [VERIFY]), then return to active parole |
| Full revocation | Suspended sentence executed (Module D math; concurrent/consecutive designation per Art. 901) | Remainder of sentence per DOC computation; re-parole eligibility per committee rules [VERIFY — confirm re-parole eligibility rules after revocation] |
| Automatic revocation | — | Felony conviction for offense committed on parole → parole deemed revoked, La. R.S. 15:574.10 |

## E-2. Post-revocation options — probation

1. **Motion to reconsider** — ask the sentencing court to reconsider the revocation or the executed sentence's severity. A motion to reconsider sentence under La. C.Cr.P. art. 881.1 has a 30-day window [VERIFY — confirm Art. 881.1's applicability to a revocation-executed sentence]; a court also retains authority to correct an illegal sentence at any time (La. C.Cr.P. art. 882 [VERIFY CITATION]). Even where no vehicle strictly applies, an early motion practically invites the judge to soften the disposition (amend to time served + treatment) while attention is fresh.
2. **Supervisory writ — the review path.** A probation-revocation ruling is **not appealable** under La. C.Cr.P. art. 912; review is by **application for supervisory writs** to the court of appeal (see, e.g., *State v. Manuel*, 349 So.2d 882 (La. 1977) [VERIFY CITATION — confirm this case and that it states the writ-only rule]). Consequences: (a) the return period is set by the trial court and the Uniform Rules — La. Unif. R. Ct. App. Rule 4-3's 30-day period [VERIFY — confirm current rule number and period]; **give notice of intent and obtain a return date at the hearing's close**; (b) writ review is discretionary — the written-findings and preservation work from Module C is what makes a grant possible. Feed the writ deadline to `dw-deadline-engine-crim`.
3. **Credit/computation corrections** — jail-credit and concurrency errors are corrected by motion in the sentencing court and through DOC administrative remedies (La. R.S. 15:1171 et seq. [VERIFY CITATION — confirm ARP statute range]).
4. **PCR interplay** — post-conviction relief under La. C.Cr.P. art. 924 et seq. attacks the underlying *conviction/sentence*, not the revocation ruling itself; claims like ineffective assistance at the revocation hearing sit in an awkward gap ordinarily raised by writ [VERIFY — research the current vehicle for IAC-at-revocation claims before filing]. If the real target is the underlying conviction, route to `dw-post-conviction-relief-crim`.

## E-3. Post-revocation options — parole

1. **Reconsideration by the committee** — request rehearing/reconsideration under the committee's administrative rules [VERIFY — confirm current Board/Committee reconsideration rule and its window].
2. **Judicial review under La. R.S. 15:574.11** — district-court review (venue per statute; 19th JDC [VERIFY]), on the revocation record only, without a jury; petitions alleging denial of a revocation hearing are **peremptively barred 90 days after revocation** and dismissed with prejudice if late. Calendar day one; feed to `dw-deadline-engine-crim`.
3. **Re-parole** — after a technical revocation the client returns to parole automatically at term's end; after full revocation, re-parole consideration is committee-governed [VERIFY — confirm current re-parole eligibility provisions].
4. **Habeas/ARP** — custody-computation disputes go through DOC administrative remedies then judicial review; federal habeas (28 U.S.C. § 2254) for Morrissey violations only after exhaustion — route to `dw-post-conviction-relief-crim`.

## E-4. Preservation is the product

Because probation review is discretionary-writ-only and parole review is record-confined, the *hearing record* is the appeal. The Module C preservation checklist (notice objection, disclosure demand, confrontation/good-cause findings, *Bearden* inquiry, written findings request, notice of intent + return date) is mandatory output in every hearing-prep outline.
