# Module C — Hearing Preparation, Mitigation Package, Alternative-Sanctions Proposal

## C-1. Hearing-prep outline (deliverable #2)

Build the outline in this order (template in `output-format-specifications.md`):

1. **Posture block** — forum (court vs. committee panel), hearing date, custody status, classification result (Module B), governing statute version, and the CLOCK STATUS block from `dw-deadline-engine-crim` if available.
2. **Contest/concede map** — allegation-by-allegation from the Module A audit table: CONTEST (with the evidentiary attack), CONCEDE-AND-MITIGATE, or SILENT (State's burden, no concession). Never concede an allegation that carries non-technical classification without a negotiated cap.
3. **State's witnesses & cross plans** — usually the supervising officer. Cross themes: personal knowledge vs. file-reading (set up the confrontation objection), chrono inconsistencies, the client's compliance record *in the officer's own file*, staleness/pretext timing, drug-screen confirmation gaps, *Bearden* ability-to-pay facts. For a full cross architecture on a contested fact witness, route to `dw-cross-exam-architect-crim`.
4. **Confrontation demands** — list every hearsay declarant; scripted objection: "We object to proof of Allegation _ through hearsay and request confrontation of [declarant]; if the court dispenses with confrontation we request a specific finding of good cause on the record" (Morrissey/Gagnon).
5. **Defense witnesses & exhibits** — employer, treatment provider, family anchor, program certificates, pay stubs, negative screens, payment receipts. Subpoena early; committee hearings need advance witness clearance [VERIFY — confirm current committee witness/notice rules before the hearing].
6. **Preservation checklist** — written notice objection; disclosure demand; confrontation/good-cause findings; *Bearden* inquiry; request for written findings of evidence relied on and reasons; (parole) objections on the record because judicial review is confined to the revocation record (R.S. 15:574.11).

## C-2. Mitigation package

Assemble a short documentary package (5–15 pages) filed/handed up before the hearing: supervision success timeline (months compliant vs. the violation window), employment verification, treatment enrollment or completion, family/caretaking obligations, restitution and fee payments with ability-to-pay context, and a client letter where strategically sound (screen for Module F Fifth Amendment risk first — no factual admissions about new-offense conduct). Deep mitigation narratives (life history, trauma, diagnosis) route to `dw-sentencing-mitigation-specialist-crim`; this module packages, it does not author, that content.

## C-3. Proposed alternative-sanctions plan (deliverable #3)

Judges and panels accept alternatives that are **specific, verifiable, and already in motion**. The plan proposes a concrete disposition mapped to the sanction menu (Art. 900's non-revocation options / committee alternatives):

- **The ask** — e.g., reprimand + intensified supervision; modified conditions (curfew, GPS, treatment condition); capped technical sanction with credit for time served; custodial substance-abuse treatment placement in lieu of revocation; administrative-sanction track (Art. 899.1) where authorized.
- **The showing** — bed date or intake letter from the named treatment provider; employer letter confirming the job survives the proposed sanction; transportation/childcare logistics; payment schedule for arrears.
- **The safeguard** — what the client consents to if noncompliant (review date, status hearing), which gives the decisionmaker a face-saving enforcement path.
- **The credit math** — attach Module D's computation so the decisionmaker sees time already served against any custodial component.

Frame everything as the attorney's proposal for review — the client makes no admissions in the plan document unless the attorney decides a negotiated admission is the strategy.
