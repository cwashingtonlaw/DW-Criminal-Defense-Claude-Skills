# Severity Classification Matrix

## CRITICAL Issues (Case-Dispositive)

- **Exculpatory Physical Evidence:** Kit excludes accused entirely; contradicts timeline
- **DNA Exoneration:** Defendant profile absent from kit when prosecution claims involvement
- **Recanted Victim Allegation:** Alleged victim recants and investigation shows pressure/coercion
- **Statute of Limitations Expiration:** Charge barred by statute of limitations
- **Chain of Custody Breakdown:** Kit contaminated, stored improperly, evidence lost

## SIGNIFICANT Issues (Trial-Critical)

- **Delayed SANE Exam:** >5 days post-alleged assault; minimal acute findings
- **DNA Mixture Interpretation Dispute:** Probabilistic genotyping assumptions questionable
- **Leading Forensic Interview:** Multiple suggestive questions documented
- **Inconsistent Victim Statements:** Material discrepancies between interviews/testimony
- **Medical Evidence Misinterpretation:** Normal anatomy findings presented as injury

## MINOR Issues (Evidentiary Detail)

- **Documentation Gaps:** Minor missing lab notations (not affecting result)
- **Procedural Deviations:** Non-critical protocol deviations that do not affect analysis
- **Witness Credibility Details:** Minor inconsistencies in witness statements
- **Timeline Ambiguities:** Exact timing uncertain but not dispositive to elements
