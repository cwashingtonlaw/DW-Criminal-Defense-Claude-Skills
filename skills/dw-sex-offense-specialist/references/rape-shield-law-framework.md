# Rape Shield Law Framework (La. C.E. Art. 412)

## General Prohibition

**La. Code of Evidence Article 412** broadly excludes evidence of alleged victim's prior sexual behavior or reputation for sexual conduct. Purpose: prevent jury from judging victim based on sexual history.

**Rule:** Evidence of victim's sexual behavior or reputation is inadmissible, **except** as specified in exceptions.

## Exceptions to Rape Shield — Defense Use

### Exception 1: Consent Defense (Prior Sexual Relationship)

**Availability:** Where consent is issue and victim and accused had prior sexual relationship.

**Requirements:**
- Defense must make good faith offer of proof
- Prior relationship must be with accused
- Must be probative to consent issue
- Court balances probative value vs. prejudice

### Exception 2: Source of Semen/Injury/Disease

**Availability:** Where issue is whether alleged victim's injury or condition is related to alleged assault.

**Requirements:**
- Evidence must be probative to medical question
- Specific evidence of other source required
- Not general reputation or behavior

### Exception 3: Pattern of Behavior

**Availability:** In extraordinary circumstances where evidence of prior sexual conduct shows pattern similar to alleged conduct.

**Threshold:** Extremely high; rarely granted

### Exception 4: Constitutionally Required Evidence

**Availability:** Where excluding evidence violates accused's Sixth Amendment right to confront witnesses or Due Process.

**Standards:** *Crawford v. Washington*, *Davis v. Washington* — defendant entitled to confront accusers about prior statements

## La. C.E. Art. 412.1 — Sexually Assaultive Offenses Exception Procedure

**Special Procedure** if alleged victim in sexually assaultive offense case has prior sexual conduct evidence:

1. **Pretrial Motion Required:** Written motion with offer of proof (not in jury presence)
2. **Specificity Required:** Explain why evidence is admissible under exception
3. **Hearing:** Judge conducts hearing to determine admissibility
4. **Written Ruling:** Judge issues written decision stating exception applies and reasoning
5. **Limiting Instruction:** If admitted, jury receives specific limiting instruction

**Burden:** Defense bears burden of showing exception applies; preponderance of evidence standard

## La. C.E. Art. 412.2 — Prior Acts of Defendant

**Prosecution's Access to Defendant's Prior Sex Offenses:**

**Rule:** In sexually assaultive offense prosecution, evidence of defendant's **other crimes involving sexual conduct** is admissible without complying with rape shield exceptions.

**Defense Challenge:**
- Demand specifics: what prior acts?
- Require proof of prior conviction (not mere allegation)
- Object to unfair prejudice (352 balancing)
- Request limiting instruction distinguishing propensity inference

**Constitutional Limits:**
- Due Process: prior act must be reliable (not speculative)
- Sixth Amendment: accused entitled to cross-examine witnesses testifying about prior acts
- Probative value analysis required

## Procedural Requirements for Rape Shield Motions

### When Defense Seeks Admission of Victim Sexual Behavior/Reputation

1. **Written Motion (Pre-Trial)**
   - Specific description of evidence sought
   - Offer of proof (what will evidence show)
   - Reason evidence falls within exception

2. **Motion Hearing**
   - In camera, outside jury presence
   - Defense presents evidence; prosecution responds
   - Judge determines admissibility in writing

3. **If Admitted:**
   - Jury receives specific limiting instruction
   - Instruction defines scope (e.g., "solely on issue of consent")

### When Prosecution Seeks Admission of Victim Sexual Behavior

1. Similar written motion and hearing process
2. Burden on prosecution to show exception applies
3. Victim may request closed-circuit TV or protective measures
