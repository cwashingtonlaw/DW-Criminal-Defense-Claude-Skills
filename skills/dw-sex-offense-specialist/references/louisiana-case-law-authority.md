# Louisiana Case Law Authority

## Death Penalty Context

**State v. Kennedy, 957 So.2d 757 (La. 2007); Kennedy v. Louisiana, 554 U.S. 407 (2008)**
- Louisiana allowed death penalty for rape of child
- U.S. Supreme Court held death penalty for non-homicide rape violates Eighth Amendment
- Louisiana capital rape statute struck down
- **Current Impact:** No death penalty for sex offenses; maximum LWOP

## CSAAS Admissibility

**State v. Foret, 628 So.2d 1116 (La. 1993)**
- Louisiana recognizes child sexual abuse accommodation syndrome
- Expert testimony on CSAAS is admissible
- Purpose: explain victim's behavioral choices, not establish guilt
- **Defense Use:** Challenge reliability for guilt/innocence inference

**State v. Chauvin, 769 So.2d 1059 (La. App. 4 Cir. 1989)**
- Child competency framework
- Examines reliability of child witness testimony
- **Modern Application:** Recent courts more skeptical of young child testimony reliability

## Confrontation Clause

**Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004)**
- Testimonial statements by witnesses unavailable for cross-examination violate Sixth Amendment
- **Sex Offense Application:** If alleged victim available, must testify (cannot use recorded interview as substitute)

**Davis v. Washington, 547 U.S. 813 (2006)**
- Distinguishes testimonial from non-testimonial statements
- 911 calls describing ongoing emergency may be non-testimonial
- Statements made to police describing past events generally testimonial
- **Sex Offense Application:** Most forensic interviews are testimonial; prosecution must produce child witness

## Delayed Disclosure

**State v. Broadway**
- Louisiana recognizes delayed disclosure as normal trauma response
- Defense may educate jury that delay does not indicate fabrication
- Expert testimony on delayed disclosure dynamics admissible

## Jury Instructions & Propensity

**La. C.Cr.P. Art. 813**
- Jury instructions must not permit propensity inferences (prior acts to show propensity to commit crime)
- Must limit to specific purposes (modus operandi, identity, plan, knowledge)
- **Sex Offense Context:** CSAAS and prior-acts evidence require limiting instructions
