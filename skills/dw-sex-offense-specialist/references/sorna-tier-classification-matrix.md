# SORNA Tier Classification Matrix

## Tier Classification System (La. R.S. 15:540 et seq.)

| Tier | Offenses | Duration | Community Notification |
|------|----------|----------|------------------------|
| **Tier 1** | Simple sexual battery, indecent behavior | 10 years | No public notification |
| **Tier 2** | Sexual battery, aggravated sexual battery, oral sexual battery, crime against nature | 25 years | Limited notification (law enforcement, schools, institutions) |
| **Tier 3** | Rape, aggravated rape, molestation of juvenile, pornography involving juveniles, human trafficking for sexual purposes | Lifetime | Public notification (community, media, website) |

**Strategic Note:** Difference between Tier 1 and Tier 3 is enormous for plea negotiations.

## Registration Requirements

**1. Reporting Requirements:**
- In-person registration within 10 days of release
- Annual renewal (some jurisdictions quarterly)
- Address, employment, school enrollment changes within 10 days
- Internet identifiers (usernames, email accounts) must be provided
- Failure to register: felony charge

**2. Residency Restrictions:**
- Cannot reside within 1,000 feet of school, childcare facility, park
- Restriction applies throughout state
- May be homeless but must register
- **Practical Effect:** Severe housing limitations in urban areas

**3. Internet Restrictions (La. R.S. 14:91.5):**
- Cannot use Internet anonymously
- Cannot knowingly access minor-oriented chat rooms
- Cannot have online profile without disclosure of offender status
- Restrictions vary by tier but all are significant

## Employment Consequences

- Many employers conduct sex offender registry checks
- Government employment generally barred
- Professional licenses (teaching, nursing, counseling) often revoked or impossible to obtain
- Private employers frequently refuse employment

## Collateral Consequences

- Passport restrictions
- Housing discrimination
- Educational exclusions
- Financial aid ineligibility
- Mandatory treatment costs
- Polygraph testing requirements
- Vehicle registration restrictions

## Tier Assignment & Judicial Discretion

**Process:**
1. Statute specifies tier based on conviction
2. Limited judicial discretion in some circumstances
3. Appeal possible if incorrect tier assigned

**Defense Opportunity in Plea Negotiations:**
- Charging decisions affect tier classification
- Plea to lower-degree charge may reduce tier assignment
- Example: Plead to sexual battery (Tier 2) instead of rape (Tier 3)
- **Value Analysis:** Lifetime registration (Tier 3) vs. 25-year (Tier 2) is enormous bargaining point

## Juvenile Adjudication Registration Issues

**Special Rules:**
- Juveniles can be required to register
- Tier assignment based on offense (same as adults)
- Duration may be shorter (10–25 years depending on tier)
- Removal from registry possible if offense adjudicated before age 12 and met conditions

**Defense Strategy:**
- Negotiate for expungement eligibility
- Challenge registration for juvenile adjudications (due process arguments)
- Explore delinquency vs. criminal prosecution (registration consequences differ)
