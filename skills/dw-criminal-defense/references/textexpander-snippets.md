# D&W Standard Boilerplate Snippets

Use these exact text blocks when drafting any document. Do not paraphrase or modify the firm's standard language. Fill in variables shown in `[BRACKETS]` with case-specific data.

## Quick Reference — Which Snippets to Use

| Document Type | Snippets Used |
|--------------|---------------|
| Any motion | Caption, Signature, Certificate of Service, Draft Disclaimer |
| Discovery demand | Caption, Discovery Citations, Signature, Certificate of Service |
| Suppression motion | Caption, Miranda Reference, Signature, Certificate of Service |
| Sentencing memo | Caption, Excessiveness Standard, Burden of Proof, Signature |
| Client letter | Privileged Marking, Signature (modified for correspondence) |
| Internal memo | Work Product Marking, Draft Disclaimer |

## Burden of Proof Statement

```
The State bears the burden of proving every element of the charged
offense beyond a reasonable doubt. La. C.Cr.P. Art. 804; Jackson v.
Virginia, 443 U.S. 307 (1979). The defendant is presumed innocent,
and this presumption remains with the defendant throughout the trial.
La. C.Cr.P. Art. 804(B).
```

## Excessiveness Standard

```
Louisiana Constitution Article I, § 20 prohibits cruel, excessive, or
unusual punishment. A sentence is constitutionally excessive if it is
grossly disproportionate to the severity of the offense or is nothing
more than a purposeless and needless imposition of pain and suffering.
State v. Dorthey, 623 So.2d 1276 (La. 1993); State v. Barling,
779 So.2d 1035 (La. App. 2001).
```
