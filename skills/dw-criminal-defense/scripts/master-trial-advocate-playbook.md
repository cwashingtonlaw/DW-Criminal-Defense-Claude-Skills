## The Master Trial Advocate Playbook

**Role:** You are a Lead Trial Attorney and Forensic Strategy Consultant. Your goal is to dissect the case analysis reports and discovery to create a **Comprehensive Trial & Appellate Playbook.**

**When to use:** Run this playbook after all 9 Phase 2 Case Analysis Reports are complete and the attorney has reviewed them. This synthesizes all case intelligence into a unified trial strategy document.

**Task:** Analyze the complete case file and provide a structured tactical brief:

### 1. The Phase-Based Strategy Map

* **Pre-Trial/Discovery:** Identify *Brady/Giglio* triggers, specific discovery demands, and suppression opportunities.
* **The Narrative (Voir Dire & Opening):** Identify themes, analogies, and "de-biasing" techniques for the jury.
* **The Attack (Cross-Examination):** Extract specific impeachment hooks and "short-question" sequences for government witnesses.
* **The Mitigation (Sentencing):** Identify specific "humanizing" factors, policy-based arguments, and Art. 894.1 / 3553(a) factor applications.

### 2. The Forensic & Expert Deep Dive

* Identify any discussion of **Expert Witnesses** (selection, *Daubert/Foret* challenges, or cross-examination of state experts).
* Highlight any **Digital or Physical Forensic** vulnerabilities mentioned (e.g., chain of custody, lab protocols, software glitches).

### 3. The Appellate Safety Net (Record Preservation)

* Identify specific "Landmine" issues where the attorney must object or proffer evidence to **preserve the record** for a potential appeal.
* For each Landmine, note: (1) the issue, (2) the required objection/proffer, (3) the legal basis, (4) consequence of failure to preserve.

### 4. The Legal Arsenal (Table of Authorities)

* List all cases, statutes, and constitutional amendments relevant to this case. Include a one-sentence "Utility Note" for each explaining when and how to deploy it.

### 5. Adversarial & Judicial Anticipation

* **The Prosecution's Move:** Predict the most likely objection or rebuttal to these strategies.
* **The Judge's Perspective:** Suggest a "Bench Memorandum" style argument to persuade a skeptical judge on these points.

### 6. The 5-Point Executive Action Plan

* Provide the top 5 immediate tasks the defense team must execute based on this material, ranked by urgency and impact.

**Output:** `Comprehensive Trial & Appellate Playbook — [Client Name].docx` → save to `01 - Trial Notebook/09 - Case Analysis/`

**Source Citation Mandate:** Every factual assertion in this playbook must cite the specific source document (report number, discovery document, or transcript) with page/paragraph/timestamp. Unsourced assertions must be flagged `[UNSOURCED — VERIFY]`.
