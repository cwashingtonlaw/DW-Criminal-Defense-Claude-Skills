# DWI Enhancement Ladder: Louisiana R.S. 14:98

## Overview

DWI penalties in Louisiana escalate based on:
1. Number of prior DWI convictions within 10-year cleansing period
2. BAC level (≥0.15, ≥0.20 aggravate penalties)
3. Presence of child passenger under 13 (R.S. 14:98.5)
4. Accident with injury

**10-Year Cleansing Period:** Convictions older than 10 years do NOT count toward offense level.

---

## First Offense (R.S. 14:98(A))

**Offense:** Operating a motor vehicle while under the influence of alcohol/drugs OR with BAC ≥ 0.08% (or 0.04% if CDL)

**Penalties:**
- Fine: $300-$1,000
- Jail: 10 days to 6 months (48 hours mandatory minimum jail OR 32 hours minimum community service)
- Driver's License Suspension: 90 days

**Ignition Interlock (R.S. 32:378.2):**
- Optional: First offender may request ignition interlock in lieu of license suspension

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.15:**
- Fine: $300-$1,000 (no change)
- Jail: same
- License Suspension: ENHANCED to 1 year (vs. 90 days)

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.20:**
- Fine: ENHANCED to $400-$1,000
- Jail: same
- License Suspension: 1 year

**Child Passenger Under 13 (R.S. 14:98.5):**
- Offense becomes felony (from misdemeanor)
- Jail: 48 hours minimum mandatory (increased from 48 hrs OR 32 hrs community service)
- Fine: $500-$1,000
- License Suspension: 1 year (vs. 90 days)

---

## Second Offense (R.S. 14:98(B))

**Requirement:** Prior DWI conviction within 10 years

**Penalties:**
- Fine: $750-$1,000
- Jail: 30 days to 6 months (48 hours mandatory minimum)
- Driver's License Suspension: 2 years
- Ignition Interlock: MANDATORY (minimum 1 year)
- Substance Abuse Evaluation: Required

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.15:**
- Fine: ENHANCED to $800-$1,000
- Jail: 30 days-6 months (same)
- License Suspension: 3 years (vs. 2 years)

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.20:**
- Fine: $1,000
- Jail: 30 days-6 months (same)
- License Suspension: 3 years

**Child Passenger Under 13:**
- Offense becomes felony
- Jail: 48 hours mandatory minimum (same as 1st with child)
- Fine: $1,000-$1,500
- License Suspension: 2 years

---

## Third Offense (R.S. 14:98(C))

**Requirement:** Two prior DWI convictions within 10 years

**Penalties:**
- Fine: $2,000
- Imprisonment: 1-5 years (1 year mandatory minimum)
- Driver's License Suspension: 4 years
- Vehicle Seizure: MANDATORY (vehicle subject to forfeiture)
- Ignition Interlock: MANDATORY
- Substance Abuse Treatment: MANDATORY

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.15:**
- Fine: ENHANCED to $2,500
- Imprisonment: 1-5 years (same)
- License Suspension: 5 years (vs. 4 years)

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.20:**
- Fine: $3,000
- Imprisonment: 1-5 years (same)
- License Suspension: 5 years

**Child Passenger Under 13:**
- Increases to felony classification
- Fine: $3,000-$5,000
- Imprisonment: 2-8 years (2 years mandatory minimum)
- License Suspension: 4 years

---

## Fourth and Subsequent Offense (R.S. 14:98(D))

**Requirement:** Three or more prior DWI convictions within 10 years

**Penalties:**
- Fine: $5,000
- Imprisonment: 10-30 years (10 years mandatory minimum)
- Driver's License Revocation: PERMANENT
- Vehicle Seizure: MANDATORY
- Ignition Interlock: MANDATORY (if eventually permitted to drive)
- Substance Abuse Treatment: MANDATORY
- Parole Ineligibility: 10 years minimum before parole eligible

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.15:**
- Fine: $5,000 (same)
- Imprisonment: 10-30 years (same)
- Parole Ineligibility: 15 years minimum

**Special Consideration — BAC ≥ 0.20:**
- Fine: $5,000 (same)
- Imprisonment: 10-30 years (same)
- Parole Ineligibility: 20 years minimum

**Child Passenger Under 13:**
- Fine: $7,500-$10,000
- Imprisonment: 15-30 years (15 years mandatory minimum)
- Parole Ineligibility: 15 years minimum

## Key Definitions

**Mandatory Minimum:** Judge MUST sentence to at least this term (no exceptions, no suspending this portion of jail/prison time)

**License Suspension:** Driving privilege revoked for specified period; eligibility for reinstatement after period

**License Revocation:** Permanent; application for reinstatement required (and may be denied)

**Ignition Interlock Device (IID):** Vehicle start requires breath sample showing BAC < 0.02%; records download at service location for monitoring

**Cleansing Period (10 years):** Convictions older than 10 years from date of current offense do NOT count toward offense level; client "resets" to first offense if prior is >10 years old

## Aggravating Factors Checklist

- [ ] BAC ≥ 0.15% (enhances penalties)
- [ ] BAC ≥ 0.20% (further enhances)
- [ ] Child passenger under 13 in vehicle (R.S. 14:98.5 — felony enhancement)
- [ ] Accident with property damage
- [ ] Accident with injury to another person
- [ ] Refusal to submit to test (separate charge under R.S. 32:661)
- [ ] License suspended/revoked at time of offense

---

## Mitigating Factors to Investigate

- [ ] Client's age and driving record (prior traffic violations only, if any)
- [ ] Circumstances of traffic stop (checkpoint vs. individualized suspicion)
- [ ] Quality of SFST administration (protocol deviations favor client)
- [ ] Testing methodology (breath calibration issues, blood chain of custody)
- [ ] Medical conditions mimicking impairment (diabetes, hypoglycemia, inner ear disorder)
- [ ] Rising BAC defense applicability
- [ ] Substance abuse history (treatability for favorable sentence consideration)
- [ ] Employment status, family stability
- [ ] Voluntary enrollment in treatment/counseling
