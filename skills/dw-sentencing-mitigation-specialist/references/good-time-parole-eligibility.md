# Good Time Credits & Parole Eligibility Calculator

## Louisiana Good Time Credits (La. R.S. 15:571.3)

### Good Time Earning Rates

| Offense Category | Good Time Rate | Effective Service Rate |
|-----------------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Non-violent, non-sex offense** (convicted on or after 8/1/2020) | Earn 13 days credit for every 7 days served | Serve approximately 35% of sentence |
| **Non-violent, non-sex offense** (convicted before 8/1/2020; after 6/15/1995) | Earn credit at rate of 1 day for every 1 day served (varies by date) | Serve approximately 50% of sentence |
| **Crime of violence** (La. R.S. 14:2(B) enumerated offenses) | Not eligible for good time | Serve 100% of sentence (unless parole eligible) |
| **Sex offense** | Not eligible for good time | Serve 100% of sentence (unless parole eligible) |
| **Drug offenses (non-violent)** | Standard good time rates apply | Varies by conviction date |

**Critical Notes:**
- Good time rates have changed multiple times; the conviction date (not offense date) determines the applicable rate
- 2017 Justice Reinvestment Act (Act 280) significantly changed good time calculations
- 2020 amendments (Act 132) further modified earning rates for non-violent offenses
- Good time is **earned** credit -- can be forfeited for disciplinary violations
- Some offenses carry restrictions on good time regardless of violence classification

## Louisiana Parole Eligibility (La. R.S. 15:574.4)

### Parole Eligibility Rules

| Sentence Type | Parole Eligibility | Authority |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Non-violent offense, sentence < 10 years** | Eligible after serving 25% of sentence | La. R.S. 15:574.4(A)(1) |
| **Non-violent offense, sentence >= 10 years** | Eligible after serving 25% of sentence | La. R.S. 15:574.4(A)(1) |
| **Crime of violence, first offense** | Eligible after serving 65% of sentence | La. R.S. 15:574.4(A)(1) |
| **Crime of violence, second offense** | Eligible after serving 75% of sentence | La. R.S. 15:574.4(B)(1) |
| **Life sentence (non-LWOP)** | Eligible after serving 20 years (with Committee of Pardons recommendation) | La. R.S. 15:574.4(E) |
| **LWOP** | Not eligible for parole | -- |
| **Sex offense (certain)** | Not eligible for parole | La. R.S. 15:574.4(B)(2) |
| **Habitual offender (4th+, violent)** | Not eligible for parole (LWOP) | La. R.S. 15:529.1 |

**Note:** Parole eligibility does not guarantee parole; it means the client becomes eligible to be considered by the Committee on Parole.

## Quick Calculator Reference

```
NON-VIOLENT OFFENSE (post-8/1/2020):
  Good time rate: ~35% of sentence served
  Parole eligibility: 25% of sentence
  Example: 10-year sentence
    Parole eligible: ~2.5 years
    Good time release: ~3.5 years
    Full sentence: 10 years

CRIME OF VIOLENCE (first offense):
  Good time: NOT ELIGIBLE
  Parole eligibility: 65% of sentence
  Example: 10-year sentence
    Parole eligible: ~6.5 years
    Full sentence: 10 years

CRIME OF VIOLENCE (second offense):
  Good time: NOT ELIGIBLE
  Parole eligibility: 75% of sentence
  Example: 10-year sentence
    Parole eligible: ~7.5 years
    Full sentence: 10 years

NOTE: These are approximations. Actual calculations depend on
conviction date, specific offense, and current DOC policy.
Always verify against La. R.S. 15:571.3 and 15:574.4 as
currently in effect.
```
