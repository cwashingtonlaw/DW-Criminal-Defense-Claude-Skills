# Key Sentencing Case Law Quick Reference

| Case | Citation | Principle |
|------|----------|-----------|
| State v. Dorthey | 623 So.2d 1276 (La. 1993) | Mandatory minimum unconstitutional as applied if no measurable contribution to punishment goals |
| State v. Johnson | 709 So.2d 672 (La. 1998) | Rebuttable presumption mandatory minimum is constitutional; clear and convincing evidence standard |
| State v. Barling | 779 So.2d 1035 (La. App. 2001) | Court must comply with Art. 894.1 and state factual basis for sentence |
| State v. Smith | 846 So.2d 786 (La. App. 2003) | Sentencing must be individualized to the particular defendant and offense |
| State v. Sepulvado | 367 So.2d 762 (La. 1979) | Maximum sentence reserved for worst offenders and worst offenses |
| State v. Shelton | 621 So.2d 769 (La. 1993) | State must prove habitual offender predicates beyond reasonable doubt |
| State v. Lisotta | 98-648 (La. App. 5th Cir. 1999) | Court need not recite every 894.1 factor but must show adequate consideration |
| State v. Montgomery | 270 So.3d 671 (La. App. 2019) | Louisiana Miller implementation; juvenile resentencing procedures |
| Solem v. Helm | 463 U.S. 277 (1983) | Three-factor proportionality review under 8th Amendment |
| Harmelin v. Michigan | 501 U.S. 957 (1991) | Narrow proportionality principle for non-capital cases |
| Graham v. Florida | 560 U.S. 48 (2010) | LWOP for juvenile non-homicide offenders unconstitutional |
| Miller v. Alabama | 567 U.S. 460 (2012) | Mandatory juvenile LWOP unconstitutional; individualized sentencing required |
| Montgomery v. Louisiana | 577 U.S. 190 (2016) | Miller applies retroactively to existing LWOP juvenile sentences |
| Jones v. Mississippi | 593 U.S. 98 (2021) | Miller does not require permanent incorrigibility finding; individualized consideration sufficient |
| Roper v. Simmons | 543 U.S. 551 (2005) | Death penalty for juveniles unconstitutional |
| United States v. Booker | 543 U.S. 220 (2005) | Federal Guidelines are advisory, not mandatory |
| Gall v. United States | 552 U.S. 38 (2007) | Variance from Guidelines reviewed for reasonableness; not proportionality to Guidelines range |
| Kimbrough v. United States | 552 U.S. 85 (2007) | Court may disagree with Guidelines policy in exercising SS 3553(a) discretion |
