# Habitual Offender (La. R.S. 15:529.1) Quick Reference

> ⚠️ **Offense-date note:** The applicable version of La. R.S. 15:529.1 is fixed by the **date of offense** — confirm it first and select the version per `dw-shared-protocols/references/sentencing-statute-versions.md`. **Current law (offenses on/after Nov. 1, 2017):** 2nd offender **⅓**–2×; 3rd (non-life) **½**–2×; 4th-offender mandatory LWOP requires the 4th felony **and two prior** crimes of violence/sex offenses. Pre-2017 vintages differ — verify via Westlaw version-compare. (This skill is for mitigation; the dedicated `dw-habitual-offender-auditor` owns enhancement analysis.)

## Sentencing Enhancement by Offender Status

```
SECOND OFFENDER:
  Min: 1/3 of statutory maximum for current offense (current; offenses pre-Nov. 1, 2017 used 1/2)
  Max: 2x statutory maximum for current offense

THIRD OFFENDER:
  Min: 2/3 of statutory maximum for current offense
  Max: 2x statutory maximum for current offense

FOURTH OFFENDER (non-violent/non-sex):
  Min: 20 years
  Max: 2x statutory maximum for current offense (or life, whichever is greater)

FOURTH OFFENDER (violent or sex offense):
  Mandatory: Life without parole (LWOP)

CLEANSING PERIOD:
  10 years from date of completion of sentence for most offenses
  Conviction must fall within window to serve as predicate

CHALLENGE POINTS:
  - Boykinization of predicate pleas
  - Right to counsel in predicate cases
  - Expungement or pardon of predicates
  - Same-transaction predicates (cannot be separate offenses)
  - Dorthey relief from constitutionally excessive habitual sentence
```

## Enhanced Exposure Calculation

| Offender Status | Enhancement Rule | Minimum | Maximum |
|----------------|-----------------|---------|---------|
| **Second Offender** | Not less than one-third the longest term and not more than twice the longest term *(current; pre-2017 = one-half)* | 1/3 of max | 2x max |
| **Third Offender** | Not less than two-thirds the longest term and not more than twice the longest term | 2/3 of max | 2x max |
| **Fourth Offender (non-violent)** | Not less than 20 years and not more than the longest possible term x 2 | 20 years | 2x max |
| **Fourth Offender (violent / sex)** | Life imprisonment without parole | LWOP | LWOP |

**Critical Notes:**
- "Longest term" means the maximum sentence for the current offense of conviction
- La. R.S. 15:529.1(G): Court **shall** impose habitual offender sentence unless State agrees to withdraw the bill
- Predicate offenses must meet cleansing period requirements (10-year window for most offenses)
- Verify each predicate: proper Boykinization, non-expunged, not pardoned
- State v. Shelton, 621 So.2d 769 (La. 1993): State must prove predicate convictions beyond reasonable doubt
- State v. Johnson, 432 So.2d 815 (La. 1983): Defendant must be advised of right to a hearing

## Habitual Offender Challenge Checklist

```
☐ Were predicate convictions properly Boykinized?
☐ Were predicates obtained with counsel (or valid waiver)?
☐ Are predicates within the cleansing period?
☐ Has any predicate been expunged, pardoned, or set aside?
☐ Were predicates from different criminal episodes (not same transaction)?
☐ Did the State prove predicates beyond a reasonable doubt?
☐ Was the defendant advised of right to hearing and counsel?
☐ Is Dorthey relief available (sentence constitutionally excessive)?
```
