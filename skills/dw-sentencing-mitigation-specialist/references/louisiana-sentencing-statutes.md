# Louisiana Sentencing Statutes Quick Reference

This is a hand-curated lookup table of the Louisiana sentencing statutes most commonly cited by `dw-sentencing-mitigation-specialist`. It is intentionally a quick-reference matrix, not a treatise — confirm current text against Westlaw or the Louisiana Legislature's site before filing, since the legislature has revised many of these provisions repeatedly.

## Sentencing procedure & post-conviction motions

| Statute | Subject | Key Provision |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 871 | Definition of sentence | Sentence is the punishment imposed by the court upon a defendant; sentence imposed orally or in writing controls (oral pronouncement is authoritative) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 873 | 24-hour sentencing delay | Court must wait at least 24 hours after denial of motion for new trial / arrest of judgment before sentencing (felony), unless waived expressly on the record |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 875–878 | Sentencing procedure | PSI authority; sentencing hearing requirements; credit for time served |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 878.1 | Juvenile sentencing (post-Miller) | Prohibits mandatory LWOP for juveniles; requires individualized hearing under *Miller v. Alabama*, 567 U.S. 460 (2012); applies retroactively per *Montgomery v. Louisiana*, 577 U.S. 190 (2016) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 881–881.8 | Reconsideration of sentence | 30-day window after sentencing; motion preserves appellate review; specific grounds required (Art. 881.1) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 882 | Illegal sentence | Illegal sentences may be corrected at any time by the trial court that imposed it or by an appellate court on review (errors-patent reviewable; *State v. Oliveaux*, 312 So.2d 337 (La. 1975)) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 883 | Concurrent sentences | Default is concurrent unless court orders consecutive on the record |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 883.1 | Mandatory consecutive | Certain crimes of violence with firearm; offenses against different victims |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 883.2 | Restitution | Mandatory restitution to victims; failure-to-pay procedures; modification |

## Suspension, probation, and departure

| Statute | Subject | Key Provision |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 890 | Suspension of sentence | Authority to suspend and place on probation (does not apply where mandatory minimum prohibits suspension) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 890.1 | Drug offense departure | Departure below mandatory minimum for certain drug offenses (see also La. R.S. 40:983 drug court) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 890.3 | Youthful offender departure | Departure below mandatory minimum for offenders under 18 at time of offense |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 893 | Probation | Suspension of sentence and probation eligibility; conditions; revocation grounds; max 5-year supervision (most felonies) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 893.1 | Firearm penalty | Additional penalty for use of firearm during felony (note: La. R.S. 14:64.3 is the current armed-robbery firearm enhancement; Art. 893.1 is the procedural counterpart) |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 893.3 | Felony firearm penalty | Mandatory consecutive 5-year sentence for use of a firearm during a felony in certain situations |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 894 | Misdemeanor probation | Misdemeanor suspension and probation; conviction set-aside provisions for first offense |
| La. C.Cr.P. Art. 894.1 | Sentencing factors | 33-factor checklist; factors favoring imprisonment vs. suspension; court must state basis on the record (failure = errors-patent flag) |

## Habitual offender, enhancements, and crimes of violence

| Statute | Subject | Key Provision |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| La. R.S. 14:2(B) | "Crime of violence" definition | Enumerated list of offenses classified as crimes of violence; impacts good-time, parole, and habitual-offender exposure |
| La. R.S. 14:64.3 | Armed robbery firearm enhancement | Additional 5 years consecutive when armed robbery committed with a firearm |
| La. R.S. 14:95.1 | Felon in possession of firearm | 5–20 years; consecutive with sentence for any felony of which firearm-possession was an element |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1 | Habitual offender | Enhanced penalties for repeat offenders; 5-year cleansing period (most offenses); LWOP for fourth-felony where two or more are crimes of violence; *State v. Shelton*, 621 So.2d 769 (La. 1993) burden-shifting framework for predicate-plea challenges |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(A)(4)(b) | Mandatory LWOP under habitual law | Triggers under specific predicate-conviction patterns; *State v. Dorthey*, 623 So.2d 1276 (La. 1993) constitutional-excessiveness escape valve, narrowed by *State v. Johnson*, 97-1906 (La. 3/4/98), 709 So.2d 672 |
| La. R.S. 15:301.1 | Self-activation of statutory restrictions | Restrictions on parole / probation / suspension are deemed contained in the sentence by operation of law; eliminates need for remand on illegally lenient sentences (*State v. Williams*, 2000-1725 (La. 11/28/01), 800 So.2d 790, 798-99) |
