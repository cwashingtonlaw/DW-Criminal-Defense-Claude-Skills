## How the Skill Picks Cases per Bucket

For each body-region bucket the skill is building:

1. Match the body region to the bank section (A through P above).
2. Subset cases by severity tier:
   - **Mild** (no imaging finding, full recovery within 3–6 months) → bottom of the tier range
   - **Moderate** (imaging finding, conservative treatment, residual symptoms) → middle of the tier range
   - **Severe** (surgery, permanency rating, ongoing functional loss) → top of the tier range
3. Pick 4–8 cases that best match the plaintiff's profile.
4. Order them by award descending (highest first) — anchor with the high cases.
5. Build the recommendation amount that's in the upper-middle of the comp-case range — defensible, supported, and matches what a Louisiana jury would likely award for a similarly situated plaintiff.
