# Worked Example — End-to-End Walkthrough

A hypothetical case that exercises every step of the skill's 13-step workflow. Used as a calibration reference so the skill produces output of comparable volume and style to the firm's corpus.

---

## Hypothetical Scenario

**Client:** Mary Elizabeth Jones, 42-year-old female elementary-school teacher in Lake Charles. Mother of two (ages 9 and 12). Husband works offshore (4-week rotation). Mrs. Jones has been with Calcasieu Parish Schools for 14 years.

**Crash:** February 3, 2025, 4:12 PM. Mrs. Jones is stopped at a red light at the intersection of Ryan Street and Sale Road in Lake Charles. Defendant Brittany Smith, 28-year-old delivery driver for FastShip Logistics, fails to stop and rear-ends Mrs. Jones's 2020 Toyota Camry at approximately 32 mph. Smith admits at the scene she was looking at her phone. LSP Trooper Rivera issues Smith a citation for La. R.S. 32:81 (Following Too Closely). Smith pays the citation on February 27, 2025.

**Injuries:**
- Cervical strain with imaging-confirmed C5-C6 disc bulge (no surgical recommendation; conservative management with PT and one epidural injection)
- Lumbar strain with imaging-confirmed L4-L5 disc bulge (no surgical recommendation; conservative management with PT and two epidural injections)
- Closed-head contusion at scene; no LOC; subsequent post-concussion-syndrome workup negative

**Treatment:** Lake Charles ER day-of-crash; subsequent treatment with Acadiana Spine Group (orthopedics) March–November 2025; physical therapy at Rehab Plus from March 2025 through October 2025; chiropractic with Vermaelen Chiropractic March 2025 through June 2025; pain management at Lake Charles Pain Center for two epidural injections in July and October 2025.

**Past medicals:** $25,408.50 (specifically: ER $3,200; orthopedist $7,850; PT $4,800; chiropractic $3,200; pain management $5,358.50; imaging $1,000)

**Past lost wages:** $4,200 (15 missed school days at $280/day average)

**Future medicals:** Mrs. Jones may need recurring epidural injections (one every 12 months) and ongoing PT 6 visits/year per her treating orthopedist. Cost estimate: $4,200/year × estimated remaining worklife / life expectancy.

**Future lost wages:** Not factored in — Mrs. Jones is back to full-duty teaching.

**Posture:** Suit not yet filed. Defense carrier is Progressive (policy limits $250k/500k). Mediation scheduled in 30 days with Perry Dampf Dispute Solutions (Keith Richardson assigned). Mrs. Jones wants to resolve.

## Output Volume Calibration

For this scenario (mid-complexity mediation paper), expect approximately:
- **8–12 pages** in the body of the paper
- 1 ICD-10 / PARAMETER grid table or an injury bulleted list
- 1 per-provider charges table
- 1 future-medical projection table
- 2 quantum-case buckets, 3–6 cases each
- 1 ADL impact list (8 domains)
- 1 optional client block-quote
- Total attached exhibits: medical chronology, expense worksheet, LSP crash report, Mrs. Jones's deposition excerpts, vehicle damage photos (4–5 exhibits)

If the skill produces less than 6 pages or more than 15 pages for a case of this profile, reconsider section depth and exhibit-vs-inline allocation.
