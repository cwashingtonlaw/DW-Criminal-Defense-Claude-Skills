# Module F — Background Investigation Checklist

Generate background investigation protocols for prosecution witnesses and other relevant individuals.

## Background Investigation — Standard Protocol

For each prosecution witness, conduct the following background investigation:

**Public Records Search:**
- [ ] Criminal history check — Louisiana (all parishes)
- [ ] Criminal history check — NCIC/national (if accessible)
- [ ] Civil litigation search — plaintiff and defendant
- [ ] Protective/restraining order search — petitioner and respondent
- [ ] Traffic citation and accident history
- [ ] Bankruptcy filings
- [ ] Tax liens and judgments
- [ ] Property records
- [ ] Voter registration (address verification)
- [ ] Professional license verification
- [ ] Sex offender registry check
- [ ] Department of Corrections records

**Social Media Investigation:**
- [ ] Facebook (profile, posts, photos, check-ins, friends list)
- [ ] Instagram (profile, posts, stories, tagged photos)
- [ ] Twitter/X (profile, posts, replies, likes)
- [ ] Snapchat (username, public content)
- [ ] TikTok (profile, videos, comments)
- [ ] LinkedIn (employment, education, connections)
- [ ] YouTube (channel, videos, comments)
- [ ] Reddit (username, post history, comments)
- [ ] Other platforms as identified
- [ ] Preserve all relevant social media with screenshots and timestamps
- [ ] Note privacy settings and accessibility of content

**Relationship and Bias Investigation:**
- [ ] Relationship to victim (family, romantic, friendship, business, adversarial)
- [ ] Relationship to defendant (family, romantic, friendship, business, adversarial)
- [ ] Relationship to other witnesses
- [ ] Relationship to law enforcement (family, friendship, employment)
- [ ] Relationship to prosecution (prior cooperation, pending cases, deals)
- [ ] Financial interest in outcome
- [ ] History of false reporting or false testimony
- [ ] Pending criminal charges (potential for cooperation motive)
- [ ] Probation or parole status (potential for cooperation motive)
- [ ] Civil lawsuit arising from this incident (financial motive)
- [ ] Insurance claim arising from this incident (financial motive)

**Credibility Investigation:**
- [ ] Prior testimony in other cases — review for inconsistencies
- [ ] Prior statements in this case — compare all versions
- [ ] Reputation evidence in the community
- [ ] Known substance abuse issues (perception reliability)
- [ ] Known mental health issues (perception and memory reliability)
- [ ] Vision and hearing capability
- [ ] Educational background and literacy (statement comprehension)

## Background Investigation Report Format

```
BACKGROUND INVESTIGATION REPORT

Subject: [Full Name]
DOB: [Date of Birth]          SSN Last 4: [XXXX]
Address: [Current Address]
Role in Case: [Prosecution witness / Victim / Co-defendant / Other]
Report Date: [Date]
Investigator: [Name / License No.]

SUMMARY OF FINDINGS:
[Brief narrative of significant findings]

CRIMINAL HISTORY:
[Detailed criminal history with case numbers, charges, dispositions, sentences]

CIVIL LITIGATION:
[Summary of civil cases as plaintiff or defendant]

SOCIAL MEDIA FINDINGS:
[Summary of relevant social media content with screenshots preserved separately]

RELATIONSHIP/BIAS INDICATORS:
[Summary of relationships and potential bias factors]

CREDIBILITY CONCERNS:
[Summary of any credibility issues identified]

IMPEACHMENT POTENTIAL:
[Assessment of impeachment value — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW with explanation]

ATTACHMENTS:
[List of supporting documents, screenshots, and records]
```
