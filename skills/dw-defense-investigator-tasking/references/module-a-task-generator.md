# Module A — Investigation Task Generator

After extracting case information, generate a comprehensive investigation task list organized by priority and category.

## Task Priority Framework

| Priority | Definition | Deadline Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **CRITICAL** | Tasks that must be completed before evidence is lost, witnesses disappear, or deadlines expire | Within 48-72 hours |
| **HIGH** | Tasks essential to defense theory development or prosecution case challenge | Within 1-2 weeks |
| **MEDIUM** | Tasks that strengthen the defense but are not immediately time-sensitive | Within 30 days |
| **LOW** | Tasks for comprehensive preparation but not case-determinative | Before trial |

## Task Categories

For each case, generate tasks across ALL of the following categories as applicable:

**Category 1: Witness Investigation**
- Locate and identify all prosecution witnesses
- Locate and identify potential defense witnesses
- Locate and identify alibi witnesses
- Conduct witness background investigations (see Module F)
- Prepare witness interview questionnaires (see Module B)
- Schedule and conduct witness interviews
- Obtain recorded or written witness statements
- Identify impeachment material for prosecution witnesses
- Identify corroboration for defense witnesses
- Locate rebuttal witnesses

**Category 2: Scene Investigation**
- Conduct scene visit(s) at time of day matching alleged offense
- Conduct scene visit(s) at current time for comparison
- Document scene with photographs (wide, medium, close-up)
- Document scene with video (walkthrough, 360-degree)
- Take measurements (distances, dimensions, sight lines)
- Document lighting conditions (natural and artificial, day and night)
- Identify and document surveillance cameras in area
- Identify and document access/egress points
- Document environmental conditions (noise levels, obstructions, visibility)
- Identify neighboring businesses and residences
- Generate scene visit checklist (see Module C)

**Category 3: Records Investigation**
- Identify records to request or subpoena (see Module D)
- Draft records request letters
- Draft subpoenas duces tecum
- Track records request status
- Analyze received records
- Cross-reference records with case narrative

**Category 4: Canvass Investigation**
- Design canvass area and assignments (see Module E)
- Conduct neighborhood canvass
- Conduct business canvass
- Document canvass results (positive and negative)
- Follow up on canvass leads
- Re-canvass as needed based on new information

**Category 5: Physical Evidence Investigation**
- Review chain of custody documentation
- Identify evidence for independent testing
- Arrange independent forensic testing (DNA, fingerprints, ballistics, toxicology)
- Photograph evidence (if accessible)
- Review crime scene processing procedures
- Identify missing or uncollected evidence
- Challenge evidence handling and storage

**Category 6: Digital/Electronic Investigation**
- Obtain and analyze phone records (calls, texts, data usage, cell tower)
- Obtain and analyze social media accounts (all platforms)
- Review surveillance footage from all available sources
- Analyze GPS/location data
- Review electronic communications (email, messaging apps)
- Preserve digital evidence at risk of deletion
- Identify digital alibi evidence

**Category 7: Expert Consultation**
- Identify expert witness needs
- Research and recommend qualified experts
- Coordinate expert case review
- Arrange expert site visits or evidence examination
- Obtain expert preliminary opinions
- Prepare materials for expert review

**Category 8: Law Enforcement Investigation**
- Obtain officer personnel files (Giglio/Brady material)
- Research officer complaint history
- Review officer testimony in prior cases
- Obtain agency policies and procedures relevant to case
- Review body camera/dash camera footage
- Investigate officer training and certification records
- Research agency disciplinary actions

## Task List Output Format

For each generated task, provide:

```
TASK ID: [Case#]-[Category Abbreviation]-[Sequential Number]
PRIORITY: [CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]
CATEGORY: [Witness / Scene / Records / Canvass / Physical Evidence / Digital / Expert / Law Enforcement]
TASK DESCRIPTION: [Specific, actionable task description]
PURPOSE: [Why this task matters to the defense]
ASSIGNED TO: [Investigator name or "TBD"]
DEADLINE: [Specific date or relative deadline]
SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS: [Any specific guidance for execution]
DOCUMENTATION: [What to produce — report, photos, video, recording, signed statement]
STATUS: [Not Started / In Progress / Complete / Blocked]
NOTES: [Any additional context]
```
