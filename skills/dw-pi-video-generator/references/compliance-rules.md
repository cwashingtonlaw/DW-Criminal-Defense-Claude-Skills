# Louisiana Attorney Advertising Compliance Rules
# For PI Video Content — Daniels & Washington

These rules derive from Louisiana Rules of Professional Conduct 7.1–7.10
and the LSBA Handbook on Solicitation and Lawyer Advertising.

---

## Mandatory Elements in Every Video

### In the Script (spoken by avatar):
1. **Attorney's full name** — "I'm [Full Legal Name] with Daniels & Washington"
2. **City and state** — "Lake Charles, Louisiana"
3. **No result guarantees** — never say "we will win" or promise dollar amounts

### In Instagram & Facebook Captions:
Full disclaimer block (copy exactly):
> This is educational information, not legal advice. No attorney-client
> relationship is created by viewing this content. Results may vary.
> Every case is different. This video uses AI-generated presentation
> technology. Content reviewed and approved by [Attorney Name],
> licensed in Louisiana.

### In TikTok & YouTube Captions:
Abbreviated disclaimer acceptable:
> Educational info, not legal advice. Results may vary. AI-generated presentation.

---

## Prohibited Language

NEVER use any of the following in scripts or captions:

| Prohibited | Why | Use Instead |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| "We will win your case" | Guarantees outcome | "We fight for the best possible outcome" |
| "You'll get $X" | Promises specific amount | "You may be entitled to compensation" |
| "We're the best in Louisiana" | Unverifiable comparison | "We're committed to fighting for you" |
| "No other firm can match us" | Comparison to competitors | [Remove entirely] |
| "Exposed" / "exposed the truth" | Inflammatory language | "Uncovered important evidence" |
| "100% success rate" | Misleading statistic | [Remove entirely] |
| Client names without consent | Privacy violation | "A recent client" or anonymize |

## Permitted Language

These phrases are safe to use:

- "You may be entitled to compensation"
- "You could recover damages for..."
- "Louisiana law provides that..."
- "Many people don't realize..."
- "Call us for a free consultation"
- "You don't pay unless we win" (contingency fee — permitted)
- "Results may vary. Every case is different."
- "We've helped clients across Southwest Louisiana"
- "Our team has [X] years of combined experience" (if verifiable)

## AI Avatar Specific Rules

Louisiana has not issued a specific ethics opinion on AI avatars (as of April 2026),
but these rules apply by extension:

1. **Rule 7.2(c)(1)(I)** — requires disclaimers for depictions that are "not actual or authentic"
   → Every video using the AI avatar must disclose it is AI-generated
   → The disclaimer text handles this: "AI-generated presentation technology"

2. **The avatar should represent the actual attorney** — do not use a stock avatar
   that looks like a different person. The avatar should be a digital twin of the
   attorney whose name appears in the video.

3. **Voice clone must be the attorney's actual voice** — do not use a stock voice
   or a voice clone of someone else. Misrepresentation of who is speaking would
   violate Rule 7.2.

## LSBA Filing Requirements

### What Requires Filing
- Videos with persuasive content + call to action (most PI videos)
- Content that goes beyond "safe harbor" (name, location, practice areas)

### What's Exempt (Rule 7.8)
- Content limited to: firm name, address, phone, website, practice areas,
  attorney bios, office hours, fee arrangements
- Educational content with no CTA *may* be exempt — consult LSBA

### How to File
1. Call LSBA Ethics Counsel: (504) 566-1600
2. Ask about series filing for a consistent video format
3. Submit: copy of video, transcript, filing form, fee
4. File before or concurrently with first dissemination
5. Retain all copies for 5 years

### "Champions for Justice" Moniker
Rule 7.2(c)(1)(N) prohibits monikers that imply the ability to obtain results.
"Champions for Justice" should be formally reviewed by LSBA before use in
advertising. Call (504) 566-1600 to request guidance.

## Compliance Checklist (Run Before Every Script)

- [ ] Script includes attorney's full legal name
- [ ] Script includes "Lake Charles, Louisiana"
- [ ] No promises of specific results or dollar amounts
- [ ] No comparisons to other firms
- [ ] Uses "may/could/might" for potential outcomes
- [ ] CTA is "free consultation" + contingency fee disclosure
- [ ] Instagram caption includes full disclaimer
- [ ] Facebook caption includes full disclaimer
- [ ] TikTok caption includes abbreviated disclaimer
- [ ] YouTube description includes abbreviated disclaimer
- [ ] No client names or identifying details without consent
- [ ] AI avatar disclosure is present in captions
- [ ] Content is educational, not sensational
