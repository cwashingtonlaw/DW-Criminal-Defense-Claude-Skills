# Quick Reference Tables

## Louisiana Habitual Offender Statute — Section Index

| Section | Subject | Key Content |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(A)(1)(a) | Second offender | Enhancement for defendants with one valid predicate felony |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(A)(1)(b)(i) | Third offender (no violence) | Enhancement for two predicates, none involving crimes of violence |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(A)(1)(b)(ii) | Third offender (with violence) | Enhanced range without probation/parole eligibility when violence predicates present |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(A)(1)(c)(i) | Fourth offender (no violence) | Enhancement for three+ predicates, none involving crimes of violence |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(A)(1)(c)(ii) | Fourth offender (with violence) | Mandatory LWOP when violence predicates present |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(C) | Cleansing period | 10-year window between sentence completion and new offense; exceptions for crimes of violence, sex offenses, certain drug offenses |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(D) | Filing procedure | Requirements for filing the habitual offender bill of information |
| La. R.S. 15:529.1(G) | Determination procedure | Post-2017: jury determination (formerly three-judge panel) |

## 2017 Justice Reinvestment Reforms — Impact on Habitual Offender Law

Act 282 of 2017 (effective November 1, 2017) made significant changes to La. R.S. 15:529.1:

| Change | Pre-November 1, 2017 | Post-November 1, 2017 |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Violence distinction** | Enhancement tiers did not distinguish between violent and non-violent predicates | Third and fourth offender tiers split into "with violence" and "without violence" |
| **Fourth offender (no violence)** | Mandatory minimum of 20 years to life | Not less than the longest prescribed time to life |
| **Fourth offender (with violence)** | N/A — no separate category | Mandatory LWOP |
| **Cleansing period** | 10 years; exceptions for crimes of violence and sex offenses | 10 years; expanded exceptions to include certain drug offenses |
| **Determination** | Three-judge panel (La. R.S. 15:529.1(G)) | Jury determination |
| **Applicability** | Applies to offenses committed before November 1, 2017 | Applies to offenses committed on or after November 1, 2017 |

**Critical:** Determine whether the current offense was committed before or after November 1, 2017. The applicable version of the statute is determined by the date of the current offense, not the date of the habitual offender bill filing or the hearing date.

## Crimes of Violence — La. R.S. 14:2(B) Quick Reference

The following is a non-exhaustive list of offenses designated as "crimes of violence" under La. R.S. 14:2(B). **Always verify against the current statutory text** — this list is amended frequently:

| Category | Offenses |
|----------|----------|
| **Homicide** | First degree murder, second degree murder, manslaughter, negligent homicide (certain circumstances), vehicular homicide (certain circumstances) |
| **Sexual Offenses** | First degree rape, second degree rape, third degree rape (simple rape), first degree sexual battery, second degree sexual battery, oral sexual battery, sexual battery of the infirm, human trafficking |
| **Assault/Battery** | Aggravated assault, aggravated battery, second degree battery, aggravated assault with a firearm, assault by drive-by shooting, battery of a police officer (certain circumstances), domestic abuse battery (certain circumstances), battery of a dating partner (certain circumstances) |
| **Robbery** | Armed robbery, first degree robbery, second degree robbery, simple robbery (certain circumstances), carjacking, purse snatching |
| **Kidnapping** | First degree kidnapping, second degree kidnapping, simple kidnapping, aggravated kidnapping |
| **Burglary** | Simple burglary of an inhabited dwelling, aggravated burglary, home invasion |
| **Other** | Arson, terrorism offenses, stalking (felony), cruelty to juveniles, second degree cruelty to juveniles, witness intimidation, obstruction of justice (certain circumstances) |
