# Verification Formats

Some Louisiana criminal pleadings require verification by the defendant or by counsel under oath. This reference covers the standard formats.

## When verification is required

- Motion to recuse (La. C.Cr.P. art. 674) — verified by motion or supported by affidavit
- Motion for change of venue (La. C.Cr.P. art. 622) — affidavit required
- Application for post-conviction relief (La. C.Cr.P. art. 926) — verified by petitioner
- Motion to quash on certain grounds — sometimes requires verification
- Some bond reduction motions where factual representations are made

When unsure, attach a verification — it is rarely improper to verify, but commonly fatal to omit one when required.

## Verification by counsel (most common for procedural motions)

```
                       VERIFICATION

STATE OF LOUISIANA
PARISH OF {{PARISH}}

       BEFORE ME, the undersigned authority, personally came and appeared
CHRISTOPHER J. WASHINGTON, who, being duly sworn, did depose and say
that he is counsel for {{DEFENDANT_NAME}}, that he has read the foregoing
{{MOTION_TITLE}}, and that the allegations of fact contained therein are
true and correct to the best of his knowledge, information, and belief.



                              ___________________________________
                              CHRISTOPHER J. WASHINGTON

Sworn to and subscribed before me this _______ day of ____________, 20___.



                              ___________________________________
                              NOTARY PUBLIC
                              Notary I.D. No.: ___________
```

## Verification by defendant (post-conviction, recusal)

```
                       VERIFICATION

STATE OF LOUISIANA
PARISH OF {{PARISH}}

       BEFORE ME, the undersigned authority, personally came and appeared
{{DEFENDANT_NAME}}, who, being duly sworn, did depose and say that he/she
has read the foregoing {{MOTION_TITLE}}, that the allegations of fact
contained therein are true and correct to the best of his/her knowledge,
information, and belief, and that he/she has authorized the filing of
this {{motion|application}}.



                              ___________________________________
                              {{DEFENDANT_NAME}}

Sworn to and subscribed before me this _______ day of ____________, 20___.



                              ___________________________________
                              NOTARY PUBLIC
                              Notary I.D. No.: ___________
```

## Affidavit format (separate document)

When an affidavit is required as a separate document (e.g., change of venue), use the same notarial framework but title the document `AFFIDAVIT OF [name]` and include numbered factual paragraphs in lieu of an integrated motion.

## Notes

- Verification language is forgiving but missing a verification when one is required can be fatal — always verify the local rule and the article authorizing the motion.
- "to the best of his/her knowledge, information, and belief" is the safe formulation; do not swap for "to the best of my own personal knowledge" unless the affidavit is genuinely first-hand.
- Do not back-date verifications. Notary date controls.
