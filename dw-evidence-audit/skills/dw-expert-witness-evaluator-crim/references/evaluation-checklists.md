# Evaluation Checklists & Motion Templates

## Daubert/Foret Viability Checklist

Use this checklist when evaluating whether a Daubert/Foret challenge is viable:

```
DAUBERT/FORET VIABILITY CHECKLIST
Expert: _________________ Discipline: _________________

QUALIFICATION CHALLENGES:
[ ] Expert lacks degree in relevant discipline
[ ] Expert lacks board certification where one exists
[ ] Expert's certification has lapsed
[ ] Expert lacks experience in the specific analysis at issue
[ ] Expert's training is outdated (10+ years without update)
[ ] Expert has been excluded by another court
[ ] Expert's CV contains exaggerations or omissions

METHODOLOGY CHALLENGES:
[ ] Method has not been empirically tested (Factor 1)
[ ] Method has not been subjected to peer review (Factor 2)
[ ] Method has no established error rate (Factor 3)
[ ] Method has an established error rate that is not negligible (Factor 3)
[ ] No published standards exist for the method (Factor 4)
[ ] Published standards exist but were not followed (Factor 4)
[ ] Method is not generally accepted in the scientific community (Factor 5)
[ ] Method is accepted by practitioners but criticized by scientists (Factor 5)
[ ] NAS Report (2009) criticized this discipline
[ ] PCAST Report (2016) found limited or no foundational validity
[ ] Analytical gap exists between data and conclusion (Joiner)
[ ] Expert used prohibited language (DOJ Uniform Language)
[ ] Expert failed to follow laboratory SOPs

REPORT / TESTIMONY CHALLENGES:
[ ] Report lacks methodology section
[ ] Report lacks limitations discussion
[ ] Expert claims absolute certainty
[ ] Expert fails to consider alternative hypotheses
[ ] Expert opinion invades Art. 704(B) territory
[ ] Expert disclosure is incomplete (Art. 719/723)
[ ] Underlying data not made available for review

BIAS INDICATORS:
[ ] Expert testifies exclusively/predominantly for prosecution
[ ] Expert earns substantial income from forensic testimony
[ ] Expert uses advocacy language in report
[ ] Expert was aware of case context before analysis
[ ] Expert refuses to acknowledge known limitations

VIABILITY ASSESSMENT:
[ ] STRONG -- multiple factors favor exclusion; file motion
[ ] MODERATE -- some factors favor challenge; consider scope limitation
[ ] WEAK -- limited grounds; focus on cross-examination instead
[ ] NOT VIABLE -- expert is well-qualified and methodology is sound
```

## Common Daubert/Foret Motion Structure

Use this template to draft motions to exclude or limit expert testimony:

```
MOTION TO EXCLUDE EXPERT TESTIMONY
(or MOTION IN LIMINE TO RESTRICT EXPERT TESTIMONY)

[CAPTION]

I.    INTRODUCTION
      [Brief statement of who the expert is, what they will
       testify about, and why the testimony should be excluded]

II.   FACTUAL BACKGROUND
      [Expert's qualifications, the analysis performed, the
       opinions to be offered, and the basis for those opinions]

III.  LEGAL STANDARD
      [La. C.E. Art. 702; State v. Foret; Daubert; Kumho Tire;
       Joiner; gatekeeper role of the trial court]

IV.   ARGUMENT
      A. The Expert Is Not Qualified to Offer the Opinions Proffered
         [If credential challenge applies]
      B. The Methodology Is Not Reliable
         1. The method has not been tested / has been tested with
            concerning results [Factor 1]
         2. The method has not been subjected to meaningful peer
            review [Factor 2]
         3. The error rate is unknown or unacceptably high [Factor 3]
         4. No standards govern the method, or the expert did not
            follow existing standards [Factor 4]
         5. The method is not generally accepted in the relevant
            scientific community [Factor 5]
      C. An Analytical Gap Exists Between the Data and the
         Expert's Conclusions [Joiner]
      D. The Expert's Testimony Would Not Assist the Trier of
         Fact [Art. 702 relevance/fit]
      E. Even If Admissible, the Probative Value Is Substantially
         Outweighed by Prejudicial Effect [Art. 403]

V.    CONCLUSION AND PRAYER FOR RELIEF
      [Request: exclude testimony entirely, or in the alternative,
       limit testimony to specific areas within the expert's
       demonstrated competence; request Daubert/Foret hearing]

[CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE]
[SIGNATURE BLOCK]
```

## Expert Discovery Demands

When the State discloses an expert, consider demanding the following under La. C.Cr.P. Art. 718-723 and constitutional authority:

| Item | Authority | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Complete CV with all prior testimony | Art. 719, 723 | Credential audit and impeachment mining |
| All reports, notes, and bench notes | Art. 719 | Methodology audit and basis review |
| Underlying raw data | Art. 719; *Brady* | Independent analysis and verification |
| Laboratory SOPs for the analysis performed | Art. 719; *Brady* | Standards compliance audit |
| Laboratory accreditation records | Art. 719; *Brady* | Quality assurance verification |
| Proficiency test results | Art. 719; *Brady* | Competence verification |
| Contamination logs for the relevant period | Art. 719; *Brady* | Quality control audit |
| Validation studies for the methodology | Art. 719; Daubert | Reliability assessment |
| Software validation studies (if applicable) | Art. 719; Daubert | Software reliability |
| Expert's fee agreement and billing records | Art. 719; cross-exam | Bias assessment |
| Prior testimony transcripts (all available) | *Brady*; *Giglio* | Impeachment mining |
| Corrective action reports involving the expert | *Brady*; *Giglio* | Competence and credibility |
| Cases where expert's findings were overturned | *Brady*; *Giglio* | Credibility and reliability |
| Internal review or quality assurance findings | *Brady*; *Giglio* | Institutional issues |

## Critical Implementation Notes

### File Intake Hard Stop
Never analyze uploaded documents without first clearing the hard stop in Step 0. Always confirm: "No more uploads now" before proceeding.

### Distinguish Challenge Types
- **Credential challenge**: Expert is not qualified to testify at all
- **Methodology challenge**: Expert may be qualified but method is unreliable
- **Scope limitation**: Expert qualifies in some areas but not others
- These are legally and strategically distinct; address each independently

### Pre-Trial vs. Trial Distinction
Daubert/Foret challenges are pre-trial motions decided by the judge as gatekeeper. Credential and methodology attacks can also occur during trial cross-examination before the jury. A challenge that fails as a pre-trial exclusion motion can still succeed as a trial credibility attack. Always address both avenues.

### Cite Accurately
When citing the PCAST Report, NAS Report, or published error rate studies, represent their findings accurately. Do not overstate what these sources conclude. The most effective expert challenges are grounded in accurate science.

### Verify Citations
Flag any case law citations that may need currency verification: `[VERIFY CITATION -- confirm this case has not been overruled or modified]`.
