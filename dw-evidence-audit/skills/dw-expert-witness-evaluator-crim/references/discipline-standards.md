# Discipline-Specific Standards Bodies & Expert Qualifications

## Quick Reference — Discipline-Specific Standards Bodies

| Discipline | Standards Body | Key Standards / Guidelines |
|-----------|---------------|--------------------------|
| DNA / Forensic Biology | SWGDAM; FBI QAS; OSAC | SWGDAM Interpretation Guidelines (2017); FBI QAS for DNA Testing; OSAC DNA standards |
| Latent Prints | OSAC Friction Ridge (formerly SWGFAST); IAI | OSAC Friction Ridge standards; IAI Resolution 2010-18 |
| Firearms / Toolmarks | AFTE; OSAC | AFTE Theory of Identification; AFTE Range of Conclusions |
| Digital Forensics | SWGDE; NIST | SWGDE Best Practices; NIST SP 800-86 (Digital Forensics); NIST SP 800-101 (Mobile Forensics) |
| Toxicology | SOFT; ABFT; OSAC | SOFT/AAFS Forensic Toxicology Laboratory Guidelines; ABFT standards |
| Forensic Pathology | NAME; ABP | NAME Autopsy Standards; Accreditation standards |
| Mental Health | APA; AAPL | APA Ethical Principles; AAPL Practice Guidelines |
| Accident Reconstruction | ACTAR; SAE | ACTAR accreditation standards; SAE technical papers |
| Bloodstain Pattern Analysis | IABPA; OSAC (formerly SWGSTAIN) | IABPA Recommended Terminology; OSAC BPA standards |
| Cell Site / Geolocation | No universal body | Vendor-specific training; *Carpenter v. United States*, 585 U.S. 296 (2018) |

## Certification Standards by Discipline

### DNA / Forensic Biology
- **Board Certification**: No universal board certification; FBI QAS and SWGDAM guidelines serve as de facto standards
- **Training**: Formal coursework in biochemistry, molecular biology, or genetics preferred; hands-on training with probability calculations
- **Expected Education**: Degree in science (biochemistry, biology, genetics) preferred
- **Key Skill Gap**: Population genetics -- expert should understand allele frequency databases and match probability calculation

### Latent Fingerprints
- **Board Certification**: IAI Certified Latent Print Examiner (CLPE) (optional but expected)
- **Certification Requirements**: Minimum 200 hours of training, 100 documented cases, pass examination
- **ACE-V Competency**: Training in Analysis, Comparison, Evaluation, Verification framework
- **Proficiency Testing**: Should maintain current proficiency through regular testing
- **Red Flag**: No certification, or certification lapsed; lack of documented proficiency testing

### Firearms / Toolmarks
- **Board Certification**: No board certification exists; AFTE membership expected
- **AFTE Accreditation**: Optional but expected; demonstrates standard training
- **Training**: Typically on-the-job under experienced examiner
- **Red Flag**: Training primarily on-the-job without formal scientific background; no AFTE involvement
- **"Sufficient Agreement"**: No objective standard for determining this -- subjective judgment

### Digital Forensics
- **Tool-Specific Certification**: EnCase, Cellebrite, Magnet, etc. -- examiner should be certified on the tool used
- **Operating System Knowledge**: Should be certified or extensively trained on the device/OS relevant to the case
- **Write-Blocker Protocol**: Should be familiar with forensic imaging protocols and write-blocker use
- **Hash Verification**: Should demonstrate knowledge of MD5/SHA hash verification for data integrity
- **Red Flag**: Certified on tool but not on the specific device type/OS; no validation of extraction tool on known datasets

### Toxicology
- **Board Certification**: ABFT (American Board of Forensic Toxicology) certification expected
- **Degree**: Pharmacology, pharmacokinetics, toxicology, or clinical chemistry preferred
- **Laboratory Standards**: SOFT/AAFS guidelines and laboratory accreditation (ASCLD/LAB)
- **Methodology**: Understanding of immunoassay vs. GC-MS vs. LC-MS/MS and their respective error rates
- **Red Flag**: No board certification; degree in unrelated field; unfamiliar with back-calculation limitations

### Forensic Pathology
- **Board Certification**: ABP (American Board of Pathology) forensic pathology certification expected
- **Medical Degree**: MD or DO required
- **Autopsy Experience**: Substantial history of performing autopsies
- **Fellowship Training**: Forensic pathology fellowship (1-2 years) standard for those claiming expertise
- **Red Flag**: No board certification; limited autopsy experience; disciplinary history

### Mental Health (Competency / Sanity)
- **Board Certification**: Forensic psychology (American Board of Professional Psychology) or forensic psychiatry certification preferred
- **Degree**: Ph.D. in psychology, Psy.D., M.D., or D.O.
- **Forensic Training**: Postdoctoral training or coursework in forensic assessment
- **Standardized Instruments**: Familiarity with ECST-R, MacCAT-CA, R-CRAS, SIRS-2, M-FAST and other validated instruments
- **Malingering Assessment**: Should include malingering detection in evaluation protocol
- **Red Flag**: No forensic training; limited evaluation history; reliance on clinical impression without standardized instruments

### Accident Reconstruction
- **ACTAR Accreditation**: Accreditation Commission for Traffic Accident Reconstruction certification expected
- **Education**: Degree in physics, engineering, or related field preferred
- **Training**: Formal coursework in accident reconstruction principles
- **Software Validation**: Should be familiar with reconstruction software limitations and validation
- **Scene Inspection**: Should conduct personal scene and vehicle inspection when possible
- **Red Flag**: No ACTAR accreditation; reliance solely on photographs and reports; inability to justify parameter assumptions

### Bloodstain Pattern Analysis
- **Certification**: IAI Certified Bloodstain Pattern Analyst preferred; OSAC BPA standards
- **Training**: Formal coursework in physics, biology, and pattern interpretation
- **Peer Review**: Publication history in peer-reviewed journals preferred (rare in this discipline)
- **Red Flag**: No IAI certification; training limited to agency courses; no publication history; no consideration of alternative mechanisms

### Cell Site / Geolocation
- **RF Engineering Degree**: Electrical engineering, telecommunications, or RF engineering degree preferred
- **Tool-Specific Training**: Pen-Link, ZetX, Hawk Analytics, or SkyHook certification
- **Drive Testing**: Evidence of RF propagation testing or drive testing in relevant area
- **Vendor Training**: Completion of vendor-specific training program
- **Red Flag**: No RF background; tool training only; no RF testing experience; excessive certainty in location determination
## Discipline-Specific Daubert Guidance (carried over from SKILL.md Module B)

For detailed guidance on how each factor applies to specific forensic disciplines, consult `references/discipline-standards.md` and `references/scientific-reports.md`:
- DNA/Forensic Biology: validation of software, contributor assumptions, secondary transfer
- Latent Fingerprints: ACE-V subjectivity, Black Box study error rates, contextual bias
- Firearms: "sufficient agreement" is subjective, PCAST limited validity findings
- Digital Forensics: tool validation, hash verification, deleted data limitations
- Toxicology: immunoassay cross-reactivity, back-calculation assumptions, impairment determination
- Bloodstain Pattern Analysis: subjective interpretation, NAS findings, lack of error rate data
