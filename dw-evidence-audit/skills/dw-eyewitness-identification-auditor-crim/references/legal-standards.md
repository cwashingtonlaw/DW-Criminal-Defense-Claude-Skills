## Quick Reference -- Constitutional & Louisiana Legal Standards

| Situation | Authority | Holding / Rule |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Due process standard for identification | Manson v. Brathwaite, 432 U.S. 98 (1977) | Two-step test: (1) was procedure suggestive? (2) if so, was identification reliable under totality of circumstances? |
| Reliability factors | Neil v. Biggers, 409 U.S. 188 (1972) | Five factors: opportunity to view, degree of attention, accuracy of prior description, certainty, time elapsed |
| Showup necessity | Stovall v. Denno, 388 U.S. 293 (1967) | Showup permissible only when necessitated by circumstances |
| Right to counsel at post-indictment lineup | United States v. Wade, 388 U.S. 218 (1967) | Sixth Amendment right to counsel at post-indictment lineups |
| No right to counsel for photo arrays | United States v. Ash, 413 U.S. 300 (1973) | Sixth Amendment does not apply to photo arrays |
| Pre-indictment identification -- no 6th Amendment right | Kirby v. Illinois, 406 U.S. 682 (1972) | Right to counsel attaches at initiation of adversarial proceedings |
| Fruit of suggestive identification | Gilbert v. California, 388 U.S. 263 (1967) | In-court ID tainted if fruit of unconstitutional out-of-court procedure |
| Due process limited to police-arranged procedures | Perry v. New Hampshire, 565 U.S. 228 (2012) | Due process protections only apply when police arranged the suggestive identification |
| Right to counsel at preliminary hearing identification | Moore v. Illinois, 434 U.S. 220 (1977) | Wade applies to identification at preliminary hearing |
| Louisiana identification procedures | La. C.Cr.P. Art. 163 | Procedure must promote reliability and minimize misidentification risk |
| Louisiana lineup requirements | La. C.Cr.P. Art. 164 | Post-indictment lineup; right to counsel |
| Louisiana authentication | La. C.E. Art. 901 | Authentication and identification requirements |
| Louisiana expert testimony | La. C.E. Art. 702 | Daubert/Foret standard for expert admissibility |
| Louisiana identification procedure standards | State v. Guillory, 735 So.2d 684 (La. App. 1999) | Louisiana identification procedure evaluation |
| Suggestive identification review | State v. Higgins, 898 So.2d 1219 (La. 2005) | Suggestive identification analysis under La. law |
| Daubert standard in Louisiana | State v. Foret, 628 So.2d 1116 (La. 1993) | Louisiana adoption of Daubert framework |
| 5th Circuit identification standard | United States v. Bautista, 23 F.3d 726 (5th Cir. 1994) | 5th Circuit application of Manson/Brathwaite |
| 5th Circuit photo array challenge | United States v. Sanchez, 988 F.2d 1384 (5th Cir. 1993) | Photo array suggestiveness analysis |
| Henderson system/estimator variables | State v. Henderson, 27 A.3d 872 (N.J. 2011) | Comprehensive framework (persuasive, not binding in LA) |
