# Long-Form Initial Client Interview Template

**Use when:** First substantive meeting with the client. Time available (60+ minutes). Comprehensive intake.
**Target length:** 5–7 pages.
**Sections:** A–Q (base) plus injected charge modules between Section F and Section I.

This is the master template for new-case intake. When charge modules are injected, place them between **Section F (Co-defendants, Witnesses & Victims)** and **Section I (Alibi & Defense Leads)**.

## SECTION A — IDENTITY, BACKGROUND & PRIVILEGE

**Guidance:** Open warmly. Verify the basics first — corrects PSA/intake errors and builds rapport.

- Full legal name, all aliases / nicknames / street names used in last 10 years
- Confirm DOB. Place of birth. U.S. citizen? If naturalized, when?
- Last residence before arrest. Who lived there? On the lease?
- Phone numbers for client, spouse/partner, mother, closest sibling, employer
- Best mailing address for jail mail. Best phone for family contact
- Marital status. Children — names, ages, whereabouts. Child support? Custody / visitation?
- Anyone holding power of attorney, durable financial POA, or healthcare proxy?
- Explain attorney-client privilege — jail calls/email/visits NOT privileged unless to office
- Confirm client wants D&W to represent. Sign representation paperwork if scope allows

---

## SECTION B — THE ARREST

**Guidance:** Get the client's narrative before testing it. Let them talk. Then circle back with targeted questions.

### B.1 Pre-arrest movements

- Walk me through that day from when you woke up until officers approached you
- Where had you been in the 24 hours before? With whom?
- Were you driving, walking, on a bike, in a vehicle as passenger? Whose vehicle?
- Anyone with you in the moments before officers arrived?
- Had you used alcohol or drugs that day? Prescribed medications?
- Any reason police would have been looking for you specifically (warrant, BOLO, suspect description)?

### B.2 First police contact

- Exact location — street address or nearest cross streets
- Time of day (confirm hour)
- How many officers? Marked or unmarked? Uniforms or plainclothes?
- Traffic stop, foot stop, dispatched call, or arrest at residence?
- What did the first officer say? Did they show a warrant?
- Did they say why they were stopping you? Did they say someone identified you?
- Were you free to leave at any point? Did you ask?
- Were you handcuffed? At what point?
- Pat-down then full search? In what order?
- K-9 used? At what point did it arrive?
- Anyone else stopped, detained, or arrested with you?

### B.3 Use of force

- Were you struck, tackled, tased, pepper-sprayed, or pointed at with a firearm?
- Any injuries? EMS on scene? Hospital before booking?
- Visible injuries now? (Document for photographs)
- Badge numbers, names, unit designation, descriptions you remember?
- Body-worn cameras visible? Any officer turn one on/off in your presence?

---

## SECTION C — STATEMENTS, MIRANDA & INTERROGATION

**Warning:** Critical — statement suppression depends on detail here. Do not let the client describe the substance of the statement until Miranda timeline is locked in.

- Were you read your rights? When — scene, car, station?
- Who read them? From a card, memory, or paper waiver?
- Did anyone ask you questions BEFORE reading rights? What?
- Did you sign anything — Miranda waiver, consent to search, anything else?
- Did you tell them you wanted a lawyer? When, to whom? Did questioning continue?
- Did you say you didn't want to talk? Did they keep asking?
- Did you talk to a detective at central lockup, the station, or anywhere else?
- How long were you questioned? Multiple sessions? Breaks?
- Was the room recorded — video, audio? Any officers say it was off?
- Were you offered food, water, bathroom? Denied any of those?
- Were you sick, withdrawing, sleep-deprived, intoxicated, or injured during questioning?
- Any promises made? ("Help yourself," "we can talk to the DA," etc.)
- Any threats made? About you, family, additional charges?
- Did anyone tell you they had video, witnesses, prints, DNA, or co-defendant statements? Was that to get you to talk?
- What did you ultimately tell them, in your own words? (Get gist — do not rehearse.)

---

## SECTION D — SEARCH & SEIZURE

**Guidance:** Push for spatial precision — possession charges and search-related suppression issues hinge on it.

### D.1 Person

- What was on your person when first stopped? (Pockets, waistband, bag)
- Wearing a jacket / hoodie / bag? Searched immediately or later?
- Anything fall, get tossed, get dropped during the encounter? When and where?

### D.2 Vehicle (if applicable)

- Vehicle searched? Whose? Who was driving? Who else inside?
- Where exactly was each seized item found? Specific compartment.
- Items in a container — bag, box, glove box, console, trunk, under-seat?
- Anyone consent to the search? Who? Were you asked separately?
- Probable cause stated for stop / search?
- Vehicle towed? Inventory done? Receipt provided?

### D.3 Residence (if applicable)

- Home or anywhere you were staying searched?
- Warrant shown before, during, or after entry? Did you read it?
- Who was inside the home? Anyone consent?
- Knock and announce? How long before entry?
- What was seized? Provide inventory if shown.
- Anything seized that wasn't yours? Whose?

---

## SECTION E — THE ALLEGED OFFENSE

**Guidance:** Open-ended first. Do not lead. Once you have the client's narrative, work the elements.

### E.1 Open narrative

- Without me asking specific questions, tell me what you know about what they are accusing you of
- Were you at the scene where they say this happened? If yes, why? With whom?
- Do you know the alleged victim / complaining witness? How? Name, nickname, where from?
- Any prior dealings, debts, romantic history, neighborhood conflict with that person?

### E.2 Element-by-element probes

*[Charge modules will inject element-specific probes here based on detected statutes. If no module applies, ask:]*

- For each charge, what is the State alleging you did?
- What is your account of each event the charge corresponds to?
- Who else was present for each event?
- Any physical evidence you know about (photos, video, items recovered)?

## SECTION F — CO-DEFENDANTS, WITNESSES & VICTIMS

- Anyone arrested with you? Names, nicknames, what they said to police, family contacts
- Anyone present who was NOT arrested? Names and contact info if known
- Anyone who can confirm where you were before / during / after the alleged events?
- Identity / nickname / neighborhood of alleged victim. Common contacts?
- Has the alleged victim contacted you, your family, or social media since the arrest?
- Anyone you suspect is a confidential informant or cooperating witness?
- Anyone with motive to frame you — ex-partners, business disputes, neighborhood rivalry?
- Prior 911 calls / police reports involving you and the alleged victim? Either direction?

## SECTION I — ALIBI & DEFENSE LEADS

**Guidance:** Collect leads — do NOT discuss strategy.

- Where were you BEFORE the alleged offense? Who can confirm?
- Where DURING the offense window? Who saw you?
- Where AFTER? Who saw you?
- Phone — carrier, number, location services on?
- Surveillance cameras at places you were that day (store, ATM, gas station, doorbell)?
- Receipts, rideshare records, transit cards, social media posts from that window?
- Anyone you spoke to by phone, text, or video during the relevant window?
- Bank / ATM / debit activity that places you elsewhere?
- Willing to give us full access to your phone / social media accounts via consent?

---

## SECTION J — CRIMINAL HISTORY VERIFICATION

**Guidance:** Walk through each prior to confirm and probe for Boykin / habitual challenge grounds.
**Warning:** Do NOT discuss habitual offender exposure in detail — confirm priors only.

- Insert prior-record table from PSA / NCIC / Atlas / state rap sheet
- For each prior: do you remember pleading guilty? Did the judge ask about your rights? (Boykin probe)
- Did you have a lawyer for each? Public defender, private, who?
- Any prior you believe was wrongful or that you tried to appeal / withdraw?
- Any pending PCR, expungement, set-aside, pardon, or first-offender petition?
- Currently on probation or parole? Supervising officer?
- Pending charges in other parishes, states, or federal?
- Active warrants anywhere?
- Open child support, traffic, or municipal cases?

---

## SECTION K — BOND & PRETRIAL RELEASE

- Current bond amount. Surety used or commercial bondsman?
- Who would post or co-sign? Family, friend, employer? Contact info
- Equity in any property to pledge — home, vehicle, business?
- Stable address if released? Whose? Willing to provide affidavit?
- Employment offered? Employer name, written confirmation possible?
- School / training program enrollment possible?
- Treatment program willing to accept — substance abuse, mental health, anger management?
- GPS / ankle monitor acceptable? Who would pay?
- Travel ties — out-of-parish trips or relocations affecting FTA risk?
- Past FTAs — what happened? Health, transportation, didn't know about court date, in jail elsewhere?
- Surrenders — ever turn yourself in voluntarily?
- Statements client wants to make to magistrate?

---

## SECTION L — PERSONAL HISTORY & MITIGATION SEEDS

### L.1 Family & upbringing
- Raised where? Who raised you? Stable home?
- Parents / guardians — alive, where, contact, willing to support?
- Siblings — ages, relationships, anyone in law enforcement / military / clergy / professional?
- ACEs — abuse, neglect, foster care, witness to violence, parent incarceration?
- Major losses — death of parent, sibling, child?

### L.2 Education
- Highest grade / degree. Schools attended
- Special ed, IEP, repeated grades, expulsions?
- Reading and writing comfort level?
- Vocational training, certifications, apprenticeships?

### L.3 Employment
- Most recent employer — dates, role, supervisor contact
- Longest job ever held. Reason for leaving
- Skilled trades, union membership, professional licenses?
- Self-employment — business records, EIN, 1099 work?

### L.4 Medical & mental health
- Current medications. Need continued in jail?
- Mental health diagnoses — ever hospitalized, psychiatric meds, therapy?
- Suicidal ideation — current or history? (Trigger jail medical hold if current)
- Head injuries, TBI, seizures, stroke?
- Chronic conditions — diabetes, hypertension, cardiac, asthma, HIV, Hep C?
- Disability — SSI / SSDI / VA?

### L.5 Substance use
- Substances used past year — type, frequency, last use
- Treatment history — inpatient, outpatient, MAT, NA/AA?
- Withdrawal risk in custody — opioids, benzos, alcohol? (Flag for medical)
- Drug court eligibility historically considered or ruled out?

### L.6 Military & immigration
- Military — branch, dates, discharge type, combat exposure, VA enrollment?
- Citizen? If not — status, applications pending, prior deportation?
- ICE detainer or hold currently? Citizenship questions in custody?

### L.7 Community & character
- Church, mosque, temple, civic organizations — letter writers?
- Children / dependents who rely on you. Caregiving responsibilities
- Volunteer work, coaching, mentoring, mutual aid?
- Anyone willing to write a character letter or testify?

## SECTION M — COLLATERAL CONSEQUENCES

- Currently on probation / parole? Officer, parish, conditions
- Driver's license status — valid, suspended, revoked, never had?
- Sex offender registration currently required?
- Public housing / Section 8 — current occupant or applicant?
- Public benefits — SNAP, Medicaid, SSI/SSDI, unemployment?
- Civil suits, judgments, family court matters?
- Protective orders / TROs / stay-away orders involving anyone in this case?
- Bond / surety obligations on any other case?

---

## SECTION N — IMMEDIATE CUSTODY NEEDS

- Physically OK? Medical complaint not being addressed?
- Mental health crisis or PC (protective custody) needed?
- Threats from anyone inside? Known beef on the tier?
- Property concerns at home — vehicle towed, animals, kids' care, work?
- Bills, utilities, leases that will lapse — anyone with authority to act?
- Money on books? Family knows how to send?
- Phone / video visit access — family enrolled?
- Religious / dietary needs communicated to jail?

---

## SECTION O — COMMUNICATION & FAMILY CONTACT

- ONE family point of contact?
- Permission to discuss case generally with that person? Anyone OFF-limits?
- Confirm: do not discuss case on jail phones, visits, or with cellmates
- Confirm: do not write about case in letters except to this office
- Confirm: no social media posts about case from any device client controls
- Best way for client to reach office — family to call, write, jail email?
