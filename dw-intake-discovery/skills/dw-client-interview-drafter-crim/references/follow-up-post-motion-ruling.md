# Follow-Up Interview — Post-Motion-Ruling Debrief

**Use when:** A material motion has been ruled on — suppression, 404(b)/Prieur, Daubert/Foret, severance, bond, motion in limine, etc. Brief the client on the outcome and reassess posture.
**Target length:** 1–2 pages.

---

## HEADER

- Work product marking
- Title: "CLIENT FOLLOW-UP INTERVIEW — POST-RULING DEBRIEF"
- Mini case header
- Motion ruled on: __________
- Date of ruling: __________
- Ruling outcome (Granted / Denied / Partial / Taken under advisement): __________

---

## PRE-INTERVIEW ATTORNEY PREP

- Pull motion ruling (written or transcribed bench ruling)
- Identify what changed for the State's case
- Identify what changed for the defense's case
- Update `dw-appellate-error-monitor-crim` with any preserved error
- If ruling reshapes plea posture — be ready to re-discuss

---

## SECTION 1 — Explain the Ruling

**Plain language only.**

- What the judge decided, in simple terms
- What that means for what the jury will or will not hear
- What changes for the trial date / sentencing date
- What changes for any pending motion

---

## SECTION 2 — Impact on the Case (Honest Assessment)

- Did this help us, hurt us, or change little?
- What is now likely to come into evidence that we hoped would stay out (or vice versa)?
- Does this change the plea calculation? (Without disclosing strategy — ask how client now sees the case.)

---

## SECTION 3 — Appeal Considerations

- If ruling was adverse — does client understand we can challenge it on appeal if convicted?
- Confirm error is preserved on the record (attorney duty — not client question, but flagged here)

---

## SECTION 4 — Updated Posture

- Trial date confirmed / moved
- New motion to file? Reconsideration? Writ application?
- Discovery items now needed in light of ruling
- Witness changes (added / removed) in light of ruling

---

## SECTION 5 — Client's Reaction & Questions

- How are you feeling about where the case stands?
- Any concerns or questions about what happens next?

Five blank lines.

---
