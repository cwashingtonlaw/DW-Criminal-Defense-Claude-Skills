## SECTION 2 — The Right to Testify (Lock-Down Decision)

**Warning:** Boykin-equivalent. Document this discussion carefully.

- You understand you have an absolute right to testify and an absolute right NOT to testify
- You understand if you do not testify, no one can comment on it, and the jury will be instructed not to consider it
- You understand if you do testify, the State can cross-examine you, including about prior convictions in certain situations
- You understand the decision is YOURS alone — I will give you my recommendation but you decide

`Client's current intention: ☐ Will testify   ☐ Will not testify   ☐ Reserving decision until trial`

`Client confirms decision is informed and voluntary: ☐ Yes`

If testifying — schedule mock direct and mock cross with attorney before trial.

---

## SECTION 3 — Witness Universe Review

### 3.1 State witnesses (review Witness Threat Matrix top 5)

For each:
- What is this person likely to say about you?
- Is what they will say true / partly true / false?
- What do you know about them that we can use on cross? (Bias, motive, prior contact, criminal history, drugs, debts, romantic ties, etc.)
- Anyone we should locate before trial who can impeach them?
- Any prior inconsistent statements they made to you, family, or others?

### 3.2 Defense witnesses

For each potential defense witness:
- Will they show up willingly or do we need a subpoena?
- What is the substance of what they will say?
- Any reason their credibility could be attacked (prior record, bias, drug use)?
- Any reason they might not want to be on the stand (fear, family conflict)?
- Have you spoken to them since the case opened? About what? (Caution: witness tampering risk — make sure attorney is aware of any contact.)

---

## SECTION 4 — Exhibit Review

- Walk through each defense exhibit — confirm authenticity, foundation, no surprises
- Walk through key State exhibits — anything client should expect to see in front of the jury for the first time?
- Anything in client's possession that could become an exhibit (phone, clothing, document)?

---

## SECTION 5 — Demeanor Coaching

- How to sit / stand / move when entering and leaving the courtroom
- Where to look during witness testimony (not at the jury, not at the gallery, not at the witness with anger)
- How to communicate with attorney during trial (notepad, not whispers)
- Reaction control — no audible reactions, no head-shaking, no rolling eyes
- How to act when the jury is brought in / sent out
- If a recess is called — where do you go, what do you do
- If a verdict goes against you — composure expected

---

## SECTION 6 — Plea Posture One Last Time

- Has anything changed since last consultation about a plea?
- Final understanding: State has not offered / has offered X / offer has expired
- Confirm decision to proceed to trial (or to revisit plea before jury selection)

---

## SECTION 7 — Family / Community Plan

- Who will be in the courtroom each day?
- Who will collect personal effects if conviction
- Who has POA for family / household decisions during trial week
- Bond status during trial — at liberty or in custody — and what changes after verdict
- Plan for either outcome — acquittal release procedure / conviction next steps
