# Charge Module — Violent Felony (Non-Homicide)

**Triggers:** Robbery (14:64, 14:64.1, 14:64.2, 14:64.3, 14:65, 14:65.1), battery (14:33, 14:34, 14:34.1, 14:34.7, 14:34.9), assault (14:36–14:38.2), kidnapping (14:44–14:46), home invasion (14:62.8), and other violent crimes.
**Injection point:** After Section F.

---

## V.1 Relationship to Alleged Victim

- Do you know the alleged victim? How? For how long?
- Prior altercations, threats, debts, romantic / family ties?
- When did you last see them before the incident?
- Any contact since the incident — through family, social media, jail?

---

## V.2 The Encounter

- Where did the alleged offense happen? Specific address / location
- Time of day. Lighting. How busy was the area?
- Who started the encounter? What was said first?
- Was a weapon involved? Whose? At what point did it appear?
- Was anyone else present? Witnesses or participants?
- Anything taken from anyone? What item? Returned? Owed?
- Was force or threat used? By whom? Words said?

---

## V.3 Self-Defense / Defense of Others / Mutual Combat

- Did the alleged victim threaten you or anyone with you?
- Did they have a weapon? Show it? Reach for it?
- Were you in a place you had a right to be?
- Could you have safely retreated?
- Have they threatened you before? Any 911 calls or police reports?

---

## V.4 Injuries

- Were you injured? Where? Documented?
- Was the alleged victim injured? How seriously?
- Hospital visits — when, where, who paid?
- Medical records likely to exist?

---

## V.5 Intent & State of Mind

- What were you trying to do during the encounter?
- Were you provoked? By what?
- Intoxicated, on medication, in crisis?
- Did you intend the result that occurred?

---

## V.6 Co-Actors / Witnesses

- Anyone with you? Names, contact info
- Anyone witnessed the encounter who was not a participant?
- Anyone you spoke to immediately after?
- Anyone who has talked to police about it?

---

## V.7 Property / Restitution

- If property was taken — what was the value?
- Was it returned, recovered, or destroyed?
- Property damaged? Whose? Estimated value?
- Restitution exposure to discuss with client (high-level only — not strategy)

---

## V.8 Statements & Communications

- Have you said anything about this incident to anyone since it happened?
- Phone, text, social media, jail calls, letters?
- Anyone who might testify to admissions?
