# Charge Module — DWI / OWI

**Triggers:** La. R.S. 14:98 (DWI), 14:98.1 (commercial DWI), 14:98.6 (DWI vehicular negligent injury), 14:39.1 (vehicular homicide).
**Injection point:** After Section F.
**Coordinate with:** `dw-dwi-specialist-crim`.

---

## DWI.1 The Stop

- Where were you driving from? Where to?
- Time of day. Traffic conditions.
- What reason did officers give for the stop?
- Was it a checkpoint, traffic violation, BOLO, accident response?
- Did officers say what they observed before pulling you over?

---

## DWI.2 The Vehicle

- Whose vehicle? Who was driving?
- Anyone else in the vehicle? Where seated?
- Any alcohol containers, drugs, or paraphernalia visible? Whose?
- Vehicle condition — recent repairs, mechanical issues affecting driving?

---

## DWI.3 What You Drank / Used

- Any alcohol consumed in the 24 hours before? When, where, how much?
- Any prescribed medications? When taken, dose, side effects?
- Any other substances? When, how much?
- Last meal — when, what?

---

## DWI.4 Field Sobriety Tests (SFST)

For each test performed:

- Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus (HGN) — were instructions clear? Lighting / road conditions adequate?
- Walk and Turn — what surface, footwear, any physical issues (balance, injury, weight, age)?
- One Leg Stand — duration, footwear, physical limitations?
- Any other test offered? Did you refuse any?
- Officer's instructions — clear, in language you understood, repeated if asked?

---

## DWI.5 Physical Conditions Affecting SFST

- Any back, knee, ankle, foot injury?
- Any vision issues, contacts, glasses needed?
- Tired, ill, on medication that affects balance?
- More than 50 lbs overweight? (NHTSA validity threshold concern)
- Wearing high heels, boots, or anything affecting balance?

---

## DWI.6 Breath / Blood / Urine Testing

- Were you offered a breath test? At what point — scene, station, hospital?
- Implied consent advisement — what were you told? In writing?
- Did you submit? Refuse?
- If submitted — what was the reading? Did you see it?
- Were two samples taken? Within 15 minutes of each other?
- Operator name / certification — known?
- Maintenance log / certification of the instrument provided?

---

## DWI.7 Refusal Consequences

- If refused — were you told about license suspension?
- Were you given warning about implied consent in language you understood?
- Did anyone threaten or pressure you to submit?

---

## DWI.8 Blood Draw

- If blood was drawn — when, where, by whom?
- Hospital or jail?
- Were you conscious? Did you consent?
- Search warrant or implied consent basis?
- Anti-coagulant tube used? Chain of custody intact?

---

## DWI.9 Statements

- What did you say to officers about your drinking or driving?
- Were you read Miranda before answering questions?
- Any statements at the scene vs. station vs. hospital?

---

## DWI.10 Witnesses

- Passengers in your vehicle — what would they say about your driving?
- Bar / restaurant staff — receipts, server testimony, surveillance?
- Anyone you were with that evening?
- Civilians who witnessed the driving or the stop?

---

## DWI.11 Prior DWIs (Enhancement Ladder)

- Any prior DWI convictions? When, where, disposition?
- 10-year lookback for enhancement
- Predicate Boykin issues (uncounseled prior, valid plea colloquy)
- Diversion or DWI court history

---

## DWI.12 License Status

- Driver's license valid at time of stop?
- Hardship license needed? Eligibility?
- ALC (Administrative License Suspension) hearing — requested? Deadline?

(Coordinate hardship and ALC routes with `dw-dwi-specialist-crim`.)
