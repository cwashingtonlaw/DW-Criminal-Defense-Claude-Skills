# Charge Module — Homicide

**Triggers:** La. R.S. 14:30 (1st degree murder), 14:30.1 (2nd degree murder), 14:31 (manslaughter), 14:32 (negligent homicide), 14:32.1 (vehicular homicide).
**Injection point:** After Section F (Co-defendants/Witnesses) in the long-form sheet.

---

## H.1 Relationship to Decedent

- Did you know the decedent? How? For how long?
- Any prior altercations, threats, debts, romantic ties, family relationships?
- When did you last see the decedent alive? Where?
- Anyone else there at that last contact?

---

## H.2 Scene & Time of Death

- Were you present when the death occurred?
- If yes — walk me through everything you saw, heard, smelled, touched
- If no — where were you? Who can confirm? (Cross-reference Alibi section)
- Time of day. What was happening around you at the time the State alleges?

---

## H.3 Weapon

- Was a weapon used? What kind?
- Was the weapon yours? Whose was it? Where did it come from?
- Where is the weapon now? Recovered? Lost? Hidden?
- Have you ever fired / handled / cleaned that weapon? When?
- Were prints / DNA / ballistics potentially yours on the weapon?
- Other people with access to the weapon in the days before?

---

## H.4 Self-Defense / Defense of Others (if applicable)

- Did the decedent threaten you or someone you were with?
- Did the decedent have a weapon? Display it? Reach for it?
- Were you in a place you had a right to be?
- Were you retreating or could you have retreated safely?
- Any prior threats from decedent to you, family, friends?
- Witnesses to the threat or to the encounter?

---

## H.5 State of Mind (Manslaughter / Negligent Homicide considerations)

- What were you feeling in the moments before? Fear, anger, surprise?
- Were you provoked? By what or whom?
- How much time passed between any provocation and the death?
- Intoxicated? On medication? Sleep-deprived? Mental health crisis?

---

## H.6 Forensic Evidence Issues

- Were you injured during the incident? Where? Treated?
- Did you wash, change clothes, or shower before being arrested?
- What were you wearing? Where are those clothes now?
- Any blood, GSR, DNA evidence that could be tested against you?
- Did anyone clean up the scene before police arrived?

---

## H.7 Co-Defendants / Co-Actors

- Anyone else involved? Names, roles, contact info
- Anyone else present who was not involved?
- Anyone who has spoken to police or to others about what they saw?
- Anyone who fled, hid, or destroyed evidence?

---

## H.8 Statements About the Decedent

- Have you told anyone — before or after the death — anything about the decedent or this incident?
- Phone calls, texts, social media, jailhouse calls?
- Anyone who might testify you said something incriminating? What did they hear?

---

## H.9 Sentencing-Tier Awareness (Attorney Knowledge Only — Do Not Probe Client)

- 14:30 — death or LWOP (capital eligibility considerations)
- 14:30.1 — life imprisonment without parole
- 14:31 — 40-year max
- 14:32 — 5-year max
- 14:32.1 — 5–30 year range; vehicular variant requires BAC / impairment proof
