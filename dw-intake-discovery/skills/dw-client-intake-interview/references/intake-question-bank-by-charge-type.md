# Intake Question Bank — by Charge Type

Branching question sets for the closed phase of Module C (Client Narrative Capture) and for Module B charge-type dispatch. Each branch has charge-specific questions designed to surface defense-relevant facts that generic intake will miss.

**Use protocol:**
- After Module A (conflicts) clears, run the open phase of Module C
- Then identify the charge category and run the corresponding branch below
- Tag every answer `[CLIENT-REPORTED — UNVERIFIED]` unless documented otherwise
- Mark any answer the investigator should chase with `[VERIFY DURING INVESTIGATION]`
- For multi-charge cases, run every applicable branch

---

## Branch 1 — Drug Offenses

(Possession, distribution, manufacture, conspiracy under La. R.S. 40:961 et seq.)

### Possession context

1. Where were you when the alleged drugs were found? On your person, in a car, in a residence, in a hotel room, in a bag?
2. If in a car — whose car? Who else was in the car? Where in the car were the drugs found (driver door pocket, glovebox, console, trunk, passenger seat)?
3. If in a residence — whose residence? Who else lives there? Who else has access? Were you the only person present at the time of the search?
4. Were the alleged drugs visible (in plain view) or concealed (in a bag, container, hidden compartment)?
5. Did you know the drugs were there?
6. Whose drugs are they (if not yours)?

### Search predicate

7. Why did the police stop you, approach you, or come to the residence?
8. Were you given a reason for the stop or contact at the scene?
9. Was there a warrant? Did anyone show you a warrant? Did you ask to see it?
10. Did you consent to a search? Of what (your person, your car, your home, your phone)? Did you sign anything?
11. Was a drug-detection dog used? Did the dog alert before or after officers asked you about drugs?
12. Was the search conducted before or after you were arrested for something else?

### Quantity, packaging, distribution markers

13. How much was allegedly recovered? In what form (powder, pill, plant, edible)?
14. How was it packaged (single bag, multiple individual bags, bulk in one container)?
15. Was cash recovered? How much? In what denominations?
16. Were scales, baggies, ledgers, or other distribution paraphernalia recovered?
17. Were multiple cell phones recovered? How many phones do you regularly carry?
18. Did anyone make a controlled buy from you? When? Have you ever sold to anyone you suspect was an informant?

### Informant indicators

19. Has anyone close to you (friend, ex, relative, former co-defendant) been arrested recently and then "disappeared" from contact?
20. Has anyone been asking unusual questions about your habits or your contacts?
21. Have you been approached by police for a "talk" before the arrest? Asked to cooperate?

### Prior drug history

22. Any prior drug convictions or arrests? In what jurisdiction?
23. Are you currently in any drug treatment or court-supervised program (drug court, probation with drug conditions)?
24. Any current prescriptions for controlled substances? (Especially relevant if the alleged drugs include a substance you are prescribed.)
25. Any history of substance use disorder treatment? Currently in recovery?

## Branch 2 — DWI / DUI / OWI

(La. R.S. 14:98 series)

### Stop predicate

1. Why did the officer stop you? What did the officer say at the time?
2. Was it a checkpoint, an accident, a 911 call, or a moving-violation stop?
3. If a moving-violation stop — what violation? Were you actually committing it?
4. Were you driving, parked, asleep, or just sitting in the vehicle?
5. Where was the stop (parking lot, public road, private property, drive-thru)?

### Field sobriety conditions

6. What did the officer ask you to do (HGN, walk-and-turn, one-leg-stand, alphabet, finger-to-nose)?
7. What time of day was it? What were the lighting conditions?
8. What was the surface like (level, sloped, gravel, wet)?
9. Were you wearing footwear that affects balance (boots, heels, flip-flops)?
10. Do you have any physical conditions that affect balance, eye movement, or coordination (back injury, knee injury, inner-ear issues, prior concussion, medication side effects)?
11. Were you wearing corrective lenses? Were they removed for the HGN?
12. Did you tell the officer about any medical conditions or medications?

### Breath / blood / urine test

13. Did you take a breath test? At the scene (PBT) or at the station? What did the device say?
14. Did you take a blood test? Where was it drawn? When? Was a warrant obtained, or did you consent?
15. Did you take a urine test?
16. Did you refuse any test? At what point? Did the officer read you the implied-consent advisement?
17. How long between the stop and the test?
18. Were you given the opportunity for an independent test?

### Substance history that day

19. What did you drink, when, where, and over what time period?
20. What did you eat, when, and how much?
21. What medications did you take that day (prescription or OTC)? When?
22. Any cannabis, edibles, or other substances? When?

### Prior DWI history

23. Any prior DWI arrests or convictions? In what jurisdictions? When?
24. Did any prior DWI involve a refusal? An ignition interlock requirement?
25. Was your driver's license suspended at the time of this stop?

## Branch 3 — Violent Crime

(Homicide, attempted homicide, armed robbery, aggravated battery, kidnapping, La. R.S. 14:30 series, 14:34 series, 14:64 series, 14:44 series)

### Self-defense indicia

1. Who started the physical contact? Walk through the very first physical movement.
2. Who said what before any physical contact?
3. Did the alleged victim threaten you? With words, gestures, or a weapon?
4. Did you believe the alleged victim could carry out the threat? Why?
5. Was there a way to retreat? (Note: Louisiana stand-your-ground considerations; capture facts, not legal conclusions.)
6. Was anyone else (your family, your child) at risk during the encounter?

### Weapon possession

7. Were you armed? What with? Where on your person was it carried? Lawfully owned and lawfully carried?
8. Was the alleged victim armed? With what? Did you see the weapon? When?
9. Was a weapon recovered at the scene? By whom? From where?
10. Whose weapon discharged (if a firearm offense)? How many shots? In what sequence?

### Victim relationship

11. Did you know the alleged victim before this incident? How? For how long?
12. Any prior disputes, threats, or violence between you?
13. Any social media history with the alleged victim (DMs, posts, mutual followers)?
14. Did the alleged victim or their family make any threats before or after the incident?
15. Did you have any active protective orders, restraining orders, or no-contact orders involving this person?

### Witnesses and surroundings

16. Who else was present during the incident? Where were they standing? What did they see?
17. Were there security cameras at the location? Body cameras on responding officers? Cell phone video by anyone?
18. Did anyone film or photograph the scene?
19. Were there 911 calls? By whom? When? What was said?

### Injury severity

20. Did you sustain injuries? Where on your body? Treated where? When?
21. Did you receive any medical care, even minor (urgent care, ER, EMT at scene, your own doctor later)?
22. Did anyone photograph your injuries? Did you photograph the alleged victim's injuries?
23. What did the alleged victim's injuries look like immediately after the incident?

### Statements after the event

24. Did you call 911? Did anyone else?
25. What did you say to the first officer who arrived?

## Branch 4 — Sex Offense

(La. R.S. 14:42 series, 14:43 series, 14:80 series, 14:81 series)

**Privilege caution at intake:** This branch handles the most sensitive material. Run it only after the family is out of the room and the privilege is clean. Pause immediately if a family member or non-attorney is present.

### Relationship to complainant

1. Did you know the complainant before the alleged incident? How?
2. How long have you known each other?
3. Romantic, sexual, social, professional, or familial relationship?
4. When did the relationship (if any) begin? When (if applicable) did it end?
5. Was anyone aware of your relationship?

### Age and consent context

6. What is the complainant's age? How do you know it?
7. What is your age at the time of the alleged incident?
8. Did the complainant ever represent themselves as older or younger than they actually were?
9. Was alcohol or any substance involved in the encounter? Used by you, the complainant, or both?
10. Was the complainant conscious throughout?
11. What was said about consent — by whom, when, what words?

### Electronic communications

12. Did you and the complainant communicate by phone, text, social media, dating app, email? Which platforms?
13. Any voice messages, photos, or video exchanged? Either direction?
14. When did the most recent communication occur?
15. Have you preserved those communications, or have they been deleted? **Do not delete now.**
16. Did the complainant ever send you anything you considered intimate?

### Prior allegations and report timing

17. Has the complainant made similar allegations against anyone else, that you know of?
18. When was the alleged conduct first reported to anyone (friend, family, school, therapist, police)?
19. How much time elapsed between the alleged conduct and the report?
20. What was happening in the complainant's life at the time of the report (custody dispute, school problem, breakup, family conflict)?

### Forensic exam / SANE

21. Was a forensic medical examination conducted? When? Where?
22. Have you been told about any physical findings?
23. Were any items collected as evidence (clothing, sheets, swabs)?

### Surroundings and witnesses

24. Where did the alleged conduct occur? Who else was in the residence/location at the time?
25. Were there cameras (residential, doorbell, business)?

## Branch 5 — Firearm Offenses

(Felon-in-possession, illegal carrying, felony-with-firearm enhancements, La. R.S. 14:95 series, 14:95.1)

### Possession context

1. Where was the firearm when officers found it? On your person, in a vehicle, in a residence?
2. If in a vehicle — whose vehicle? Who else was in the vehicle? Where in the vehicle (driver pocket, console, glovebox, trunk, under seat)?
3. If in a residence — whose residence? Who else lives there? Who else has access?
4. Was the firearm visible or concealed?
5. Did you know the firearm was there?

### Ownership and acquisition

6. Is the firearm yours? Whose is it (if not yours)?
7. How was it acquired? Purchased (where, when, from whom), inherited, gifted, found, traded?
8. Is it registered to you? In any state?
9. Have you ever lawfully owned firearms?

### Prior convictions affecting eligibility

10. Any prior felony convictions? Where? When? Adjudicated as a juvenile or adult?
11. Any prior misdemeanor domestic violence convictions? (Federal firearms disqualifier — 18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(9).)
12. Any active protective orders or restraining orders against you?
13. Any honorable/dishonorable discharge from military? Any bad-conduct or dishonorable discharge? (18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(6) considerations.)
14. Any history of involuntary mental-health commitment? Adjudicated mentally defective?
15. Any history of drug use that affects firearm eligibility? (18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(3).)
16. Any non-citizen status? (18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(5).)

### Search predicate

17. Why did officers approach you, stop you, or enter the location?
18. Was there a warrant? Did anyone show you a warrant?
19. Did you consent to a search? Of what?
20. Was the firearm recovered before or after you were detained?

### Firearm condition

21. Was the firearm loaded? Round in chamber?
22. Was it functional, broken, or otherwise inoperable?
23. Any modifications (silencer, suppressor, automatic conversion)?
24. Magazine capacity?
25. Any ammunition recovered separately?

## Branch 6 — White-Collar / Fraud / Theft

(Theft, fraud, embezzlement, identity theft, forgery, computer fraud, La. R.S. 14:67 series, 14:70 series, 14:71 series, 14:73 series)

### Entity vs. individual liability

1. Are you charged as an individual, as a corporate officer, or both?
2. What entity is involved? Your role, title, time of service?
3. Who else had decision-making authority during the relevant period?
4. Is the entity itself under investigation? By whom?

### Document preservation

5. Where are the relevant business records (paper, server, cloud, third-party accountant, attorney)?
6. Have any documents been destroyed, shredded, or deleted recently? **Do not destroy or alter anything now.**
7. Who has access to the email accounts, cloud drives, accounting software, banking platforms?
8. Have any document-retention policies been suspended or modified recently?

### Financial transaction map

9. What transactions are at issue? Amounts, dates, accounts, counterparties.
10. What accounts (personal, business, foreign) might be relevant?
11. Any cryptocurrency, peer-to-peer payment, or off-the-books transactions?
12. Any cash deposits, structured deposits, or large withdrawals near relevant dates?

### Parallel proceedings

13. Any civil litigation involving the same facts (creditor suit, regulatory action, divorce)?
14. Any regulatory investigation (SEC, IRS, banking regulator, professional licensing board)?
15. Any internal investigation by an employer or insurer?
16. Any cooperation, proffer, or grand jury appearance offered or completed?

### Statements to investigators or auditors

17. Have you spoken to any investigator, auditor, regulator, or law enforcement officer? When? With or without counsel?
18. Have you signed any document admitting wrongdoing (severance, settlement, regulatory consent)?
19. Have you given any sworn statement in any proceeding?

### Prior matters

20. Any prior fraud, theft, or financial-crime allegations? Civil or criminal?
21. Any pending civil judgments, bankruptcies, IRS issues?

## Branch 7 — Domestic Violence / Intimate Partner Violence

(La. R.S. 14:35.3 domestic abuse battery; protective-order violations under La. R.S. 14:79; aggravated assault on family member)

### Relationship

1. What is your relationship to the alleged victim (spouse, ex-spouse, dating, ex-dating, co-parent, cohabitant, household member)?
2. How long? Children together?
3. Current living arrangement at the time of the incident?
4. Any prior separation, divorce, custody proceedings, or restraining orders?

### Incident specifics

5. Walk through the encounter chronologically. What was the trigger? Who said what first?
6. Was alcohol or any substance involved? Used by whom?
7. Who initiated physical contact?
8. Who called 911? Why? At what point in the encounter?
9. Were children present? Where were they during the incident? What did they see?

### Mutual-arrest considerations

10. Did you sustain injuries? Where? Treated when?
11. Did the alleged victim sustain injuries? What kind? Visible to officers?
12. Were photographs taken of either party's injuries?
13. Did you tell the responding officers your account? What did you say?

### Protective orders

14. Is there a current protective order against you? Issuing court, date, terms?
15. Has the alleged victim sought a protective order? When? Granted or denied?
16. Have you ever been the petitioner on a protective order against this person? Or anyone else?

### Prior incidents

17. Prior domestic incidents with this person — anyone called police, even if no arrest?
18. Prior domestic charges with anyone?
19. Anyone else witnessed the dynamic of this relationship (family, neighbors, counselor, pastor)?

### Communications

20. Texts, calls, voicemails between you and the alleged victim around the time of the incident? Preserve them.
21. Any social media exchanges related to the relationship or incident?
22. Has the alleged victim contacted you (in violation of any order or otherwise) since the incident? Has anyone?

## Branch 8 — Juvenile (Client Under 17 at Time of Offense)

(Louisiana Children's Code; transfer-to-adult-court considerations)

**Privilege caution:** Juvenile intake usually involves a parent or guardian. Document the parent's role precisely. The privilege belongs to the juvenile client; the parent does not get to overrule the client on representation decisions, even if paying. Run the privileged narrative phase with the juvenile alone if developmentally appropriate.

### Identification

1. Juvenile's full name, DOB, age at the alleged offense.
2. Parent/guardian present at intake — name, relationship, role.
3. Is the juvenile in custody? At what facility?
4. Has the juvenile been Mirandized? Made any statements?

### Education and developmental context

5. School attended, grade, attendance record.
6. Any IEP, 504 plan, special education services?
7. Any prior diagnoses (ADHD, autism spectrum, learning disability, mental health)?
8. Any medications?

### Family and home

9. Living arrangement (intact household, single parent, foster, group home, kinship care)?
10. DCFS / OCS involvement? Past or current?
11. Other adults in the household — relationships?

### Prior juvenile justice history

12. Prior arrests, adjudications, dispositions?
13. Currently on probation, supervised release, or in any program?
14. Any pending matters in juvenile court?

### Charges and transfer exposure

15. What are the charges? Are any subject to mandatory or discretionary transfer to adult court?
16. Has anyone (police, DA) raised the possibility of transfer?
17. Is the juvenile co-charged with adults?

### Co-respondents

18. Other juveniles or adults charged with the same conduct?
19. Has the juvenile spoken to any of them since the incident?
20. Are any of the co-respondents represented? By whom?

### Custodial statement context

21. Was a parent present during any police questioning?
22. Was the juvenile advised of rights in age-appropriate language?
23. Where did questioning occur (school, home, station)?
24. Was the questioning recorded?
25. Did the juvenile sign a waiver?

## Cross-Branch — Always-Ask Questions

Regardless of branch, ask every client:

1. Have you spoken to any law enforcement officer about this case? When? Where? With counsel? Recorded? Mirandized? What did you say (in substance)?
2. Have you been to court yet on this matter? When? Result?
3. Are you on probation, parole, supervised release, or any other supervision (any state, any matter)?
4. Are there any pending charges in any other jurisdiction?
5. Are you currently in compliance with any sex-offender registration, gun-disability, or similar requirement?
6. Do you have any current civil or family-court proceedings (divorce, custody, restraining order, protective order)?
7. Are you a U.S. citizen? If not — country of citizenship, immigration status, any prior immigration proceedings? **(Padilla flag)**
8. Do you hold any professional license or government clearance? **(collateral consequences flag)**
9. Are you a veteran? Branch, dates, discharge type? **(Veterans Treatment Court, VA benefits)**
10. Is there anything you are worried about that I have not asked? Anything you think the police got wrong? Anything you want me to know that the prosecutor would not want me to know?
