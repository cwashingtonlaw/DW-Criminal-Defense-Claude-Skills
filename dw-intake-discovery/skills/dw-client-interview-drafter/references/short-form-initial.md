# Short-Form Initial Client Triage Sheet

**Use when:** First jail visit within hours / days of arrest. 30-minute window. Goal is triage and stabilization, not complete intake.
**Target length:** 1 page.
**Follow-up:** Schedule the long-form interview within 2 weeks.

---

## HEADER

- Work product marking
- Title: "INITIAL CLIENT TRIAGE — JAIL VISIT SHEET"
- Mini case header (Client, DOB, Arrest date, Charges abbreviated, Court/Section, Jail facility)

---

## 1. PRIVILEGE & REPRESENTATION (≤5 min)

- Confirm full name and DOB
- Explain attorney-client privilege; jail phones/email/visits NOT privileged
- Confirm client wants D&W to represent. Get signature if scope allows
- Confirm: NO talking to anyone about the case (cellmates, family on jail calls, social media)

---

## 2. MIRANDA & STATEMENTS (≤8 min)

**Warning:** Lock down whether client has given a statement BEFORE discussing facts.

- Have you talked to police, detectives, or anyone in custody about the case?
- When? Where? Was it recorded?
- Were you read your rights before? Did you sign anything?
- Did you ask for a lawyer at any point?
- If you talked — what is the gist of what you said? (Do not rehearse details)

**If client has open statement risk:** STOP factual probing. Tell client not to discuss the case further with anyone. Move to next section.

---

## 3. CUSTODY WELFARE (≤5 min)

- Any injuries from arrest? Medical attention received?
- Currently sick, withdrawing, or on medications you need?
- Mental health crisis or suicidal ideation? (Trigger medical hold if yes)
- Threats from anyone inside? PC needed?

---

## 4. BOND POSTURE (≤5 min)

- Bond set? Amount?
- Anyone who can post or co-sign?
- Stable address if released? Whose?
- Employment / school / treatment program available?
- Any prior FTAs to explain to the magistrate?

---

## 5. IMMEDIATE FAMILY / WORLD ISSUES (≤4 min)

- ONE family contact for our office
- Anything urgent at home — kids, pets, vehicle towed, work, lease, bills?
- Anyone with POA / authority to act?

---

## 6. CLIENT'S TOP QUESTIONS (≤3 min)

Three blank lines. Capture verbatim. Answer what is appropriate; defer the rest to next visit.

---

## CLOSING (≤2 min)

- Confirm: long-form interview scheduled within 2 weeks
- Confirm: client has the office phone number memorized or written on legal mail
- Confirm: nothing about the case in jail letters except to the office
