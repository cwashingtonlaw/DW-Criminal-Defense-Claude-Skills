# Charge Module — Drug Offense (CDS)

**Triggers:** La. R.S. 40:966–40:970 (CDS possession, distribution, manufacturing, PWID).
**Injection point:** After Section F.

---

## D.1 The Substance

- What substance is the State alleging? (Marijuana, cocaine, fentanyl, heroin, meth, MDMA, pills, other)
- Schedule (I, II, III, IV, V)
- Form — powder, rock, pill, plant, liquid, vape cartridge?
- Amount / weight as charged

---

## D.2 Location of Recovery

- Exactly where was each substance found?
- On person — pocket, waistband, mouth, sock, bag?
- Vehicle — which compartment, console, glove box, trunk, hidden compartment?
- Residence — which room, container, who else lived there?
- Plain view or concealed?

---

## D.3 Ownership & Possession Theory

- Whose drugs are they?
- When did the container/package last move? Who put it there?
- Anyone else with access to that location in the past week?
- Have you ever touched, opened, used, or moved that specific stash?
- Anyone in your presence claim or deny the drugs?

---

## D.4 Knowledge

- Did you know the drugs were there?
- If yes — for how long? How did they get there?
- If no — best guess?

---

## D.5 Personal Use vs. Distribution

- Do you use this substance? How often?
- Last use — when, where, how much?
- Is the quantity consistent with personal use or more?
- Packaging — single dose vs. multiple individual bags, vacuum-sealed, bulk?
- Scale, ledger, bags, large currency, multiple phones nearby?

---

## D.6 Field Test & Lab Analysis

- Officers do a field test on scene?
- Did you see the result? Color change?
- Was the substance ever weighed in your presence?
- Lab report received yet? What did it confirm?
- Lab analyst name — known issues with that analyst or facility?

---

## D.7 Source / Buyer Questions (Caution)

**Warning:** Do NOT probe source / buyer identities unless attorney has specifically authorized. Doing so creates discoverable info that may need to be turned over if cooperation is later considered.

- Without naming anyone — how did the substance come into your possession (if it did)?
- Generic only: purchase, gift, found, planted, never had it?

---

## D.8 Constructive Possession / Joint Possession

- Anyone else in proximity to the drugs when found?
- Do you assert any of them had dominion / control?
- Anyone admit or deny possession in your presence?
- Vehicle / residence ownership — whose registration, lease, utilities?

---

## D.9 Drug Court / 893 Diversion Eligibility

- Prior CDS convictions — how many, when, what schedule?
- Any prior drug court / diversion participation?
- Current treatment engagement?
- Willingness to enter treatment if eligible?
