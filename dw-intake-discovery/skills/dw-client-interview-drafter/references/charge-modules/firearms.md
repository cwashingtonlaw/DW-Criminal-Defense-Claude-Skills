# Charge Module — Firearms / Weapons

**Triggers:** La. R.S. 14:95, 14:95.1, 14:95(E), 14:94 (illegal use), 14:37.4 (aggravated assault with firearm), 14:64.3 (armed robbery with firearm enhancement); federal 18 U.S.C. § 922(g), § 924(c).
**Injection point:** After Section F.

---

## F.1 The Firearm Recovered

- Was a firearm recovered? Make, model, color, caliber?
- Loaded? Round in chamber?
- Serial number visible, intact, obliterated?
- Magazine — extended, hi-cap, drum?
- Modifications — switch, suppressor, threaded barrel?

---

## F.2 Location of Recovery

- Exactly where was the gun found — on person, vehicle, residence, ground, container?
- If on person — which pocket, waistband, holster, bag?
- If vehicle — which seat, compartment, container? Whose vehicle? Who else was in it?
- If residence — which room, container, who else lived there?
- Was it in plain view or concealed?

---

## F.3 Ownership & Possession

- Whose gun is it?
- When was it last in that location? Who put it there?
- Who else had access to that location in the past week?
- Have you ever touched, held, moved, or fired that specific gun?
- If yes — when, where, why?

---

## F.4 Knowledge

- Did you know the gun was there?
- If yes — for how long? Who told you?
- If no — what is your best guess as to how it got there?
- Anyone in your presence claim or deny knowledge of the gun?

---

## F.5 Predicate Felony (14:95.1 / 922(g))

- Do you know what prior felony the State will use to charge you with felon-in-possession?
- Cleansing period — was the prior more than 10 years ago with no recent supervision?
- Have your civil rights been restored? Pardon? First-offender pardon?
- Federal vs. state predicate — does it qualify under federal 922(g)?

---

## F.6 Concealed Carry / Permit History

- Do you have, or have you ever had, a concealed carry permit (LA or any other state)?
- Have you applied? Been denied? Why?
- Are you aware of LA constitutional carry post-2024 changes? (Attorney clarifies — not asked.)

---

## F.7 Forensic Evidence on the Firearm

- Were prints / DNA swabs taken from the firearm in your presence?
- Did anyone else handle the gun after it was recovered?
- Was the gun bagged, tagged, photographed in your presence?
- Anyone touch the gun without gloves?

---

## F.8 Use of the Firearm

- Was the firearm fired during the incident?
- If yes — by whom? Where did it discharge to?
- Was anyone struck by the firearm or by a round?
- Pointed at anyone? Brandished?

---

## F.9 Drugs + Gun Combination (14:95(E))

- Were drugs recovered with the gun? Same container? Same compartment? On person?
- Whose drugs?
