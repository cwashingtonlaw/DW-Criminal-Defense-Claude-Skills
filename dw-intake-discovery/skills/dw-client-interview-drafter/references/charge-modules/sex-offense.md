# Charge Module — Sex Offense

**Triggers:** La. R.S. 14:41–14:43 (rape), 14:43.1–14:43.6 (sexual battery), 14:80–14:81.4 (carnal knowledge, indecent behavior, pornography of juveniles, computer-aided solicitation), 14:89 (crime against nature), 14:92 (contributing).
**Injection point:** After Section F.
**Special handling:** Sensitivity, rape shield (La. C.E. Art. 412), SORNA implications. Coordinate closely with `dw-sex-offense-specialist`.

---

## S.1 Relationship to Complainant

- Do you know the complaining witness? How? For how long?
- Family member, household member, romantic partner, acquaintance, stranger?
- Prior contact — when, where, how often?
- Any romantic or sexual history before the alleged offense? (Probe carefully — rape shield implications)
- Any conflict, debts, custody issues, other motive to fabricate?

---

## S.2 The Alleged Incident

- Date / approximate date alleged
- Location alleged
- Who else was present or nearby?
- What is the complainant alleging happened?
- What is your account of any contact that occurred (or that no contact occurred)?

---

## S.3 Consent Issues (Adult Complainant)

- Was the complainant of age?
- Was there any verbal or non-verbal indication of consent?
- Was alcohol or substance involved? For either of you?
- Any communications before, during, or after the alleged contact?
- Anyone aware of the relationship — texts, social media, witnesses?

---

## S.4 Age / Incapacity Issues

- If complainant alleged to be under age — what age did you understand them to be?
- How did you meet?
- Any communications about age, school, parents?
- Were others present who would know complainant's age?

---

## S.5 Forensic Evidence

- Was a SANE (Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner) exam conducted?
- DNA recovery from complainant, scene, or you?
- Did you provide DNA sample? Voluntary or court-ordered?
- Clothing seized — yours, complainant's, scene?
- Photographs of injuries, marks, scene?

---

## S.6 Digital Evidence

- Text messages, social media DMs, dating-app messages with complainant?
- Phone calls or video calls?
- Photographs or video — taken by either party, before, during, after?
- Anything sent or received that could be subject to child-pornography exposure? (Probe carefully — invokes additional federal exposure)

---

## S.7 Witnesses

- Anyone present at the alleged encounter?
- Anyone the complainant told before or after — and what was said?
- Anyone you told before or after — and what was said?
- Anyone with motive to influence complainant's statement?

---

## S.8 Prior Allegations

- Has the complainant made similar allegations against anyone else? (Probe carefully — Art. 412 implications)
- Has anyone else made similar allegations against you?
- Any prior law enforcement contacts involving the same complainant?

---

## S.9 Outcry & Reporting Timeline

- When did the alleged offense come to police attention?
- How much time between alleged offense and report?
- Who reported — complainant, family, mandatory reporter, third party?
- Anything happen in the intervening time that affects credibility?

---

## S.10 SORNA / Tier Awareness (Attorney Prep)

- If convicted, registration tier and duration
- Geographic restrictions, employment restrictions, school exclusion zones
- Reporting requirements

(Attorney prep — not asked of client unless plea is on the table.)

---

## S.11 Coordinate with Specialist Skills

- `dw-sex-offense-specialist` — framework

- `dw-eyewitness-identification-auditor` — if cross-racial or stranger ID
- `dw-confession-interrogation-auditor` — if statements taken from client
