# Charge Module — Property / Financial Crimes

**Triggers:** Theft (14:67), shoplifting (14:67.10), burglary (14:60–14:62.4), criminal damage (14:56–14:57), forgery (14:72), bank fraud (14:71), identity theft (14:67.16), check fraud (14:71.1), embezzlement (14:67.10), Medicaid/insurance fraud (14:70).
**Injection point:** After Section F.

---

## P.1 The Property / Funds

- What property or funds is the State alleging was taken / damaged / misappropriated?
- Estimated value
- Who owned it? Custodian?
- How did the loss come to light?

---

## P.2 Your Relationship to the Property

- Did you have any authorization to access, possess, or transact with the property?
- Employer / fiduciary / family / friend relationship with the owner?
- Any written contracts, agreements, or understandings affecting authority?

---

## P.3 The Alleged Act

- What is the State alleging you did?
- Where did it happen? When?
- Witnesses present?
- Surveillance — store cameras, ATM, doorbell, traffic, body cam?

---

## P.4 Intent

- Did you intend to deprive the owner permanently?
- Did you intend to return the property / repay the funds?
- Did you believe you had a right to the property / funds?
- Any debt owed to you by the alleged victim?

---

## P.5 Records & Paper Trail

- Bank records, wire transfers, receipts, invoices, ledgers
- Communications — emails, texts, calls with alleged victim
- Co-actors — anyone who instructed, authorized, or participated?
- Records held by third parties (banks, employers, vendors)?

---

## P.6 Identity Theft / Account Fraud Specifics

- Whose identity / account information is involved?
- How did you come into possession of that information?
- Were you given it willingly? Did you find it? Buy it?
- Any victims you can name or contact?

---

## P.7 Burglary / Entry Specifics

- What structure was entered?
- Authorized entry — owner, family, employer authority?
- Time of day. How was entry made?
- Anything taken, attempted, or damaged?
- Anyone with you?

---

## P.8 Damages

- Estimated dollar value of damage
- Receipts, repair estimates, insurance claims existing?
- Restitution exposure considerations (attorney prep only)

---

## P.9 Statements

- Have you spoken to anyone about the loss / the act?
- To the alleged victim, family, co-workers, social media?
- Anyone you've made admissions to that might testify?
