# Certificate of Service

Required on every filed pleading. Must be signed and dated. Format varies by court and service method.

## State court — standard COS

```
                       CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

       I HEREBY CERTIFY that a copy of the foregoing has been served upon
all counsel of record this _______ day of ____________, 20___, by
{{SERVICE_METHOD}}.


                              ___________________________________
                              CHRISTOPHER J. WASHINGTON
```

## Service method language by method

Replace `{{SERVICE_METHOD}}` with the appropriate phrase:

- **Email:** `electronic mail to {{ADA_EMAIL}}`
- **Email + hand:** `electronic mail to {{ADA_EMAIL}} and hand delivery to the {{PARISH}} Parish District Attorney's Office`
- **Hand delivery only:** `hand delivery to the {{PARISH}} Parish District Attorney's Office`
- **U.S. mail:** `placing same in the United States mail, postage prepaid and properly addressed, to {{ADA_NAME}}, {{ADA_ADDRESS}}`
- **Multiple methods to multiple parties:** itemize each recipient and method on a separate line

## Federal court — CM/ECF certificate

```
                       CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

       I HEREBY CERTIFY that on this _______ day of ____________, 20___, a
copy of the foregoing was filed electronically with the Clerk of Court using
the CM/ECF system. Notice of this filing will be sent to all counsel of
record by operation of the court's electronic filing system.


                              /s/ Christopher J. Washington
                              CHRISTOPHER J. WASHINGTON
```

## Auto-filled date

When the consuming skill writes the COS, it should auto-fill the current date in the format used by the court. Leave blank lines for handwritten dates only when explicitly drafting for ink-signature.

## When NOT to attach a COS

- Proposed orders (the order is filed with the motion; the motion's COS covers it)
- Internal memoranda not filed
- Drafts being circulated for attorney review only

## When to attach a COS

- Every filed motion, opposition, and memorandum in support
- Every notice of hearing
- Every supplemental filing
- Every notice of intent (e.g., notice of intent to introduce expert testimony)
- Every discovery request and response

## Multi-defendant cases

When co-defendants are represented by separate counsel, list each by name in the COS and confirm service to each. Do not assume "all counsel of record" suffices when a service issue could matter on a contested motion.
