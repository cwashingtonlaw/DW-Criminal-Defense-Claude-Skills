# Notice of Hearing

Many Louisiana state criminal motions require a separate notice of hearing — they don't auto-set on filing. Federal court typically does not require a separate notice (set by court scheduling order).

## State court — standard notice of hearing

```
                       NOTICE OF HEARING

TO:    {{ADA_NAME}}
       Assistant District Attorney
       {{PARISH}} Parish District Attorney's Office
       {{ADA_ADDRESS}}

       PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the foregoing {{MOTION_TITLE}} will be brought
on for hearing before the Honorable {{JUDGE_NAME}}, {{COURT_NAME}}, {{DIVISION_LABEL}}
"{{DIVISION}}", on the _______ day of ____________, 20___, at _______ o'clock
___.M., or as soon thereafter as counsel may be heard.


                              ___________________________________
                              CHRISTOPHER J. WASHINGTON
                              ATTORNEY FOR DEFENDANT
```

## Auto-set vs. requires-notice (Louisiana state)

**Typically auto-set on filing** (no separate notice usually required, but verify local rule):
- Initial appearance
- Arraignment
- Bond hearings (sometimes)

**Typically require a separate motion to set or notice of hearing:**
- Motion to suppress
- Motion in limine
- 404(b) opposition
- Motion to compel discovery
- Motion to quash
- Motion for continuance
- Motion to reduce bond (often requires notice + setting)
- Motion for severance
- Motion for change of venue
- Motion to recuse

When in doubt, file a notice of hearing — it costs nothing and avoids a procedural objection.

## Federal court

Federal motions are typically set per the court's scheduling order or the magistrate's case management plan. A separate notice of hearing is generally NOT required unless the motion specifically requests oral argument and the local rule requires a separate request. Confirm against current WDLA local rules before omitting.

## 14th JDC — local convention

In 14th JDC, the practice is generally to file a contemporaneous Notice of Hearing with the motion. The clerk routes it to the assigned division for setting. The setting comes back as a court date assigned by the division minute clerk.

## Variables

- `{{ADA_NAME}}` — assigned ADA name
- `{{ADA_ADDRESS}}` — DA office address
- `{{MOTION_TITLE}}` — the exact motion title from the caption block
- `{{JUDGE_NAME}}`, `{{COURT_NAME}}`, `{{DIVISION_LABEL}}`, `{{DIVISION}}` — from caption
- `{{HEARING_DATE}}`, `{{HEARING_TIME}}` — leave blank for clerk to set, OR fill in if attorney has coordinated a setting
