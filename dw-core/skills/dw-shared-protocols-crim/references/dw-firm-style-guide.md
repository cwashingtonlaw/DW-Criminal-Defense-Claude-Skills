## 1. Caption Format

### Louisiana State Court Caption (Primary)

```
STATE OF LOUISIANA
PARISH OF [PARISH NAME]
STATE OF LOUISIANA
v.
[DEFENDANT NAME]

DOCKET NO. [XXXX-XXXXX]
DIVISION [#] / SECTION [#]
JUDGE: [JUDGE NAME]

FILED: [DATE]
```

### Federal Court Caption (Western District of Louisiana)

```
UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
WESTERN DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA
[DIVISION] DIVISION

UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
v.
[DEFENDANT NAME]

CASE NO. [6:XX-cv-XXXXX] / [6:XX-cr-XXXXX]
JUDGE: [JUDGE NAME]

FILED: [DATE]
```

### Caption Guidelines
- Captions must appear on all documents submitted to court
- Use official court names precisely (check local court rules for current divisions)
- Docket/Case numbers must match original complaint or indictment
- Insert actual judge name if assigned; otherwise leave blank
- "FILED:" date is entered by clerk upon filing (may be left blank in drafts)

## 2. Typography and Formatting

### Font and Size
- **Body text:** Times New Roman, 12pt
- **Headings:** Times New Roman, 14pt, bold
- **Citations:** Times New Roman, 12pt (same as body)
- **Footnotes:** Times New Roman, 10pt

### Margins and Spacing
- **Margins:** 1 inch on all sides (top, bottom, left, right)
- **Page numbers:** Bottom center, 0.5 inches from bottom edge
- **Body text line spacing:** Double-spaced
- **Block quotations:** Single-spaced, indented 1 inch from left margin
- **Footnotes:** Single-spaced
- **Paragraph indentation:** 0.5 inches (first line only)

### Page Numbering
- Start numbering on page 2 (caption page is unnumbered)
- Format: centered, lowercase (e.g., "–2–" or simply "2")
- Position: 0.5 inches above bottom margin

## 3. Citation Style

### Louisiana State Cases
Louisiana follows the **Uniform Rules—Louisiana Courts of Appeal**, not Bluebook, for state court citations.

**Format:**
- Court of Appeal: *State v. Smith*, 2023-00456 (La. App. 1st Cir. 03/15/23), 365 So.3d 789
- Louisiana Supreme Court: *State v. Smith*, 2023-00789 (La. 03/15/23), 365 So.3d 456
- Include docket number in parentheses; include reporter citation (So.3d) if available
- Use "*italics*" for case name

### Federal Cases (5th Circuit)
Use **Bluebook format** for federal cases.

**Format:**
- *United States v. Smith*, 456 F.3d 789 (5th Cir. 2023)
- District court: *United States v. Smith*, 456 F. Supp. 3d 789 (W.D. La. 2023)

### Louisiana Statutes and Rules
- **Revised Statutes:** La. R.S. 14:64 (not "LSA-R.S." or "La. R.S.")
- **Code of Criminal Procedure:** La. C.Cr.P. art. 215
- **Code of Evidence:** La. C.E. art. 801
- **Louisiana Constitution:** La. Const. art. VI, § 5

### Secondary Sources
- **Restatements:** *Restatement (Second) of Contracts* § 90 (1981)
- **Law review articles:** Use Bluebook format for federal law reviews

## 4. Document Structure Conventions

### File Naming System
All D&W documents follow the **3-digit prefix naming system**:

```
[XXX] - [Document Name].docx
```

**Examples:**
- `001 - Motion to Suppress.docx`
- `005 - Memorandum in Support of Motion.docx`
- `010 - Brief in Opposition.docx`

Prefixes are assigned sequentially within each case.

### Motion Document Structure

1. **Caption** (using format in Section 1 above)
2. **Motion Header:** "MOTION TO [ACTION]" (centered, bold)
3. **Body:** Single narrative paragraph or numbered sections explaining the motion
4. **Prayer for Relief:** Ends with specific request (e.g., "WHEREFORE, it is respectfully requested that this Honorable Court grant Defendant's Motion to Suppress and order that all evidence obtained...")
5. **Respectful Submission:** "Respectfully submitted," followed by attorney signature block
6. **Certificate of Service:** Lists method and date of service
7. **Attached Order:** Blank order form for judge signature

### Memorandum in Support Structure

1. **Caption**
2. **Introduction:** Brief statement of purpose and relief sought
3. **Statement of Facts:** Numbered paragraphs detailing procedural and factual background
4. **Argument Section:** Numbered main headings with lettered subheadings; each paragraph begins with topic sentence
5. **Conclusion:** One paragraph restating requested relief
6. **Certificate of Service**

### Certificate of Service Template

```
CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

I hereby certify that a true and correct copy of the foregoing [DOCUMENT NAME] 
has been served upon [District Attorney Name], District Attorney, Parish of 
[PARISH], [METHOD OF SERVICE: hand-delivered/mailed via U.S. mail/electronically 
via ECF/delivered by courier] this _____ day of ____________, 20_____.

Respectfully submitted,

[ATTORNEY NAME]
[ATTORNEY BAR #]
[ADDRESS]
[PHONE]
[EMAIL]
```

## 5. Language Conventions

### Standard Terminology
- **Use:** "Defendant" (not "the accused," "client," or "Mr. [Name]" in motions)
- **Use:** "State" (not "prosecution" or "government" in state cases)
- **Use:** "The Court" (not "the judge" or "Your Honor" in written filings)
- **Use:** "respectfully requests" (standard closing language)
- **Avoid:** First-person pronouns in formal motions (use "Defendant respectfully submits...")

### Writing Style
- **Voice:** Active voice preferred throughout
- **Paragraph length:** Generally 3–5 sentences in argument sections
- **Paragraph openings:** Every argument paragraph must begin with a topic sentence that previews the paragraph's point
- **Sentence structure:** Vary sentence length; avoid strings of long, complex sentences
- **Objections:** Use "Defendant objects to..." rather than "Defendant challenges..."

### Quotations in Motions
- **Block quotes:** Single-spaced, indented 1 inch from left margin, no quotation marks around the indented block
- **In-line quotes:** Use quotation marks; integrate seamlessly into sentence
- **Quote accuracy:** All quotations must match source exactly; use brackets for minor edits `[sic]` for errors in original

## 6. Privileged Markings

### Internal Work Product
For internal D&W documents (drafts, research memos, strategy notes):

```
ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT — PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL
```

### Client Communications
For letters and correspondence to client:

```
PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL — ATTORNEY-CLIENT COMMUNICATION
```

### Court Filings
**Do NOT mark court filings with privilege designations.** Once filed with the court, documents are public record.

## 8. Implementation Notes

### Authority and Updates
This style guide governs all documents produced by D&W Cowork skills. When a specific court (state or federal) publishes **local rules that conflict with this guide, local rules control**. 

Attorneys should update this reference when:
- Court rules change or are amended
- Appellate decisions establish new citation standards
- Firm procedures or client preferences require modification

### Quality Control Checklist
Before filing any document:
- [ ] Caption matches current court format (check local rules)
- [ ] All citations follow Louisiana Uniform Rules or Bluebook as appropriate
- [ ] Font is Times New Roman, 12pt body / 14pt headings
- [ ] Margins are 1" all sides; line spacing is double-spaced
- [ ] Page numbers appear bottom center (if multi-page)
- [ ] Privilege markings removed from court filings
- [ ] Certificate of Service included and accurate
- [ ] All quotations match source text exactly
- [ ] No first-person pronouns in motions ("Defendant respectfully..." not "I respectfully...")
- [ ] Every argument paragraph opens with a topic sentence
