## Notes

- **Divisions of WDLA:** Lake Charles, Lafayette, Alexandria, Shreveport, Monroe. Use the division corresponding to the parish where the offense occurred. Calcasieu Parish → Lake Charles Division.
- **Case number format:** WDLA uses `[division code]:[YR]-cr-[5-digit number]` — e.g., `2:24-cr-00123`. Pull exact format from Case Brain. Division codes: Shreveport=5, Monroe=3, Alexandria=1, Lafayette=6, Lake Charles=2.
- **Citations:** Federal motions use federal citation conventions (Bluebook). See `louisiana-citation-style.md` for hybrid guidance when a federal motion cites Louisiana cases.
- **Service:** Federal motions are served via CM/ECF. See `certificate-of-service.md` federal section.
- **No notice of hearing typically needed** in federal practice unless a hearing is specifically requested or required by the magistrate's scheduling order.
- **Page limits:** WDLA local rules impose page limits — verify against current LR before finalizing.
