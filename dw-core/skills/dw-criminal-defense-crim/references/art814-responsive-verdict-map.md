# Art. 814 Responsive-Verdict Map (skill reference)

**Reference file:** `dw-criminal-defense-crim/references/art814-responsive-verdict-map.md`
**Authority:** La. C.Cr.P. art. 814, effective Aug. 1, 2022 (as reproduced in the firm's West's LSA copy; KeyCite red flag — amended by 2026 La. Sess. Law Serv. Act 103 (H.B. 92); **[VERIFY]** whether Act 103 altered any subsection below before relying on it at trial).

**How the Case Profile uses this file.** In § 4 Charges & Exposure, the **Responsive Verdicts** cell is emitted from this map — not hand-typed. Match the billed offense to its art. 814(A) entry **by offense name** (the authoritative key; the R.S. column is a convenience crosswalk only), then output that verdict set **verbatim and in statutory order**. If the billed offense is **not** listed in art. 814(A), emit `Art. 815 (lesser & necessarily included)` per ¶ B and identify the included offenses the defense will argue.

**Two universal trial rules to carry in the cell's footnote:**
- **¶ C (evidence-sufficiency exclusion):** on motion of either party or the court's own motion, the court **shall exclude** any ¶ A responsive verdict if, after all evidence is in and viewed most favorably to the State, the evidence cannot reasonably support a finding of guilt on that responsive offense. (Responsive verdicts are presumptively available at charging but can be struck at the close of evidence.)
- **¶ D (value/damage ceiling):** where an offense is graded by property value or damage amount, responsive verdicts **cannot exceed** the value/amount charged in the indictment.
- **¶ B / CDS weight (¶ B(2)):** for CDS offenses graded by substance weight, responsive verdicts include lower-weight grades of the charged offense; other CDS offenses not in ¶ A are governed by art. 815.

## Charges NOT in art. 814(A)

If the billed offense is not one of the 71 above, its responsive verdicts are governed by **art. 815** (any lesser and necessarily included offense) and, for attempts, **art. 816**. In the § 4 cell, emit:

`Art. 815 — lesser & necessarily included: [list the included offenses the defense will argue]`

Per Official Revision Comment (b), **attempt is responsive to any non-enumerated offense** (R.S. 14:27). Use the two-part responsiveness test from Comment (c): (1) every essential element of the lesser offense is included in the charged offense, and (2) the lesser offense is of the same generic class.
