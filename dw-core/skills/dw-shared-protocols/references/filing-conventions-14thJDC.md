# Filing Conventions — 14th JDC Calcasieu

Local conventions for filing pleadings in the 14th Judicial District Court, Parish of Calcasieu, Lake Charles.

## Document formatting

- **Font:** Times New Roman, 12 pt body
- **Headings:** Times New Roman, 12 pt, bold, all caps
- **Margins:** 1" all sides
- **Line spacing:** double-spaced body, single-spaced block quotes and footnotes
- **Page numbers:** bottom center, starting on page 2 (page 1 typically unnumbered)
- **Block quotes:** indented 0.5" left and right, single-spaced, no quotation marks
- **Footnotes:** Times New Roman, 10 pt

## Length and structure

- **Page limits:** 14th JDC does not impose a uniform page limit by local rule, but motions exceeding 25 pages should be justified or split.
- **Section headings:** use bold, all-caps, centered headings for major sections (e.g., `STATEMENT OF FACTS`, `LAW AND ARGUMENT`)
- **Argument sub-headings:** use bold, sentence-case, left-justified for sub-headings within argument
- **Numbered argument paragraphs:** use Arabic numerals (1., 2., 3.); sub-arguments use lowercase letters (a., b., c.)

## Filing mechanics

- **E-filing:** 14th JDC uses the Louisiana eFile portal. Confirm current portal URL in firm config.
- **PDF requirements:** PDF/A preferred for e-filing; OCR'd if scanned; bookmarked for documents over 20 pages
- **Exhibit handling:**
  - Exhibits attached to motions should be tabbed (Exhibit 1, Exhibit 2, etc.)
  - Each exhibit gets a cover sheet identifying the exhibit and the matter
  - Sealed exhibits filed separately under a motion to file under seal
- **Courtesy copy:** confirm with judge's chambers — some divisions request paper courtesy copies for hearings, others do not.

## Hearing setting in 14th JDC

- Most contested motions require a Notice of Hearing filed contemporaneously (see `notice-of-hearing.md`).
- Hearings are scheduled by the division minute clerk based on the assigned division's calendar.
- Pre-trial conferences, motion hearings, and trial dates are typically set at status conferences.

## Service in 14th JDC

- Service on the Calcasieu Parish District Attorney's Office is generally accepted via:
  1. Email to the assigned ADA (preferred — confirm email is on record)
  2. Hand delivery to the DA's office at the Calcasieu Parish Courthouse
- Always pull the assigned ADA's name and email from the Case Brain — do not assume from prior cases.

## Common pitfalls (to avoid)

- Filing a motion without a contemporaneous notice of hearing → motion sits unset
- Filing a motion without a proposed order → court has nothing to sign
- Caption errors (wrong division letter, wrong judge) → clerk may reject or judge may strike sua sponte
- Missing certificate of service → opposing counsel can object on procedural grounds

## Variables to confirm before filing

- Assigned division and judge (current — judge rotations happen)
- Assigned ADA name and email (current)
- Docket number (verify against most recent court filing)
- Current e-filing portal URL and credentials
