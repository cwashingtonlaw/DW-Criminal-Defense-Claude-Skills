## Statute Comparison Matrix

| Statute | Offense | Key Elements | Penalty | Registration |
|---------|---------|--------------|---------|--------------|
| **La. R.S. 14:42** | First Degree Rape | Vaginal intercourse by force, threat of force, or when victim unable to consent | Life without parole (mandatory) | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:42.1** | Second Degree Rape | Vaginal intercourse: victim under 13 w/out consent; consent by threat; incapacitated; authority figure abuse | 25–99 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:43** | Third Degree Rape (Simple Rape) | Vaginal intercourse: victim 13–17; without consent; under 17 with authority figure | 5–20 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:43.1** | Sexual Battery | Intentional touching of genitals/breasts w/o consent; or non-consensual sexual contact | 2–10 years hard labor | Tier 2 |
| **La. R.S. 14:43.2** | Second Degree Sexual Battery | Sexual contact by threat; incapacitated victim; victim under 13; authority figure abuse | 5–20 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:43.3** | Oral Sexual Battery | Intentional oral sexual contact w/o consent; separate from genital contact | 2–10 years hard labor | Tier 2 |
| **La. R.S. 14:81** | Indecent Behavior with Juveniles | Indecent/immoral/sexual conduct in presence of minor w/intent to arouse | Up to 5 years; registrable at age 17+ | Tier 2 |
| **La. R.S. 14:81.1** | Pornography Involving Juveniles | Possession, distribution, production of material involving minor in sexual conduct | Up to 10 years; life if repeat | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:81.2** | Molestation of Juvenile | Intentional molestation of a child under 17 by force or threat; or touching w/intent to arouse | 10–20 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:89** | Crime Against Nature | Non-procreative sexual conduct; statute narrowed post-*Lawrence v. Texas* | 5–20 years hard labor | Tier 2/3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:89.1** | Aggravated Crime Against Nature | Non-procreative conduct by force, threat, or with minor | 10–20 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:46.2** | Human Trafficking | Inducing person into trafficking for labor/sexual exploitation | 5–20 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
| **La. R.S. 14:46.3** | Trafficking of Children for Sexual Purposes | Knowingly recruiting child for sexual abuse/exploitation | 25–99 years hard labor | Tier 3 |
