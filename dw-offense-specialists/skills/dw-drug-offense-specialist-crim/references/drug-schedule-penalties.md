## SCHEDULE I (R.S. 40:966)

Substances: Heroin, LSD, psilocybin, MDMA (ecstasy), GHB, marijuana (>2.5 lbs).

### Marijuana Special Rules

**Quantity thresholds:**
- Less than 14g: Misdemeanor, up to $300 fine (first offense)
- 14g to 2.5 lbs: Felony, 4-10 years hard labor
- More than 2.5 lbs: Felony, 5-20 years hard labor

**Post-Act 274 (2024):** Small amount first offense = misdemeanor treatment, not felony.

### Non-Marijuana Schedule I

**Simple possession:** 4-10 years hard labor

**Possession with intent to distribute (PWID):** 5-30 years hard labor

**Distribution:** 5-30 years hard labor

**Manufacturing/cultivation:** 10-30 years hard labor

## SCHEDULE II (R.S. 40:967)

Substances: Cocaine, methamphetamine, fentanyl, oxycodone, PCP, morphine.

### Cocaine-Specific Threshold Penalties

**Critical for trafficking charges — these are MINIMUM sentences:**

- **Simple possession:** 2-5 years hard labor

- **Possession with intent to distribute:** 2-30 years hard labor

- **Distribution:** 2-30 years hard labor

- **Trafficking thresholds (MANDATORY MINIMUMS):**
  - 10g or more: 5-year mandatory minimum + 2-year mandatory parole
  - 28g or more: 10-year mandatory minimum + 2-year mandatory parole
  - 200g or more: 20-year mandatory minimum + 2-year mandatory parole
  - 400g or more: 40-year mandatory minimum + 2-year mandatory parole

### Methamphetamine-Specific

- **Simple possession:** 2-5 years hard labor
- **PWID:** 2-30 years hard labor
- **Distribution:** 2-30 years hard labor
- **Trafficking (500mg or more):** Enhanced penalties, check current statutory threshold

### Fentanyl

- **Simple possession:** 2-5 years hard labor
- **PWID:** 2-30 years hard labor
- **Distribution:** 2-30 years hard labor (fentanyl carries enhanced penalties in some contexts due to overdose risk)

---

## SCHEDULE III (R.S. 40:968)

Substances: Anabolic steroids, ketamine, hydrocodone + acetaminophen combinations.

**Simple possession:** Up to 5 years hard labor, fine up to $5,000

**PWID or distribution:** Up to 10 years hard labor, fine up to $5,000

---

## SCHEDULE IV (R.S. 40:969)

Substances: Alprazolam (Xanax), diazepam (Valium), zolpidem (Ambien), lorazepam (Ativan), tramadol.

**Simple possession:** Up to 5 years hard labor, fine up to $5,000

**PWID or distribution:** Up to 5 years hard labor, fine up to $5,000

---

## SCHEDULE V (R.S. 40:970)

Substances: Cough syrups containing codeine, antidiarrheal medications with difenoxin, other low-strength preparations.

**Simple possession:** Up to 2 years hard labor, fine up to $5,000

**PWID or distribution:** Up to 3 years hard labor, fine up to $5,000

## MANDATORY ENHANCEMENTS

### Drug-Free Zone Enhancement (R.S. 40:981.3)

**Triggering circumstances:** Offense committed within 2,000 feet of:
- Public or nonpublic school (K-12)
- Church or other house of worship
- Public housing development
- Community center
- Youth recreation center
- College campus

**Penalty effect:** DOUBLES the sentence for the underlying offense + adds 5-year mandatory minimum hard labor

**Example:** Simple possession of cocaine normally carries 2-5 years. Within drug-free zone: 4-10 years base + 5-year mandatory = effectively 10 years minimum exposure.

**Defense challenge:** Prosecution must prove defendant KNEW they were within 2,000 feet. Mere proximity is insufficient. If defendant was unaware of school location or zone boundary, challenge this element.

### Prior Drug Felony Enhancement

**If defendant has prior felony drug conviction:**
- Escalates penalties on new drug felony
- Second felony: baseline penalties increased
- Accumulating prior felonies: escalating mandatory minimums
- Can trigger habitual offender status (route to dw-habitual-offender-auditor-crim)

### Firearm Enhancement

**Possession or use of firearm during drug offense:**
- Adds 5-year mandatory hard labor to base sentence
- Firearm must be actually used or found in immediate vicinity (dominion and control)

### Minor Present Enhancement

**Drug offense committed while minor (under 17) present in immediate vicinity:**
- Enhances base penalties
- Exact enhancement varies by underlying charge

## WEIGHT DETERMINATION CRITICAL POINTS

### Gross vs. Net Weight

**Gross weight = substance + packaging, containers, cutting agents, moisture, etc.**

**Net weight = pure substance only.**

**Why this matters:** For cocaine trafficking charges, 28g gross weight might be only 18g net weight — difference between 10-year mandatory and 2-5 years simple possession.

**Audit requirement:** When prosecution proves weight, demand clear documentation of:
- Container tare weight
- Gross weight (substance + container)
- Net weight calculation (gross - tare)
- Moisture content testing (if applicable)

### Mixture Analysis

For drug mixtures (cocaine cut with levamisole, heroin with filler, etc.):
- **Lab must identify pure drug weight separately**
- Prosecution may charge based on mixture weight (inflated), but defense should argue net pure substance weight
- Example: 50g cocaine + levamisole mixture; pure cocaine = 35g. Charge may be based on 50g, but defense argues 35g applies.

### Pill/Tablet Dosing

For pharmaceutical pills (oxycodone, alprazolam):
- Each pill's weight is often pre-determined (known weight per tablet)
- If lab reports aggregate weight of 100 pills, verify actual drug content per pill
- Fillers in pills can inflate gross weight

- **Substance → Schedule mapping** under R.S. 40:964
  - Schedule I: heroin, LSD, marijuana >2.5 lbs, ecstasy, GHB
  - Schedule II: cocaine, methamphetamine, fentanyl, oxycodone, PCP
  - Schedule III: anabolic steroids, ketamine, hydrocodone combinations
  - Schedule IV: alprazolam, diazepam, zolpidem, tramadol
  - Schedule V: cough syrups with codeine

- **Charge tier → penalty matrix:**
  - Simple possession: 4-10 years (Schedule I/II), up to 5 years (III/IV), up to 2 years (V)
  - PWID: 5-30 years (most Schedules)
  - Distribution: 2-30 years depending on Schedule
  - Trafficking: mandatory minimums (cocaine 10g = 5 years mandatory, 28g = 10 years, 200g = 20 years, etc.)

- **Penalty enhancements to flag:**
  - Drug-free zone (R.S. 40:981.3): offense within 2,000 feet of school, church, public housing → penalties DOUBLED + 5-year mandatory
  - Firearm enhancement: possession or use of firearm during drug offense → add 5-year mandatory
  - Minor present: drug offense with child present → enhanced penalty
  - Prior drug felony: escalates penalties substantially
