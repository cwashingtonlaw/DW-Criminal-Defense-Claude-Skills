# DWI Enhancement Ladder: Louisiana R.S. 14:98

> ⚠️ **OFFENSE-DATE / VERSION CONTROL — read before quoting any value in this file.** The statute version in force on the **DATE OF OFFENSE** controls. Louisiana DWI penalties are amended frequently: Act 385 of 2014 (eff. Jan. 1, 2015) moved the per-offense penalties out of R.S. 14:98(A)-(D) into **R.S. 14:98.1 (first), 14:98.2 (second), 14:98.3 (third), and 14:98.4 (fourth+)** — 14:98 now defines the offense and houses the child-endangerment and vehicle-seizure provisions, and current R.S. 14:98.5 is "Special provisions and definitions" (NOT child endangerment). Further amendments run through Acts 2024, No. 662 and Acts 2025, No. 194 (eff. June 8, 2025). Core tier values below were checked against the current compiled statutes (Aug. 2026); anything flagged `[VERIFY CITATION]` could not be confirmed in the current text — verify on Westlaw or legis.la.gov before relying on it, and never apply current values to a pre-amendment offense date without a version compare. License-suspension periods are largely administrative (Title 32, R.S. 32:414 et seq.) — verify those separately. Never fabricate a prior-version value.

## Overview

DWI penalties in Louisiana escalate based on:
1. Number of prior DWI convictions within 10-year cleansing period
2. BAC level (≥0.15, ≥0.20 aggravate penalties)
3. Presence of child passenger under 13 (child-endangerment provision of R.S. 14:98 under current law; the former "14:98.5" cite predates the 2015 restructuring)
4. Accident with injury

**10-Year Cleansing Period:** Convictions older than 10 years do NOT count toward offense level.

**Penalties:**
- Fine: $300-$1,000

## Key Definitions

**Mandatory Minimum:** Judge MUST sentence to at least this term (no exceptions, no suspending this portion of jail/prison time)

**License Suspension:** Driving privilege revoked for specified period; eligibility for reinstatement after period

**License Revocation:** Permanent; application for reinstatement required (and may be denied)

**Ignition Interlock Device (IID):** Vehicle start requires breath sample showing BAC < 0.02%; records download at service location for monitoring

**Cleansing Period (10 years):** Convictions older than 10 years from date of current offense do NOT count toward offense level; client "resets" to first offense if prior is >10 years old

## Aggravating Factors Checklist

- [ ] BAC ≥ 0.15% (enhances penalties)
- [ ] BAC ≥ 0.20% (further enhances)
- [ ] Child passenger under 13 in vehicle (child-endangerment provision of R.S. 14:98 under current law — mandatory minimum not suspendable; the former "14:98.5 felony enhancement" cite `[VERIFY CITATION]`)
- [ ] Accident with property damage
- [ ] Accident with injury to another person
- [ ] Refusal to submit to test (separate charge under R.S. 32:661)
- [ ] License suspended/revoked at time of offense

---

## Mitigating Factors to Investigate

- [ ] Client's age and driving record (prior traffic violations only, if any)
- [ ] Circumstances of traffic stop (checkpoint vs. individualized suspicion)
- [ ] Quality of SFST administration (protocol deviations favor client)
- [ ] Testing methodology (breath calibration issues, blood chain of custody)
- [ ] Medical conditions mimicking impairment (diabetes, hypoglycemia, inner ear disorder)
- [ ] Rising BAC defense applicability
- [ ] Substance abuse history (treatability for favorable sentence consideration)
- [ ] Employment status, family stability
- [ ] Voluntary enrollment in treatment/counseling
