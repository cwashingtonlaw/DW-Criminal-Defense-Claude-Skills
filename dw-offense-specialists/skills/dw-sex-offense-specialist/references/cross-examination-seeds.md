# Cross-Examination Seeds

For integration with dw-cross-exam-architect skill.

## SANE Nurse Cross-Examination Seeds

```
CERTIFICATION & TRAINING:
Q: You are SANE-A or SANE-P certified?
Q: What specific training did you complete for [certification]?
Q: How many sexual assault exams have you performed?
Q: What percentage of your exams resulted in findings of injury?

TIMING & ACUITY:
Q: This exam was performed [TIME] hours after the alleged assault, correct?
Q: Is it true that acute injuries are less likely to be visible beyond 72 hours?
Q: If this exam showed no injury, would that be consistent with consensual
   intercourse?

FINDINGS INTERPRETATION:
Q: You found [SPECIFIC FINDING]—can that finding occur in non-abused individuals?
Q: Explain the range of normal hymenal variations in [age] year-olds
Q: [If no injury]: Is it unusual to find no genital injury in confirmed abuse cases?

METHODOLOGY:
Q: Were you following [hospital protocol] during this examination?
Q: Can you explain the specific technique you used for swabbing the [area]?
Q: How did you document chain of custody for the specimens collected?
```

## DNA Analyst Cross-Examination Seeds

```
MIXTURE INTERPRETATION:
Q: Can you define what a DNA mixture is and explain its significance in this case?
Q: What probabilistic genotyping software was used to interpret the mixture?
Q: Who programmed the assumptions fed into [TrueAllele/STRmix]?
Q: Can a DNA profile be present in a specimen through innocent contact (not related
   to sexual assault)?

TOUCH DNA:
Q: Touch DNA can be transferred without direct contact, correct?
Q: Touch DNA does not indicate sexual contact or assault, does it?
Q: If the defendant had been in the alleged victim's home before [date of alleged
   assault], could his DNA be present for that reason alone?

ACCREDITATION & STANDARDS:
Q: Is your laboratory accredited? [Which certification?]
Q: Can you describe your quality assurance procedures?
Q: What are the results of your lab's proficiency testing for mixture interpretation?

STORAGE & DEGRADATION:
Q: This kit was stored for [TIME] before analysis—what is the risk of degradation?
Q: What storage temperature and conditions are required for proper preservation?
Q: Can storage conditions affect the reliability of the DNA results?
```

## Forensic Interviewer Cross-Examination Seeds

```
PROTOCOL COMPLIANCE:
Q: What interview protocol were you following? [NCMEC/ICAN/state protocol?]
Q: Were you trained in this protocol before conducting this interview?
Q: Step through the interview—did you follow open-ended questions first or
   leading questions?

LEADING QUESTIONS:
Q: You asked the child "[QUOTE LEADING QUESTION]"—is that an open-ended question
   or a leading question?
Q: Did you ask the child the same question multiple times? [Review transcript for
   repetition]
Q: Can repeated questioning about the same topic influence a child's response?

RECORDING:
Q: This interview was recorded, correct?
Q: Is the full recording available, or only portions?
Q: Were there any conversations off-camera or off-recording with this child before
   the formal interview?

ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS:
Q: Did you inquire about [innocuous explanation] as alternative to alleged assault?
Q: Can [alternative explanation] explain [detail of child's statement]?
Q: Did you document the child's ability to distinguish fantasy from reality?
```
