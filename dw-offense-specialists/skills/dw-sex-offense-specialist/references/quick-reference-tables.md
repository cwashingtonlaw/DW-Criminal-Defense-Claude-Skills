## DNA Analysis Methodology Comparison

| Method | Purpose | Mixture Capability | Individual Identification | Limitations |
|--------|---------|-------------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| Short Tandem Repeat (STR) | Individual identification | Limited in complex mixtures | High (if <2–3 contributors) | Struggles with 4+ contributors |
| Probabilistic Genotyping (TrueAllele/STRmix) | Interpret complex mixtures | High | Possible but probabilistic | Results depend on software assumptions |
| Y-STR | Male DNA screening | Limited | Identifies male contributor only | Cannot identify individual male |
| Mitochondrial DNA | Maternal lineage | Limited | Identifies maternal line only | Low discrimination power |
