# Module I — Case-Type Specific Templates

Pre-built investigation checklists for common case types. These supplement (not replace) the universal task generation in Module A.

## Homicide Investigation Template

**Immediate (CRITICAL Priority — 48-72 hours):**
- [ ] Scene visit with full photographic and video documentation
- [ ] Identify and photograph all surveillance cameras within 3-block radius
- [ ] Contact all surveillance camera owners for footage preservation
- [ ] Begin neighborhood canvass (3-block radius minimum)
- [ ] Obtain 911 recordings and CAD records
- [ ] Obtain all body camera and dash camera footage
- [ ] Identify and locate all eyewitnesses
- [ ] Begin alibi investigation and verification
- [ ] Obtain autopsy report and request independent review
- [ ] Identify cell tower locations near scene for CSLI analysis
- [ ] Preserve defendant's social media and digital accounts
- [ ] Secure defendant's cell phone and vehicle (if not seized)

**High Priority (1-2 weeks):**
- [ ] Conduct witness interviews (all identified witnesses)
- [ ] Obtain victim's background (criminal history, civil litigation, social media, reputation)
- [ ] Investigate victim's activities in 72 hours before death
- [ ] Investigate defendant's activities in 72 hours before incident
- [ ] Obtain and analyze cell phone records (defendant and victim)
- [ ] Investigate relationship between defendant and victim
- [ ] Identify and investigate alternative suspects
- [ ] Obtain forensic reports (DNA, ballistics, fingerprints, trace evidence)
- [ ] Arrange independent forensic review
- [ ] Investigate officer personnel records (Giglio material)
- [ ] Obtain crime scene processing records and photos (complete set)
- [ ] Obtain medical examiner's complete file (notes, photographs, specimens)

**Medium Priority (30 days):**
- [ ] Conduct background investigations on all prosecution witnesses
- [ ] Obtain prior testimony of prosecution witnesses in other cases
- [ ] Research similar cases with same officer(s) for pattern evidence
- [ ] Investigate confidential informant involvement (if applicable)
- [ ] Obtain neighborhood crime statistics and pattern data
- [ ] Arrange defense expert consultations (forensics, pathology, reconstruction)
- [ ] Obtain defendant's complete medical and mental health records
- [ ] Investigate mitigation evidence (background, family, trauma, substance abuse, mental health)

## Drug Offense Investigation Template

**Immediate (CRITICAL Priority — 48-72 hours):**
- [ ] Scene visit — document who has access to location
- [ ] Document proximity to drug-free zones (measure exact distances)
- [ ] Obtain search warrant and affidavit
- [ ] Obtain surveillance operation records (if applicable)
- [ ] Identify and locate all persons present at time of search
- [ ] Preserve surveillance footage from area
- [ ] Obtain confidential informant records (if applicable)

**High Priority (1-2 weeks):**
- [ ] Investigate basis for stop/search — challenge reasonable suspicion/probable cause
- [ ] Investigate search warrant reliability — informant reliability, staleness, particularity
- [ ] Obtain officer personnel files and complaint history
- [ ] Investigate confidential informant background and reliability
- [ ] Interview all persons present at time of arrest/search
- [ ] Obtain lab reports and chain of custody
- [ ] Arrange independent lab testing if appropriate
- [ ] Investigate constructive possession issues (access, knowledge, dominion/control)
- [ ] Obtain lease/rental/ownership records for location
- [ ] Obtain defendant's phone records (to challenge drug distribution allegations)
- [ ] Investigate entrapment defense (if applicable)

**Medium Priority (30 days):**
- [ ] Background investigations on prosecution witnesses
- [ ] Research officer's drug arrest history and patterns
- [ ] Investigate informant's deal and benefits received
- [ ] Obtain agency drug enforcement policies and procedures
- [ ] Research lab accreditation and analyst qualifications
- [ ] Investigate money and asset seizure procedures

## Sex Offense Investigation Template

**Immediate (CRITICAL Priority — 48-72 hours):**
- [ ] Obtain SANE examination report and photographs
- [ ] Identify and preserve all digital communications between defendant and complainant
- [ ] Preserve social media accounts (defendant and complainant)
- [ ] Identify alibi witnesses and corroboration
- [ ] Obtain all forensic evidence reports (DNA, biological evidence)
- [ ] Scene visit (if applicable)

**High Priority (1-2 weeks):**
- [ ] Investigate complainant's background (prior false allegations, motive, bias)
- [ ] Investigate relationship history between defendant and complainant
- [ ] Obtain and analyze text messages, social media communications, and emails between parties
- [ ] Interview defense witnesses (consent witnesses, relationship witnesses)
- [ ] Obtain defendant's medical records (if injuries claimed)
- [ ] Investigate timing and circumstances of disclosure
- [ ] Investigate forensic interview of complainant (if child victim)
- [ ] Obtain CAC (Child Advocacy Center) records (if applicable)
- [ ] Investigate collateral contacts by complainant (friends, family, therapist)

**Medium Priority (30 days):**
- [ ] Background investigations on prosecution witnesses
- [ ] Investigate complainant's civil litigation (pending or planned)
- [ ] Research expert witnesses (forensic psychology, DNA, false memory, child suggestibility)
- [ ] Obtain complainant's mental health and counseling records (with court order if necessary)
- [ ] Investigate delayed disclosure patterns and alternative explanations

## Robbery Investigation Template

**Immediate (CRITICAL Priority):**
- [ ] Scene visit — document layout, sight lines, lighting, cameras
- [ ] Obtain surveillance footage from scene and surrounding area
- [ ] Investigate identification procedure (photo lineup, live lineup, show-up)
- [ ] Challenge eyewitness identification reliability
- [ ] Establish alibi investigation
- [ ] Obtain 911 calls and initial descriptions

**High Priority (1-2 weeks):**
- [ ] Compare initial victim description to defendant's actual appearance
- [ ] Investigate all identification procedures for suggestiveness
- [ ] Interview all witnesses to identification
- [ ] Obtain defendant's location data (phone, GPS, transactions, surveillance)
- [ ] Investigate prior robberies with similar MO (alternative suspect)
- [ ] Obtain weapon analysis (if applicable)
- [ ] Interview witnesses to defendant's whereabouts at time of offense

## DUI/DWI Investigation Template

**Immediate (CRITICAL Priority):**
- [ ] Scene visit — road conditions, visibility, signage, lane markings
- [ ] Obtain dash camera and body camera footage
- [ ] Obtain breathalyzer/intoxilyzer maintenance and calibration records
- [ ] Obtain officer DUI detection training records
- [ ] Obtain dispatch records

**High Priority (1-2 weeks):**
- [ ] Investigate basis for traffic stop — challenge reasonable suspicion
- [ ] Analyze field sobriety test administration (compare to NHTSA standards)
- [ ] Obtain breathalyzer operator certification records
- [ ] Obtain breathalyzer device maintenance logs and error logs
- [ ] Obtain blood draw procedures and chain of custody (if blood test)
- [ ] Obtain toxicology lab accreditation and analyst qualifications
- [ ] Investigate alternative explanations for driving pattern and physical symptoms
- [ ] Obtain defendant's medical records (conditions mimicking impairment)
- [ ] Interview passenger witnesses
- [ ] Obtain traffic camera or surveillance footage of driving
