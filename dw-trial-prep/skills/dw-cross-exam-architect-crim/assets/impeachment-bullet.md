# ASSET — Impeachment Bullet Formats (Step 5)

**Where these go.** Nowhere on the chapter page. The chapter table is `SOURCE/EXHIBIT | QUESTIONS` and ends in a blank notes box; no impeachment bullet, branch, or flag is printed there.

These formats are for the **Step 5 report to the attorney** — delivered in the build conversation, chapter by chapter — and, for anything that could draw an objection, for the **`Ground to state` / `Proffer substance` columns of the Preservation Log** (`preservation-log.md`).

The impeachment work is not optional because it is unprinted. Every one of these still gets written; it just does not ride to counsel table on the chapter page.

---

## Standard prior inconsistent statement

> ⚠ **IMPEACHMENT:** Witness stated [X] in [(N) Doc A, p. ___] but stated [Y] in [(N) Doc B, p. ___].
> • IF ADMITS → bank it, move to next chapter
> • IF DENIES → confront with (N) [Document Title], [ref]
> • IF NO RECALL → refresh with (N), then confront
> • La. C.E. art. 613 foundation required before **extrinsic** proof only — not before asking. Do not telegraph.

## Prior conviction

> ⚠ **CONVICTION IMPEACHMENT:** [Offense], [date], [sentence] — (N) [source].
> • `DETAILS HELD IN RESERVE — admissible only if witness denies or explains (La. C.E. art. 609.1(C)(1)-(2))`
> • Art. 609.1(B): no inquiry into arrest, warrant, indictment, prosecution, or acquittal.
> • **Reserve questions are drafted and reported, never placed in the QUESTIONS column** — nothing should be readable aloud that the door has not opened.

## Omission

> ⚠ **OMISSION:** [Standard/duty] required [X]; (N) [Document] shows it absent.
> • Establish the duty first, then the absence. An omission the witness must concede is often stronger than a contradiction he can explain.

## Credibility item failing the art. 608(B) gate

> `[608(B) REVIEW REQUIRED]` — [material]. Does not route through conviction (609.1), bias (607(D)(1)), accuracy of this testimony (607(C)), or prior inconsistent statement (613). Attorney to assess constitutional exception. **Preservation Log row required.**

---

Every bullet carries the `(N)` source register prefix, using the Evidence Item title exactly as the Source Register writes it. No exceptions.

Every flagged question named in a Step 5 report must also appear as a Preservation Log row with its ground and proffer substance filled in. A flag reported with no log row is a defect.
