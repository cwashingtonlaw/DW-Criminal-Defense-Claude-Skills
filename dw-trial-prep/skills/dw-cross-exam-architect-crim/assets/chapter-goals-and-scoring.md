# ASSET — Chapter Goals Block & LE Scoring

Every chapter opens with a goals block:

```
CHAPTER GOALS:
• Goal 1 — what this chapter must establish
• Goal 2
• Goal 3
[Law Enforcement only: Impact: _/3 | Fragility: _/3]
```

**Rules**
- Two to four goals. A chapter needing five is two chapters.
- Each goal states what the chapter must *establish*, not what it will *ask*.
- The case theme is referenced in every substantive chapter's goals and appears in at least one chapter title.

## Impact / Fragility scoring — Law Enforcement chapters only

| Score | Impact | Fragility |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor concession | Officer likely to concede |
| 2 | Meaningful damage | May resist |
| 3 | Potential case-winner | Will fight hard |

**Use of the scores**
- Order CRITICAL DMAR findings and Impact-3 chapters into positions you are certain to reach.
- **Fragility 3 requires all three branches pre-drafted plus the second and third questions of the confrontation** — improvising a fight in front of the jury is where crosses go wrong.
- Impact 1 / Fragility 3 is usually a chapter to cut. High cost, low return.
