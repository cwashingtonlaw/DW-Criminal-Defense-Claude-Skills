# ASSET — Preservation Log (Step 5.5)

Appended to every cross-examination outline as the **final section**. It is the one place in the outline where prep text still prints — the chapter pages carry only sources, questions, and the blank notes box, so art. 841 and art. 103(A)(2) protection has nowhere else to live, and it cannot be composed at the podium.

**Four columns arrive pre-filled. Four are filled in during trial.**

| Chapter | Question # | Ground to state | Proffer substance | Ruling | Proffer made | Form of proffer | Issue code |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *pre-filled* | *pre-filled* | *pre-filled* | *pre-filled* | Sustained / Overruled | Yes / No | Q&A / statement / document | |

- **Chapter** and **Question #** — one row per flagged question, in chapter and question order. Question numbers restart at 1 in each chapter, so a row is identified by chapter **and** number, never by number alone.
- **Ground to state** — the specific ground to assert on the record at the moment of the ruling, by article. Grounds by flag type: `references/error-preservation-protocol.md` §3.
- **Proffer substance** — one sentence on what the witness would have testified or what the exhibit shows, with the `(N)` source citation.

Never write "object and preserve." Name the ground and name the substance. An attorney reading the log mid-cross has no time to compose either.

**Example row (illustrative only — not case facts):**

| Chapter | Q# | Ground to state | Proffer substance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 10 | Prior inconsistent statement, La. C.E. art. 613; cross extends to any matter relevant to any issue including credibility, La. C.E. art. 611(B) | The witness swore at the suppression hearing that only one person was inside, contradicting his own supplemental report — (2) Supplemental Incident Report (03/14/2024), Bates 0052 — and his body-worn camera, (3) Body-Worn Camera Recording, 00:04:12. Request the proffer be taken outside the jury's presence. La. C.Cr.P. art. 841; La. C.E. art. 103(A)(2) |

**Filled in during trial:** Ruling, Proffer made, Form of proffer, Issue code.

**After trial:** any sustained objection with no proffer is marked **UNPRESERVED** so the attorney can decide whether to revisit before the State rests. The completed log hands off to `dw-appellate-error-monitor-crim` (harmless-error pre-assessment) and `dw-issue-code-tracker-crim`.

**Delivery note.** Because the chapter pages no longer carry preservation bullets, say plainly when handing over the package which chapters contain flagged questions and that the grounds for them are in this log. An attorney working only from the chapter pages is working without them.
