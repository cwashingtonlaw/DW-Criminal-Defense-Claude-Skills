# Red-Team Checklist (Step 8.5)

A **self-check**. Do not hand the outline to `dw-adversarial-stress-test-crim` — see the direction warning in `integration-map.md`.

Run before presenting the package, while there is still time to fix what it finds. A red-team pass the night before trial produces anxiety, not improvement.

| # | Target | What you are looking for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Door-openers** | Questions inviting damaging testimony, opening art. 609.1(C) conviction details against the defense, or handing the State a redirect it would not otherwise get |
| 2 | **Chapters resting on unproduced material** | A Discovery Gap Report item that a chapter silently depends on. If the document never arrives, does the chapter collapse? |
| 3 | **Explainable impeachment** | Contradictions with an innocent explanation the outline does not anticipate. Assume the witness has the best available answer |
| 4 | **Missing denial branches** | Fragility-3 chapters drafted only for the admit answer. These need the second and third questions of the confrontation pre-drafted, not just the first |
| 5 | **Preservation gaps** | Flagged questions with no ground named and no proffer substance |
| 6 | **Art. 608(B) exposure** | Credibility material not routing through conviction (609.1), bias (607(D)(1)), accuracy of this testimony (607(C)), or prior inconsistent statement (613) |
| 7 | **Theory conflict** | Any chapter that contradicts or undermines the selected defense theory |
| 8 | **Sequencing risk** | CRITICAL DMAR findings and Impact-3 chapters buried where the cross may not reach them |
| 9 | **Length** | A cross the attorney cannot deliver in the time available is a cross that ends badly. What gets cut if half the time disappears? |

**Output:** report what you found and what you changed. Surface anything you cannot fix without attorney input rather than shipping it. Optional on a Fast Path build — note it in the Fast Path notice.
