**Multi-agency and task force witnesses.** When an officer testifies as a member of a narcotics task force, a federal task force (DEA/ATF/FBI TFO), or a multi-parish unit, treat the agency question as a cross-examination target in its own right:

- Which agency employs the officer, and which agency's policy manual governed his conduct that day?
- Whose report-writing standard applied — the home agency's or the task force's?
- Whose evidence-handling and BWC policy applied at the scene?
- Was the officer deputized federally, and does that change the applicable recording or disclosure obligation?
- Two policy manuals means two chances to prove a violation. Demand both.

Mark any answer the attorney has not confirmed as `[UNSOURCED — VERIFY BEFORE USING AT TRIAL]`.

**Citation convention.** Spell the lab out in full in the Source Register and in every SOURCE/EXHIBIT bullet, with the `(N)` register prefix — no abbreviations, no exceptions:

`(6) Lab Report — Southwest Louisiana Crime Lab, Case #2026-00789, p. 4, Conclusion`

The abbreviations in the table above are for **your** internal shorthand while working, never for a deliverable. One name per document, everywhere. The register is read by co-counsel and investigators who may not share the shorthand, and two renderings of one citation is how the wrong report gets pulled at counsel table.

**Standing analyst-cross demands** (add any missing item to the Discovery Gap Report):
- Analyst CV and current certification
- Lab accreditation certificate covering the discipline **and the date of the analysis**
- Standard operating procedure in force on the analysis date (not the current SOP)
- Instrument calibration and maintenance logs for the specific instrument
- Bench notes and raw data, not just the signed report
- Proficiency test results for the analyst
- Corrective action / non-conformance records for the relevant period
- Chain of custody from seizure through analysis

## 3. Standing Document Demands — All Law Enforcement Witnesses

Every LE cross should confirm these were requested and note in the Discovery Gap Report whether each was produced. Do **not** assert in an outline that a document exists or does not exist unless it is sourced.

**Recordings**
- Body-worn camera — full unedited file, not a clip; all officers on scene, not just the affiant
- In-car / dash camera
- Station or booking video
- Dispatch audio and CAD/call-for-service log with timestamps
- Jail calls (route to `dw-jail-call-analyzer-crim`)
- Interview room recordings

**Written**
- Initial incident report AND every supplemental
- Field notes (frequently exist, frequently not produced)
- Arrest affidavit / probable cause affidavit
- Evidence and property receipts; chain of custody log
- Use of force reports
- Consent-to-search forms
- Miranda waiver forms
- Search warrant, application, affidavit, and return

**Policy and background**
- The agency policy manual sections in force on the incident date — BWC activation, report writing, evidence handling, use of force
- Training records for the officer in the relevant discipline
- Officer's personnel/disciplinary file — route to `dw-brady-giglio-auditor-crim`. Do not draft credibility questions from an unproduced file. **And note that production does not equal admissibility:** in Louisiana state court, La. C.E. art. 608(B) bars using particular acts or courses of conduct to attack truthfulness. A produced disciplinary file is usable on cross only through one of the four lawful routes — conviction (art. 609.1), bias/interest/corruption (art. 607(D)(1), extrinsic proof permitted), accuracy of this testimony (art. 607(C)), or prior inconsistent statement (art. 613) — or on a preserved constitutional-exception argument. Run the art. 608(B) gate in `witness-prioritization-audit.md` before drafting

**Auto-flag rule.** When a standing demand item is absent for a witness whose testimony depends on it, add a chapter goal targeting the absence itself. An omission the officer must concede on the stand is often stronger than a contradiction he can explain. Frame it as a short-question sequence under `witness-type-modules.md` — establish the policy, establish the duty, establish the absence.
