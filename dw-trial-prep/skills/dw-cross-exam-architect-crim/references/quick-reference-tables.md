# Quick Reference — Louisiana Evidence Rules for Cross

**Criminal cases, Louisiana state court.** For federal matters, switch to the federal column in §4 of `jurisdiction-and-court-map.md`.

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Scope of cross — wide open** | **La. C.E. art. 611(B)** — "A witness may be cross-examined on any matter relevant to any issue in the case, including credibility." Cross is NOT limited to the scope of direct |
| Court's control over interrogation | La. C.E. art. 611(A) |
| Leading questions permitted on cross | La. C.E. art. 611(C) |
| Redirect and recross | La. C.E. art. 611(D) |
| Prior inconsistent statement — foundation | La. C.E. art. 613 |
| Attacking and supporting credibility generally | La. C.E. art. 607 |
| **Intrinsic attack — any matter tending to disprove truthfulness OR ACCURACY of the testimony** | **La. C.E. art. 607(C)** — broad; not limited by art. 608(B), which governs general character. Aim at the account, not the man |
| Extrinsic evidence of bias, interest, corruption, defect of capacity — **admissible** | **La. C.E. art. 607(D)(1)** |
| Other extrinsic evidence (prior inconsistent statements, contradiction) — admissible subject to balancing | La. C.E. art. 607(D)(2) |
| Character for truthfulness — reputation and opinion | La. C.E. art. 608(A) |
| **⚠ Specific acts to attack truthfulness — BARRED in Louisiana** | **La. C.E. art. 608(B)** — "particular acts, vices, or courses of conduct… may not be inquired into or proved by extrinsic evidence," other than convictions under arts. 609/609.1 or as constitutionally required. **Opposite of FRE 608(b)**, which permits it on cross. Re-route via bias (art. 607(D)(1)), accuracy of this testimony (art. 607(C)), prior inconsistent statement (art. 613), or a preserved constitutional-exception argument |
| **Prior convictions — CRIMINAL cases** | **La. C.E. art. 609.1** (art. 609 is the CIVIL rule — do not cite it in a criminal outline) |
| Convictions only; no arrests, warrants, indictments, prosecutions, acquittals | La. C.E. art. 609.1(B) |
| Details of a conviction — when the door opens | La. C.E. art. 609.1(C): witness denies conviction or recollection; witness testifies to exculpatory circumstances; or probative value outweighs prejudice |
| Conviction on appeal still admissible (goes to weight) | La. C.E. art. 609.1(D) |
| Pardon based on innocence bars impeachment | La. C.E. art. 609.1(E) |
| Juvenile adjudications generally inadmissible | La. C.E. art. 609.1(F) |
| Hearsay definitions and exceptions | La. C.E. arts. 801–804 |
| Expert opinion — qualification and scope | La. C.E. arts. 702–705 |
| Rape shield | La. C.E. art. 412 |
| **Sequestration / exclusion of witnesses** | **La. C.E. art. 615** — on a party's request the court **shall** order exclusion. Exceptions: natural-person parties; one designated representative of a non-natural person; a person shown essential to presenting a party's case (e.g., a case agent or expert); **crime victims and their family members**. Sanctions for violation: contempt, jury instruction, or disqualification of the witness |
| Brady/Giglio material | *Brady v. Maryland*; *Giglio v. United States*; *United States v. Bagley* |
| Motion to suppress | La. C.Cr.P. art. 703 |
| **Contemporaneous objection — preserve at the moment** | **La. C.Cr.P. art. 841** |
| **Offer of proof when evidence is excluded** | **La. C.E. art. 103(A)(2)** — "the substance of the evidence was made known to the court by counsel" |
| Confrontation — surrogate forensic analyst | *Crawford*; *Melendez-Diaz*; *Bullcoming*; ***Smith v. Arizona*, 602 U.S. ___ (2024)** |

## Corrections from prior versions of this table

Four citations in earlier revisions were wrong or misleading and have been corrected:

- **"Scope of cross — La. C.Cr.P. art. 761"** — art. 761 governs **commencement of trial** ("A jury trial commences when the first prospective juror is called for examination. A trial by a judge alone commences when the first witness is sworn."). It has nothing to do with the scope of cross-examination. The correct authority is **La. C.E. art. 611(B)**.
- **"Prior convictions — La. C.E. art. 609"** — art. 609 is the **civil** impeachment rule. The criminal rule is **art. 609.1**.
- **"Character for truthfulness — conduct and reputation — art. 608"** — this wording invited improper specific-acts questions. La. C.E. art. 608(B) **bars** conduct inquiry; only reputation and opinion come in under 608(A). The prior phrasing imported the federal FRE 608(b) rule, which runs the opposite direction.
- **Expert disclosure "La. C.Cr.P. art. 705"** — art. 705 is in the Code of **Evidence** (facts or data underlying an expert opinion). Criminal discovery of examination and test reports runs through **La. C.Cr.P. art. 719** and the art. 716–729 articles.
