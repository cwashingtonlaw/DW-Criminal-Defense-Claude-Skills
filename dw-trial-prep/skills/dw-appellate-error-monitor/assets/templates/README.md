**Important:** Templates here are real prior firm filings with prior client names, docket numbers, and facts. When using a template:

1. Strip ALL case-specific content (client name, docket number, parish, judge, facts, dates).

## Critical drafting notes

### Motion for New Trial (Ross template)

**Caption error in source:** The Ross template has an internal inconsistency — the caption identifies "32ND JUDICIAL DISTRICT COURT IN AND FOR THE PARISH OF CALCASIEU," but paragraph 1 of the body says the defendant was "arrested by the Houma Police Department" (Terrebonne Parish), and the proposed Order is captioned "PARISH OF TERREBONNE" and signed at "Houma, Louisiana." The 32nd JDC is Terrebonne Parish, not Calcasieu (which is the 14th JDC). **The original template caption is wrong** — when using this template, fix the caption to "32ND JUDICIAL DISTRICT COURT, PARISH OF TERREBONNE" (consistent with the Houma references) OR change the venue references entirely to match the actual filing parish.

**Theory:** Art. 851(1) — verdict contrary to the law and the evidence — paired with a self-defense argument under La. R.S. 14:20 (the State "failed to produce any evidence to rebut Ross' self-defense claim"). This is a useful exemplar when the defense theory at trial was self-defense and the conviction is being challenged on weight-of-the-evidence grounds.

**Timing:** Art. 853 — must be filed before sentence. Filing deadline drives everything.

### Motion for Appeal (Jefferson Parish template)

**Use case:** Filed after sentencing to perfect appeal under Art. 914 and designate the record under Art. 914.1(A). Appoints the Louisiana Appeals and Writ Service (LAWS) — the State Public Defender's office that handles indigent appeals statewide — to represent the defendant on appeal, and withdraws trial counsel.

**Public Defender context:** This template is specifically structured for public defender practice (24th JDC Public Defender Office). For retained counsel, adapt the LAWS appointment language to the actual appellate counsel arrangement (retained appellate counsel, continuation by trial counsel, or Office of the State Public Defender appointment for indigent defendants).

**Timing:** Art. 912 — appeal must be taken within 30 days of denial of timely post-trial motions, or within 30 days of sentence if no post-trial motions are filed. The clock starts running fast.
