# Module D — Records Request Generator

Identify all records relevant to the defense and generate request letters and subpoena language.

## Universal Records Request Checklist

The following records should be considered in EVERY criminal case:

**Law Enforcement Records:**
- [ ] Complete police report (all supplements)
- [ ] CAD (Computer-Aided Dispatch) records for the call
- [ ] CAD records for the reporting officer's shift (pattern of activity)
- [ ] 911 call recordings and transcripts
- [ ] Radio dispatch recordings and transcripts
- [ ] Body-worn camera footage (all officers on scene)
- [ ] In-car camera/dash camera footage (all units responding)
- [ ] Crime scene photographs (complete set, including those not in discovery)
- [ ] Crime scene diagrams and measurements
- [ ] Evidence collection logs and chain of custody forms
- [ ] Property/evidence room records
- [ ] AFIS/fingerprint comparison reports
- [ ] DNA lab reports and underlying data
- [ ] Arrest report and booking records
- [ ] Officer use-of-force reports (if applicable)
- [ ] Internal affairs complaints against involved officers
- [ ] Officer personnel files (Giglio/Brady material)
- [ ] Officer training records relevant to case (DUI detection, firearm qualification, interview techniques)
- [ ] Agency policies and procedures relevant to case conduct
- [ ] Detective case notes and working file
- [ ] Crime Stoppers tips (if applicable)

**Prosecution Records:**
- [ ] Grand jury transcript (if indicted)
- [ ] Prosecution file review (Rompilla obligation)
- [ ] Witness interview notes
- [ ] Expert communications
- [ ] Plea offers and correspondence
- [ ] Victim impact materials

**Medical Records (with proper authorization or subpoena):**
- [ ] Victim medical records (emergency room, hospital, follow-up)
- [ ] Victim autopsy report and underlying materials (homicide)
- [ ] Defendant medical records (injuries, mental health, substance abuse)
- [ ] Toxicology reports (victim and/or defendant)
- [ ] Sexual assault examination records (SANE report)
- [ ] EMS/ambulance run reports

**Communications Records:**
- [ ] Defendant cell phone records (calls, texts, data, cell tower/CSLI)
- [ ] Victim cell phone records
- [ ] Witness cell phone records (if relevant and obtainable)
- [ ] Social media account data (Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, Twitter/X)
- [ ] Email records
- [ ] Messaging app records (WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram)

**Institutional Records:**
- [ ] Jail/prison records (calls, visits, incident reports, medical)
- [ ] Probation/parole records
- [ ] DCFS/child protection records (if relevant)
- [ ] School records (if relevant)
- [ ] Employment records (defendant, victim, or witnesses)
- [ ] Military records (if relevant)
- [ ] Immigration records (if relevant)

**Surveillance and Digital Records:**
- [ ] Business surveillance footage (identified in scene visit)
- [ ] Government surveillance footage (traffic cameras, public safety cameras)
- [ ] Residential doorbell/security camera footage (Ring, Nest, etc.)
- [ ] License plate reader (LPR/ALPR) data
- [ ] GPS/vehicle tracking data
- [ ] Rideshare records (Uber, Lyft — driver and passenger)
- [ ] Transaction records (credit/debit card, ATM, point of sale)

## Records Request Letter Template Structure

```
[FIRM LETTERHEAD — apply per dw-shared-protocols-crim/references/letterhead.md]

[Date]

[Records Custodian Name/Title]
[Organization]
[Address]

RE: Records Request — Defense Investigation
    State v. [Defendant Name]
    Case No.: [Number]
    [Parish] Parish, Louisiana

Dear [Custodian]:

This firm represents [Defendant Name] in the above-referenced criminal
matter. Pursuant to [applicable authority — see below], we request the
following records:

[SPECIFIC RECORDS REQUESTED — itemized list]

Time period: [Start date] through [End date]

[APPLICABLE AUTHORITY PARAGRAPH — select based on record type]:

For law enforcement records:
"This request is made pursuant to the defendant's rights under the
Sixth and Fourteenth Amendments to the United States Constitution,
Article I, Sections 2, 13, 16, and 17 of the Louisiana Constitution,
La. C.Cr.P. Art. 718-729, and Brady v. Maryland, 373 U.S. 83 (1963)
and its progeny."

For medical records with authorization:
"Enclosed please find an authorization for release of medical records
signed by [patient name]. This request is made in connection with
pending criminal proceedings."

For third-party business records:
"Enclosed please find a subpoena duces tecum issued by the [Court].
Please produce the requested records on or before [date]. If you have
questions or concerns about this request, please contact our office
before the return date."

Please direct all responsive materials to the undersigned at the address
above.

Respectfully,

[Attorney Name]
Daniels & Washington
[Contact Information]
```
