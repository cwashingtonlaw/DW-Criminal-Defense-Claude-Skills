# Error Preservation — Direct Exam

Read at SKILL.md STEP 7 and the "Error Preservation — Direct Exam" section: arts. 841 / 103(A)(2), the Preservation Log table, the proffer form, and art. 615 sequestration.

---

- **La. C.Cr.P. art. 841** — object or state the desired action **at the moment of the ruling**. Nothing preserved contemporaneously is generally reviewable.
- **La. C.E. art. 103(A)(2)** — when the court **excludes** your evidence, error may not be predicated on it unless *"the substance of the evidence was made known to the court by counsel."* A sustained objection that kills a line of your own direct is an appellate issue **only if you proffer**.

**This is the sharper edge of the two exams.** On cross, an adverse ruling costs you a line of attack. On direct, it excludes your own case — and the record shows nothing at all unless you put it there.

---

## The Preservation Log

Appended to every direct-exam outline. **Chapter, Question #, Ground to state and Proffer substance arrive pre-filled** for every question carrying an anticipated objection; the rest is completed during trial.

| Chapter | Question # | Ground to state | Proffer substance | Ruling | Proffer made | Form of proffer | Issue code |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *pre-filled* | *pre-filled* | *pre-filled* | *pre-filled* | Sustained / Overruled | Yes / No | Q&A / statement / document | |

- **Chapter** and **Question #** — question numbers restart at 1 in each chapter, so a row is identified by chapter **and** number, never by number alone.
- **Ground to state** — the specific ground to assert on the record at the moment of the ruling, by article.
- **Proffer substance** — one sentence on what the witness would have testified, with the `(N)` source citation where a document is involved.

Never write "object and preserve." Name the ground and name the substance. An attorney reading the log mid-examination has no time to compose either.

**Preservation text never appears on a chapter page.** The chapter table is `SOURCE/EXHIBIT | QUESTIONS` and closes with a blank notes box; this log is where the grounds live.

**Example row (illustrative only — not case facts):**

| Chapter | Q# | Ground to state | Proffer substance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 6 | Present sense impression / then-existing state of mind, La. C.E. art. 803(1)–(3); offered not for the truth but for the declarant's state of mind | The witness would testify that the defendant said he was leaving for Sulphur at 8:15 p.m., corroborated by (3) GPS Log — Defendant's Vehicle (03/15/2026), DEF 00052. Request the proffer outside the jury's presence. La. C.Cr.P. art. 841; La. C.E. art. 103(A)(2) |

## Proffer form, in order of preference

1. **Question-and-answer proffer** with the witness on the stand and the jury excused — the best record
2. **Counsel's detailed statement** of the expected testimony
3. **The excluded document** marked and filed into the record, under seal if necessary

Ask for the proffer outside the jury's presence. Courts routinely allow it, and the request itself is on the record even if refused. Get a ruling; if the court defers, renew it.

**After trial:** any sustained objection with no proffer is marked **UNPRESERVED** so the attorney can decide whether to revisit before the defense rests. The completed log hands off to `dw-appellate-error-monitor-crim` and `dw-issue-code-tracker-crim`.

## Sequestration (La. C.E. art. 615)

On a party's request the court **shall** exclude witnesses. Exceptions in 615(B) include natural-person parties, one designated representative, a person shown essential to presenting a party's case, and crime victims and their family.

Confirm the order was requested — an unrequested sequestration is a waived advantage — and confirm your own defense witnesses have complied before they testify. Sanctions under 615(C): contempt, jury instruction, or disqualification of the witness.
