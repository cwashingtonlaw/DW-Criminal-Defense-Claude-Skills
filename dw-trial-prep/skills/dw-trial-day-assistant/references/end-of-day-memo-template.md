# End-of-Day Memo Template

**The single longer-form output of the Trial Day Assistant. Read at 9 PM in a hotel room while prepping tomorrow.**

This memo is the ONLY non-terse deliverable produced by `dw-trial-day-assistant`. Every other module output is a bullet list, a table, or a short alert. The end-of-day memo is the place where the day's events are synthesized, the night's work is planned, and the next-day docket is staged. Length target: one page front-and-back maximum.

## Format Notes

- **One page front-and-back is the target.** Two pages maximum. If it spills past two pages, compress sections 1, 2, and 3 to bullets only and move detail to attached daily artifacts.
- **Time stamps everywhere.** Every assertion should be locatable in the transcript when it lands.
- **Tables over paragraphs.** Sections 3, 4, 5, 8 should always be tables.
- **Action items first, narrative last.** The attorney needs the checklists (sections 9, 10) to plan the night. Theory and escalations come at the end.
- **Hyperlink to daily artifacts.** Section 13 is mandatory — the memo references but does not duplicate the daily Module A-G outputs.

---

## How to Use the Memo

**Tonight:**
1. Read sections 9 and 10 first — they drive the night's work.
2. Read section 4 (missed objections) — mitigate before tomorrow.
3. Read section 8 (tomorrow's witnesses) — refresh cross outlines.
4. Read section 12 (escalations) — call lead attorney if needed.

**Tomorrow morning:**
1. Read section 9 (first-thing checklist) before walking into court.
2. Reference section 6 (rulings pending) so you're ready when court rules.
3. Carry section 8 (witness order) on top of trial notebook.

**End of trial:**
- All daily memos roll up into `dw-trial-notebook-builder` as the day-by-day record.
- Section 4 (missed objections, cumulative) feeds `dw-appellate-error-monitor` MODULE B.
- Section 12 (escalations) becomes the running lead-attorney briefing log.
